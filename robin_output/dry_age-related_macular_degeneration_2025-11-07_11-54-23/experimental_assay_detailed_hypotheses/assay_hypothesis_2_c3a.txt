Functionally, complement‑competent serum (source of C3a/C5a) triggers RPE phenotypes relevant to dry AMD—APOE/C5b‑9 sub‑RPE deposits, robust lipid accumulation, and marked TER loss—linking anaphylatoxin signaling to barrier failure and deposit biogenesis (sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 3-4). C3a alone drives ECM-rich basal deposits via a basolateral C3aR–UPP axis in hfRPE, and a C3aR antagonist prevents deposit formation, tying receptor activation to a disease hallmark (fernandezgodino2018c3atriggersformation pages 2-4, fernandezgodino2018c3atriggersformation pages 5-7). C5a activates pERK/pSmad and induces EMT in RPE, and C5aR antagonism reduces vascular leak in vivo, supporting use of pERK and barrier readouts (lloriansalvador2022complementactivationcontributes pages 1-2, lloriansalvador2022complementactivationcontributes pages 11-13). C5aR signaling also engages PI3K→pAKT in RPE and modulates mitochondrial stress responses, supporting pAKT as a proximal, disease-relevant readout (ishii2023anaphylatoxinc5areceptor pages 1-3).

Previous Use: In iPSC‑RPE, a CC‑HS–driven platform used Ca2+/AKT/ERK/NF‑κB signaling, TER, phagocytosis, and deposit readouts for screening; blocking C3aR1/C5aR1 rescued TER loss and reduced APOE+ deposits, and a focused library screen yielded hits that restored multiple endpoints (sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 13-14, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 1-2). Overall Evaluation: Strengths: strong mechanistic plausibility; human RPE models with polarized biology; direct pharmacology on C3aR/C5aR with proximal signaling readouts and functional barrier rescue; linkage to AMD‑like deposits and genotype‑dependent susceptibility (sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 13-14, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 3-4, lloriansalvador2022complementactivationcontributes pages 1-2). Weaknesses: Ca2+ can be a false‑negative for C3a in RPE; ERK/AKT coupling is context‑dependent; TER may not track deposit formation in some settings; RPE monoculture lacks immune/choroid context; C3aR expression/localization is debated across datasets, arguing for orthogonal endpoints (UPP activity; MMP‑2/TIMP‑3; APOE/C5b‑9) and complementary CC‑HS or co‑culture/chronic stress models (fernandezgodino2018c3atriggersformation pages 5-7, fernandezgodino2018c3atriggersformation pages 2-4, lloriansalvador2022complementactivationcontributes pages 11-13, behnke2022ifnβimmunomodulationand pages 31-33). Net, this assay is well‑suited as a target‑engagement/phenotypic primary screen for anaphylatoxin‑pathway modulators, provided it is paired with longer‑term structural endpoints and higher‑order models for translational confidence (sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 13-14, behnke2022ifnβimmunomodulationand pages 31-33).

The proposed C3a/C5a receptor signaling plus TEER rescue assay is mechanistically grounded and has precedent linking antagonist activity to barrier protection and reduced deposits in human iPSC‑RPE.
