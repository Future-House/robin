Assay Overview: This assay employs multiplex cytokine profiling (Luminex or MSD bead arrays) to quantify pro‐inflammatory cytokines (IL‑1β, IL‑6, IL‑8) and angiogenic factors (VEGF), as well as complement regulators, in the supernatants of retinal pigment epithelial (RPE) cells exposed to combined oxidative and complement stress. The approach models the interplay between oxidative injury, local complement activation, and consequent inflammasome engagement—a pathway central to dry AMD pathogenesis—and uses either immortalized ARPE‑19 or primary RPE cells as the biological substrate (schafer2021complementfactorhrelated pages 1-3, schafer2021complementfactorhrelated pages 9-11).

Biomedical Evidence: Oxidative stress and dysregulated complement activation in the RPE are widely recognized as key drivers of dry AMD, leading to inflammasome activation and secretion of cytokines that contribute to chronic inflammation and eventual tissue degeneration (trakkides2019oxidativestressincreases pages 11-13, brandstetter2015complementcomponentc5a pages 2-3). Clinical findings also document complement and inflammatory dysregulation in AMD patient samples, reinforcing the disease relevance of these pathways (cao2016theroleof pages 120-125, schikora2025decreasedcomplement4 pages 1-2).

Previous Use: Prior studies have demonstrated that oxidative insults in RPE cultures trigger endogenous complement activation and upregulation of cytokines such as IL‑1β, IL‑6, and IL‑8, measured via multiplex immunoassays (trakkides2019oxidativestressincreases pages 9-11, lueck2015complementstimulatesretinal pages 7-7). Moreover, ocular fluid multiplex studies have provided insights into cytokine patterns relevant to AMD pathobiology (hernandez2025roleofoxidative pages 8-10, spindler2018cytokineprofilesin pages 13-14).

Overall Evaluation: This assay’s strength lies in its ability to capture dynamic crosstalk between oxidative stress, complement activation, and cytokine signaling, providing multifaceted readouts useful for evaluating therapeutic modulation. However, limitations include the simplified in vitro environment that may not fully recapitulate the in vivo retinal microenvironment and potential issues with low-abundance analyte detection requiring stringent assay standardization (trakkides2019oxidativestressincreases pages 15-16, trakkides2019oxidativestressincreases pages 13-15).

Multiplex cytokine profiling in RPE supernatants under oxidative and complement stress provides a robust mechanistic readout for dry AMD. Its integrated measurement of critical inflammatory and angiogenic mediators makes it a valuable tool for early-stage drug discovery.
