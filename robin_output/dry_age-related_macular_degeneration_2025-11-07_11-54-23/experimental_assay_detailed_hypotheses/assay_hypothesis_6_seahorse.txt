Assay Overview: The XF Cell Mito Stress Test (oligomycin, FCCP, rotenone/antimycin A) will quantify oxygen consumption rate (OCR) in RPE to derive basal and ATP-linked respiration, maximal respiration, and spare respiratory capacity; parallel ECAR can index glycolytic compensation under oxidative stress (menadione or H2O2) and with candidate drugs in primary human adult RPE (haRPE) from AMD vs No-AMD donors and, with caution, iPSC-RPE (XF96 format; typical oligomycin/FCCP/Rot/AA conditions) (ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 3-4, ebeling2020improvingretinalmitochondrial pages 1-2, fisher2022humanipscand pages 2-4, fisher2022humanipscand pages 11-12). The readouts map directly onto OxPhos function in RPE, which relies predominantly on mitochondria for ATP, with MR/SRC definitions aligned to the study abbreviations (ebeling2020improvingretinalmitochondrial pages 1-2).

Biomedical Evidence: Mitochondrial dysfunction in RPE is a central driver of dry AMD, with reduced mitochondrial number/mass, structural disruption, mtDNA damage correlating with disease severity, and bioenergetic deficits documented in human donor RPE; mitochondria are the primary ATP source in RPE and mediate ROS signaling that escalates to apoptosis and inflammasome activation when damaged (fisher2018perspectiveonamd pages 1-2). The retina’s high metabolic demand and evidence for an RPE bioenergetic crisis justify OCR/ATP/SRC as mechanistically relevant therapeutic readouts (louie2022assessmentofposteriora pages 11-15).

Previous Use: In primary human AMD RPE, four mitochondrial-targeted drugs improved maximal respiration and ATP production selectively in AMD donors, while controls showed no benefit; responses were heterogeneous, underscoring stratification needs (ebeling2020improvingretinalmitochondrial pages 1-2). Seahorse assays defined AMD-associated bioenergetic impairment and altered oxidative-stress responses in donor RPE, establishing “AMD in a dish” (ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 1-2). Comparative work shows disease-specific treatment responses in haRPE, whereas iPSC-RPE often lack disease-specificity unless conditions emulate chronic stress (“metabolic memory”) (fisher2022humanipscand pages 2-4, fisher2022humanipscand pages 11-12). A 5,065-compound screen identified hits that restored mitochondrial function by Seahorse and improved RPE survival under stress, supporting follow-up potency ranking on OCR endpoints (cai2019highthroughputscreeningidentifies pages 1-2). Ex vivo Seahorse assays in mouse posterior eyecup detected mitochondrial defects and glycolytic shifts in PolG, sodium iodate, and diabetic models, validating OCR/ATP/ECAR endpoints in RPE-containing tissue (louie2022assessmentofposteriora pages 49-56, louie2022assessmentofposteriora pages 37-49).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths: direct measurement of the core AMD pathology (RPE OxPhos failure), human disease relevance with donor-stratified haRPE, quantitative multiparametric readouts (ATP-linked respiration, MR, SRC), and demonstrated detection of drug rescue with translational implications (ebeling2020improvingretinalmitochondrial pages 1-2, ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 1-2). Weaknesses: responder heterogeneity (~50% of AMD donors), iPSC-RPE may not recapitulate disease without chronic stress paradigms, and acute oxidants can yield complex ROS/autophagy dynamics that confound interpretation; orthogonal measures (ROS localization, mtDNA damage, MMP, mitophagy) are needed (fisher2022humanipscand pages 11-12, chan2019reactiveoxygenspeciesdependent pages 1-2, cano2021nrf2deficiencydecreases pages 5-7, bhattacharya2022modelingofmitochondrial pages 1-2). For tissue assays, normalization and tissue-size constraints require rigorous optimization (louie2022assessmentofposteriora pages 49-56, louie2022assessmentofposteriora pages 37-49).

The Seahorse Mito Stress Test in RPE is fit-for-purpose to test mitochondrial therapeutics for dry AMD, capturing disease-relevant deficits and pharmacologic rescue in human donor cells. Donor variability and model selection (haRPE vs iPSC-RPE) must be managed, and OCR metrics should be paired with orthogonal oxidative stress and quality-control readouts to ensure mechanistic linkage and translational value.
