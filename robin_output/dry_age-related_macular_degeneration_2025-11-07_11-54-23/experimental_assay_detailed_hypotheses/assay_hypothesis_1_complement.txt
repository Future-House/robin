Assay Overview: Quantify cell-associated complement deposition on mature iPSC-derived RPE (both CFH Y/Y and CFH H/H donors) by flow cytometry for C3b (and iC3b where distinguishable) and MAC (C5b-9), with orthogonal ELISA of apical/basal supernatants for SC5b-9 and C3 activation fragments; pair with functional TER and imaging of APOE/C5b-9 sub-RPE deposits for morphological linkage to drusen-like material (and optionally lysosomal markers) (sugita2018detectionofcomplement pages 3-4, fernandezgodino2015alocalcomplement pages 10-11, hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 28-31, hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 5-9, cerniauskas2020complementmodulationreverses pages 7-13). Mature iPSC-RPE are required because they robustly secrete C3, FH/FHL-1, FI and active C3 forms; assay reagents must discriminate C3b from iC3b, as some “C3b” reagents primarily detect iC3b; note that secreted FD can be low, impacting autonomous AP amplification in serum-free settings (schikora2025maturationdependentcomplementproduction pages 29-32, schikora2025maturationdependentcomplementproduction pages 16-19, schikora2025maturationdependentcomplementproduction pages 12-16).

Biomedical Evidence: Complement dysregulation is central in AMD: the retina locally produces complement; aged/AMD tissues exhibit MAC deposition; drusen contain complement proteins/regulators (warwick2014agerelatedmaculardegeneration pages 3-6). In iPSC-RPE carrying CFH(Y402H), C3 turnover, C3b and C5b-9 association/internalization and lysosomal overload increase, and APOE+ deposits co-localize with C5b-9, recapitulating AMD-like pathology (cerniauskas2020complementmodulationreverses pages 7-13, hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 28-31, hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 5-9). Complement signaling via C3a/C5a drives NF-κB activation, autophagy defects, TER loss and sub-RPE deposits, and C3 loss-of-function protects barrier integrity on AMD-like ECM, linking complement activation to functional decline (sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 1-2, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 13-14, chinchilla2021amdlikesubstratecauses pages 2-4).

Previous Use: iPSC-RPE models quantified RPE-associated C3b and C5b-9 by imaging and showed pharmacologic reversal with the C3 inhibitor Cp40; high-throughput screening in a complement-driven iPSC-RPE system identified agents that reduced deposits and rescued TER, validating complement readouts for discovery (cerniauskas2020complementmodulationreverses pages 7-13, cerniauskas2020complementmodulationreverses pages 14-15, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 1-2, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 13-14). ELISAs for C3, C3a/C5a and SC5b-9 and FACS for MAC have been deployed in RPE systems, demonstrating feasibility of the proposed quantifications (fernandezgodino2015alocalcomplement pages 10-11, sugita2018detectionofcomplement pages 3-4).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths: disease-proximal, genotype-informed readouts that are quantitative, multiplexable (flow/ELISA/imaging), and pharmacologically responsive; can be integrated with TER and deposit metrics for mechanistic depth (cerniauskas2020complementmodulationreverses pages 7-13, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 1-2, hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 28-31). Limitations: pathway attribution is context dependent—C1q-driven classical activation can dominate with human serum; high FH and low FD may blunt/obscure AP; some cultures show normal TER at baseline; antibody specificity for C3b vs iC3b is critical (johnson2011cellculturemodel pages 5-5, schikora2025maturationdependentcomplementproduction pages 12-16, schikora2025maturationdependentcomplementproduction pages 16-19, hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 5-9). Include pathway controls (C1q depletion/add-back, Factor B/FH modulation), maturation criteria, and functional endpoints to ensure translational relevance and robustness for screening (johnson2011cellculturemodel pages 5-5, schikora2025maturationdependentcomplementproduction pages 29-32, warwick2014agerelatedmaculardegeneration pages 3-6).

Measuring C3b and C5b-9 on iPSC-RPE is biologically relevant and feasible, and it correlates with lysosomal stress, drusen-like deposits, and barrier phenotypes in CFH(Y402H) models. With pathway-specific controls, mature cells, and paired functional readouts, this assay is well suited for therapeutic testing in dry AMD.
