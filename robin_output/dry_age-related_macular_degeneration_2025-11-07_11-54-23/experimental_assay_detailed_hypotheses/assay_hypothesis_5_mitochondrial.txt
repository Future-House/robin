Assay Overview: Live-cell, multiplexed imaging in RPE models to quantify oxidative injury and mitochondrial health. Cells: primary human adult RPE (AMD and No AMD donors) for disease relevance, complemented by iPSC-RPE (noting model-dependent responses) and ARPE‑19 for throughput. Readouts: cytosolic ROS (CellROX Deep Red), mitochondrial membrane potential (Δψm; TMRM), and mitochondrial content/morphology (MitoTracker Deep Red). Optional addition of MitoSOX for mitochondrial superoxide to disambiguate ROS compartment. Acquisition by spinning-disk/confocal with low-toxicity, far‑red dyes in pigmented RPE; analysis includes single-cell intensity distributions and mitochondrial fragmentation metrics. Orthogonal endpoints (Seahorse OCR, LC3 flux/mKeima-mito) are run in parallel to interpret mechanisms and flux. Stressors: H2O2, NaIO3, or menadione; dosing guided by Δψm titration curves. Biomedical Evidence: Oxidative damage and mitochondrial dysfunction in RPE are central to dry AMD; donor RPE show reduced OXPHOS, fewer/damaged mitochondria, and increased mtDNA lesions preceding degeneration. RPE-specific loss of MnSOD elevates mitochondrial ROS in vivo, drives metabolic reprogramming in RPE, and secondarily damages photoreceptor mitochondria, establishing causality. Loss of Δψm and elevated ROS are recurrent features across AMD-relevant stressors. (chen2023oxidativestressin pages 2-3, ebeling2020improvingretinalmitochondrial pages 2-3, ebeling2020improvingretinalmitochondrial pages 10-11, brown2019mitochondrialoxidativestress pages 1-2, bilbaomalave2021mitochondrialdysfunctionand pages 2-4, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 11-13, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 7-8)

Previous Use: Dye-based Δψm/ROS assays have identified protective phenotypes in RPE: NaIO3-treated ARPE‑19 showed TMRM loss and autophagy impairment that were partially rescued by rapamycin; H2O2 models using JC‑1 and CellROX with high-content imaging detected AMPK‑dependent protection; AMD cybrids showed drug‑induced increases in Δψm (JC‑1) and decreases in mitochondrial superoxide (MitoSOX). Notably, NaIO3 in ARPE‑19 elevates cytosolic ROS without increasing mitochondrial MitoSOX signal, while still driving mitochondrial dysfunction and fission, underscoring the need to multiplex ROS probes. (lin2018sodiumiodatedisrupted pages 1-2, lin2018sodiumiodatedisrupted pages 2-3, li2019artemisininprotectshuman pages 2-4, li2019artemisininprotectshuman pages 11-12, nashine2019pu91drugrescues pages 12-13, nashine2019pu91drugrescues pages 1-2, chan2019reactiveoxygenspeciesdependent pages 1-2)

Overall Evaluation: Strengths—Mechanistically aligned, live, and sensitive; captures immediate Δψm/ROS pharmacodynamics; compatible with primary donor RPE and multiplexing with mitophagy and respiration assays; has detected drug-rescue phenotypes in several RPE models. (fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 1-2, ebeling2020improvingretinalmitochondrial pages 7-9, tan2022analysisofmitochondrial pages 3-6) Weaknesses—ROS compartment specificity (cytosolic vs mitochondrial) can mislead; antioxidants can have paradoxical effects; dyes have toxicity/photobleaching and melanin quenching; NaIO3 is an acute chemical injury; high donor-to-donor variability and iPSC-RPE model-dependence. Mitigations include dual ROS probes (CellROX+MitoSOX), titrated low dye concentrations with far‑red fluorophores, stress-challenge designs, and orthogonal validation (Seahorse, LC3/bafilomycin flux, mKeima). (chan2019reactiveoxygenspeciesdependent pages 1-2, tan2022analysisofmitochondrial pages 3-6, tan2022analysisofmitochondrial pages 6-8, fisher2022humanipscand pages 12-14, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 2-3)

Live Δψm/ROS imaging in RPE is biologically justified and has precedent for detecting therapeutic rescue in AMD-relevant models. It is best deployed as part of a multiplexed workflow with respiration and autophagy/mitophagy readouts to handle compartment-specific ROS, dye artifacts, and donor heterogeneity. Careful model and probe selection are essential for robust interpretation and translation. (ebeling2020improvingretinalmitochondrial pages 2-3, lin2018sodiumiodatedisrupted pages 1-2, chan2019reactiveoxygenspeciesdependent pages 1-2)
