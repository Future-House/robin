Assay Overview: This assay uses high‐content imaging of cultured RPE cells challenged with fluorescently labeled photoreceptor outer segments (POS) combined with LysoTracker staining to quantify both the rate of POS uptake and the degradation half‐life. A Bafilomycin A1 chase is incorporated to distinguish initial receptor‐mediated internalization from lysosomal clearance defects, with kinetic parameters measured by colocalization analysis and potential FRET readouts. Cultured RPE models such as primary RPE or ARPE‐19 cells provide the biological substrate to recapitulate in vivo phagocytosis seen in the retina (keeling2018impairedcargoclearance pages 8-11, egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 32-36).

Biomedical Evidence: Inefficient POS clearance contributes to lipofuscin accumulation, oxidative stress, and subsequent RPE and photoreceptor degeneration observed in dry age‐related macular degeneration (AMD). The fundamental role of daily POS phagocytosis in maintaining retinal homeostasis is well established, with perturbations in lysosomal acidification and phagosome maturation directly linked to AMD pathology (egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 26-32, egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 21-26).

Previous Use: Similar assays have been used to dissect phagosome maturation defects in genetic models of retinal degeneration, elucidating roles for key regulators such as MerTK and lysosomal enzymes, and have validated the utility of fluorescent POS and LysoTracker assays in distinguishing uptake from degradation defects (dejos2018photoreceptorinducedrpephagolysosomal pages 1-2, egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 88-92).

Overall Evaluation: This assay is a robust tool for early-stage drug screening, offering precise kinetic measurements that target a central mechanism in dry AMD pathogenesis. Its strengths lie in the quantitative assessment of phagocytic clearance, while the major limitation is that in vitro models may not fully capture the complexity of chronic in vivo disease processes (lieffrig2023clearancephagocytosisby pages 4-6, keeling2018impairedcargoclearance pages 7-8).

This assay is valuable for testing therapeutics aimed at enhancing RPE phagocytic function in dry AMD. It provides mechanistically relevant, quantitative endpoints that support early-stage drug discovery.
