Query: Mechanisms of lipofuscin and A2E accumulation in RPE cells and its impact on autophagy flux: development of fluorescent lipid quantitative assays, confocal imaging, and LC3/p62 western blot analysis in iPSC-derived RPE monolayers

Photoreceptor outer segment (OS) phagocytosis delivers all‑trans‑retinaldehyde and ethanolamine (from PE) that form bisretinoids (A2E, isoA2E), which accumulate as lipofuscin in RPE; in SLOS models, defective phagosome maturation/heterophagy elevates bisretinoids and apical autofluorescent inclusions, establishing a causal link between stalled degradation and A2E/lipofuscin build‑up (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 23-26, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 15-20). A2E directly inhibits phagosome formation/trafficking and perturbs cholesterol metabolism/efflux, while altered sterol handling (7DHC/oxysterols, cholesterol depletion or mis‑trafficking) disrupts ORP1L/RAB7/dynein recruitment and lipid‑raft function on maturing phagosomes, retarding transport and fusion with late endosomes/lysosomes; lysosomal pH and CTSD activity can remain near‑normal, pinpointing a maturation/fusion block that reduces autophagic flux (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 23-26, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 1-5). Consistently, iPSC‑RPE show increased LC3B‑II, SQSTM1 and ubiquitinated proteins, GFP+RFP LC3 puncta, persistent 1D4‑positive phagosomes lacking juxtaposition with CTSD lysosomes, and sensitivity to 3‑MA, all indicating impaired autophagosome/phagosome maturation (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 12-15, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 53-56, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 15-20).

Fluorescent quantitative assays: confocal z‑stacks with 488‑nm excitation/500–600‑nm emission, image segmentation and size binning quantify autofluorescent inclusions and correlate with UPLC‑MS quantification of A2E/related bisretinoids using synthesized standards and calibration curves (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 37-40). A2E autofluorescence is also measurable by flow cytometry, enabling higher‑throughput burden estimates; complementary LysoSensor (ratiometric pH) and BODIPY‑pepstatin A (CTSD) assays verify lysosomal competence (cardoso2018stemcellderivedretinal pages 159-162, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 37-40). Under lysosomal stress paradigms (daily POS pulses ± chloroquine), Lysotracker flow cytometry reports expansion/redistribution of acidic vesicles, while TFEB/TFE3/MITF translocation indicates lysosomal biogenesis responses (cardoso2018stemcellderivedretinal pages 140-144, cardoso2018stemcellderivedretinal pages 159-162).

Confocal flux imaging: tandem RFP‑GFP‑LC3B (BacMam) discriminates unacidified (GFP+RFP) from acidified (RFP‑only) compartments; ~100‑cell counts per condition and chloroquine controls quantify maturation defects, which are elevated in SLOS iPSC‑RPE (e.g., ~49% GFP+RFP vs ~17% in control) (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 37-40, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 12-15). Heterophagy staging uses 1D4 rhodopsin with CTSD co‑staining/colocalization to read out phagosome–lysosome fusion (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 15-20).

LC3/p62 immunoblotting: steady‑state elevations of LC3B‑II and SQSTM1 indicate reduced flux; lysosomal inhibition (chloroquine) should raise LC3B‑II in both control and disease cells, while failure to further increase p62 in disease implies pre‑existing clearance saturation; unchanged ATG12–ATG5 supports a block post‑LC3 lipidation (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 9-12, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 53-56, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 12-15). POS feeding (e.g., 2–48 h) provides a synchronizable cargo load to couple lipid accumulation metrics with autophagy flux biochemistry (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 37-40, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 12-15).

Lipofuscin/A2E in RPE arises from POS‑derived bisretinoids and accumulates when phagosome maturation is compromised; A2E and sterol dysregulation further impair trafficking, creating a feed‑forward block in autophagic flux (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 23-26, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 1-5). In iPSC‑RPE, a robust pipeline combines autofluorescence confocal quantitation linked to UPLC‑MS, tandem LC3 reporter imaging, LC3B‑II/SQSTM1 immunoblotting with lysosomal inhibitors, and 1D4/CTSD colocalization to localize and quantify the maturation defect (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 37-40, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 53-56, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 12-15, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 15-20). Lysosomal pH/CTSD assays refine interpretation by excluding gross acidification failure (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 37-40, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 9-12).
