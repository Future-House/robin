TEER optimization: allow 2–3 weeks post-confluence with serum reduction (2% followed by 48 h serum-free) to reach stable baseline TER of ~40–45 Ω·cm2; measure with a WPI EVOM/STX2 electrode, subtract blank filter resistance, and report percent change from the starting value (obert2015targetingthetight pages 45-53, obert2015targetingthetight pages 40-45). VEGF induces time-dependent TER loss (2–4 h); αCT-1 prevents loss up to ~30 ng/mL VEGF, while 60 ng/mL produces near-complete loss; include 18-β-GCA and apyrase to probe mechanism, noting apyrase alone lowers TER without augmenting VEGF effects (obert2015targetingthetight pages 45-53, obert2017targetingthetight pages 10-11, obert2015targetingthetight pages 53-57, obert2017targetingthetight pages 11-12). ECIS or EGTA insult (0.5–1.2 mM) provides an orthogonal recovery model to quantify post-injury impedance gains with test agents (obert2017targetingthetight pages 14-15, obert2017targetingthetight pages 10-11). 

ZO-1 immunofluorescence: fix 10 min in 4% PFA, block (10% NGS/0.4% Triton X-100), stain ZO-1 at 1:200 with Alexa secondary at 1:500, include no-primary controls, and acquire standardized confocal Z-stacks for quantitation (obert2015targetingthetight pages 45-53, obert2017targetingthetight pages 12-14). Quantify junctional continuity and tiling with CellProfiler; VEGF disrupts linear ZO-1 and elongates cells, whereas protective compounds preserve continuous junctions and regular morphology (obert2015targetingthetight pages 40-45, obert2015targetingthetight pages 53-57, obert2017targetingthetight pages 14-15). 

Permeability/dextran flux: measure paracellular tracer flux (e.g., fluorescent dextran) across Transwells to validate TEER—higher flux indicates TJ disruption; protocol specifics are not provided in the excerpt (obert2015targetingthetight pages 53-57, obert2017targetingthetight pages 16-17, obert2015targetingthetight pages 40-45). 

Western blot: quantify ZO-1, occludin, claudins, and occludin phosphorylation associated with VEGF-induced permeability, correlating with TEER and ZO-1 IF; detailed blot protocols are not included here (obert2015targetingthetight pages 53-57, obert2017targetingthetight pages 16-17, obert2015targetingthetight pages 13-19, obert2017targetingthetight pages 17-18). 

A combined TEER–ZO-1 IF–dextran flux–TJ biochemistry workflow yields orthogonal evidence of barrier preservation in RPE monolayers.
