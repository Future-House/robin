Query: Crosstalk between oxidative stress and complement activation in RPE cells: designing multiplexed assays to measure ROS levels, complement component expression, cytokine release, and cell viability under combined stressors relevant to dry AMD pathogenesis

Multiplexed assay design for dissecting the interplay between oxidative stress and complement activation in RPE cells involves integrating several complementary measurement platforms. RPE cells (typically ARPE-19) are exposed to defined oxidants (e.g., H2O2 or HOHA‑lactone) to induce oxidative stress while activating endogenous complement pathways; treatments should be applied at multiple time points (e.g., 4, 24, and 48 h) to capture dynamic responses (linetsky20184hydroxy7oxo5heptenoicacidlactone pages 3-5, trakkides2019oxidativestressin pages 16-20).

ROS levels can be quantified using fluorescent probes such as Carboxy-H2DCFDA and mitochondrial ROS sensors, allowing rapid measurement via plate readers or high-content imaging (chen2022oxidativestressdifferentially pages 9-10, trakkides2019oxidativestressincreases pages 3-6). Concurrently, complement component expression—including C3 and its activated cleavage products, factor B, CFH, and CD59—as well as receptor expression (e.g., CR3, C5aR1) should be measured using immunoblotting, ELISA, or multiplex bead assays (linetsky20184hydroxy7oxo5heptenoicacidlactone pages 3-5). In parallel, cytokine release (IL-1β, IL-6, IL-8, VEGF) can be assessed by Luminex or MSD platforms to monitor inflammatory and angiogenic responses (trakkides2019oxidativestressin pages 13-16, busch2023increasedplasmalevel pages 2-3). Cell viability and barrier integrity are captured by assays such as TUNEL staining, MTT, and transepithelial resistance measurements (joseph2013oxidativestresssensitizes pages 3-4, trakkides2019oxidativestressin pages 6-10). Appropriate controls (e.g., serum-free, complement-depleted) and pharmacologic modulators (e.g., olaparib) should be included to delineate pathway-specific effects (carver2016nacetylcysteineamideprotects pages 10-11, joseph2013oxidativestresssensitizes pages 1-2).

This integrated approach enables simultaneous determination of oxidative stress, complement activation, cytokine output, and cell health under conditions relevant to dry AMD pathogenesis. It provides a robust and dynamic platform to elucidate the crosstalk between these molecular pathways.
