Query: In vitro co-culture and organ-on-chip systems recapitulating RPE-Bruch’s membrane-choroid interactions in dry AMD: optimization of extracellular matrix composition, endothelial–RPE crosstalk, barrier permeability assays, microfluidic perfusion parameters, and real-time imaging of cell–cell interactions

1. Hypothesis: Increased Bruch’s membrane stiffness from advanced glycation endproduct (AGE) crosslinking disrupts RPE polarization via YAP/TAZ nuclear translocation.  
   • Method: Fabricate chips with tunable collagen–elastin ECM treated with varying doses of glyoxal.  
   • Readouts: TEER, immunofluorescent ZO-1/occludin distribution, YAP/TAZ localization, RPE transepithelial uptake of fluorescent photoreceptor outer segments (POS).

2. Hypothesis: Drusen-associated β-amyloid oligomers deposited in BrM reduce choroidal endothelial fenestrations and elevate barrier permeability.  
   • Method: Incorporate synthetic Aβ42 oligomers into ECM layer; co-culture iPSC-derived RPE with human choroidal endothelial cells under flow.  
   • Readouts: Trans-ECM dextran flux, endothelial fenestrae density (scanning EM), IL-8/CXCL1 secretion.

3. Hypothesis: RPE-secreted complement C3a amplifies choroidal endothelial inflammation and junctional breakdown.  
   • Method: Stimulate RPE with photo-oxidative stress; add exogenous C3a or a C3aR antagonist; perfuse choroid compartment.  
   • Readouts: Endothelial ICAM-1, VCAM-1 by flow cytometry, TEER drop, monocyte adhesion under flow.

4. Hypothesis: Acute oxidative pulses in RPE trigger release of VEGF-rich extracellular vesicles (EVs) that prime choroidal angiogenic pathways.  
   • Method: Apply H2O2 pulses to RPE, collect EVs from basolateral flow, add to naïve endothelial cultures.  
   • Readouts: EV quantification (NTA), VEGF content (ELISA), endothelial tube-formation assay on Matrigel.

5. Hypothesis: Shifts in laminin isoform composition (LM511 vs. LM521) of the ECM alter RPE integrin α3β1 signaling and reduce POS phagocytosis.  
   • Method: Pattern ECM coatings with graded LM511/LM521 ratios on chip membranes.  
   • Readouts: α3β1 activation (phospho-FAK), POS uptake assay (fluorescently labeled), RPE transepithelial resistance.

6. Hypothesis: Mitochondrial dysfunction in RPE leads to mtDNA release into the sub-RPE space and activates NLRP3 inflammasomes in adjacent macrophage-like cells.

   • Readouts: IL-1β release, caspase-1 activation (Western blot), MtDNA quantification in perfusate.
