Query: Autophagy and lysosomal dysfunction in RPE cells during dry AMD progression: key regulators of autophagic flux impairment, methods to measure LC3 conversion and p62 accumulation, lysosomal pH monitoring, A2E autofluorescence assays, and high‐throughput autophagy modulators screening

Below are 15 distinct mechanistic hypotheses or cell-culture–based therapeutic strategies focused on autophagy and lysosomal dysfunction in RPE cells during dry AMD. Each can be tested by measuring LC3-II/LC3-I conversion and p62 levels, monitoring lysosomal pH, and/or assaying A2E autofluorescence.

1. TFEB Overexpression to Rescue Autophagic Flux  
   Hypothesis: Forced nuclear translocation of TFEB restores lysosomal biogenesis and autophagic flux in A2E-loaded RPE.  
   Assays: Transduce RPE with TFEB-GFP; quantify LC3-II/LC3-I ratio, p62 clearance, LysoSensor fluorescence, and A2E autofluorescence before/after chloroquine challenge.

2. Cathepsin D Activity Decline Drives Lysosomal Alkalinization  
   Hypothesis: Loss of cathepsin D activity in aged RPE leads to lysosomal pH elevation and autophagosome accumulation.  
   Assays: Inhibit cathepsin D pharmacologically or siRNA; measure lysosomal pH (LysoSensor), LC3/p62 by Western blot, cathepsin D activity assay.

3. CFH Y402H Variant Directly Impairs Autophagy  
   Hypothesis: The AMD-risk CFH Y402H protein binds RPE surface receptors, triggering mTOR activation and autophagy blockage.  
   Assays: Treat RPE with recombinant CFH WT vs. Y402H; monitor mTOR phosphorylation, LC3 conversion, p62, and lysosomal pH.

4. V-ATPase Modulators Restore Lysosomal Acidity  
   Strategy: Screen small-molecule V-ATPase activators to reacidify lysosomes in A2E-loaded RPE.  
   Assays: High-throughput lysosomal pH screen (LysoSensor) in 96-well format, secondary readouts of LC3/p62 and A2E clearance.

5. Microtubule Stabilizers Promote Autophagosome–Lysosome Fusion  
   Hypothesis: A2E disrupts microtubule tracks required for autophagosome transport; microtubule stabilizers (e.g., epothilone) can restore fusion.  
   Assays: Treat A2E-loaded RPE with epothilone; quantify LC3 puncta co-localized with LAMP1 by confocal imaging, p62 levels, and A2E removal.

6. SIRT1 Activation Enhances Autophagic Flux  
   Hypothesis: Pharmacological activation of SIRT1 (e.g., resveratrol) deacetylates key autophagy proteins and overcomes A2E-induced block.  
   Assays: Treat RPE with resveratrol; measure acetyl-LC3 by immunoprecipitation, total LC3/p62 ratio, and lysosomal pH shifts.

7. STX17 Downregulation as a Mechanism of Fusion Failure  
   Hypothesis: A2E accumulation reduces syntaxin-17 expression, blocking autophagosome–lysosome SNARE assembly.  
   Assays: Knock down STX17 by siRNA in RPE; measure LC3-II accumulation, p62, and rescue with STX17 overexpression.

8. Rab7 Overexpression to Drive Late-Stage Autophagy  
   Strategy: Enhance Rab7 activity to promote autophagosome maturation and lysosomal fusion in aged RPE.  
   Assays: Transfect RPE with Rab7-WT or GTP-locked mutant; quantify LC3 puncta clearance, p62 degradation, and A2E autofluorescence.

9. Beclin1-Targeted Peptides to Bypass PI3K-III Block  
   Strategy: Deliver cell-penetrating Beclin1 peptides to restore autophagosome nucleation in A2E-stressed RPE.  
   Assays: Treat RPE with TAT-Beclin1; monitor LC3 lipidation, p62 turnover, and lysosomal structure by Lysotracker.

10. NRF2 Pathway Activation to Indirectly Boost Autophagy  
    Hypothesis: Activation of NRF2 (e.g., with sulforaphane) upregulates p62 transcription and enhances selective autophagy of damaged organelles.  
    Assays: Treat RPE with sulforaphane; measure NRF2 nuclear translocation, p62 mRNA/protein, LC3-II, and A2E removal.

11. ER Stress/UPR Modulation Affects Lysosomal Function  
    Hypothesis: Chronic ER stress in RPE (e.g., tunicamycin treatment) impairs lysosomal acidification via altered V-ATPase assembly.  
    Assays: Induce UPR in RPE; measure BiP/CHOP, lysosomal pH, LC3/p62 levels, and rescue with chemical chaperones (4-PBA).

12. Galectin-12 Overexpression to Facilitate Lysosomal Repair  
    Strategy: Express galectin-12 in RPE to promote lysosomal membrane repair and restore autophagic flux post-A2E damage.  
    Assays: Adenoviral galectin-12 delivery; quantify LC3/p62, lysosomal integrity (galectin puncta assay), and A2E clearance.

13. CRISPR Knockout Screen of Lysosomal Genes  
    Strategy: Pooled CRISPR/Cas9 screen targeting all known lysosomal-associated genes in LC3-GFP RPE to identify new regulators of autophagy flux.  
    Assays: FACS-sort high/low LC3-GFP populations under A2E stress, deconvolve sgRNA enrichment, validate hits by individual knockout.

14. FDA-Approved Drug Repurposing via LC3-RFP Reporter Assay  
    Strategy: High-throughput screen of an FDA-approved library in RPE stably expressing tandem RFP-GFP-LC3 to identify autophagy enhancers that lower A2E autofluorescence.  
    Assays: Automated microscopy for GFP quenching/RFP persistence, secondary readouts of lysosomal pH and p62.

15. Nanoparticle–siRNA Delivery to Knock Down mTORC1 Components  
    Strategy: Use lipid nanoparticles to deliver siRNA against mTORC1 subunits (Raptor) specifically to RPE, thereby stimulating autophagy.  
    Assays: Treat RPE with nanoparticles; measure mTOR pathway inhibition, LC3-II increase, p62 reduction, and A2E phototoxicity protection.
