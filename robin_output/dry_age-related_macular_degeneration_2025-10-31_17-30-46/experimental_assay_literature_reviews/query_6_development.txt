Query: Development of co-culture systems combining RPE and choroidal endothelial cells or microglia to model inflammatory and angiogenic cross-talk in dry AMD: mechanistic readouts including TEER, cytokine profiling, barrier function assays and phenotypic screening

1. Development of co-culture systems combining RPE and choroidal endothelial cells or microglia to model inflammatory and angiogenic cross-talk in dry AMD. Mechanistic readouts: TEER for barrier integrity, multiplex cytokine profiling, FITC-dextran permeability, junctional protein immunostaining and high-content phenotypic screening for drusen-like deposits.  
2. CRISPR activation (CRISPRa) screen in human iPSC-derived RPE to identify genes that enhance phagocytosis of photoreceptor outer segments. Readouts: fluorescently labeled POS uptake, endolysosomal trafficking (LAMP1 colocalization), phagosome maturation kinetics by live-cell imaging.  
3. Targeting mitochondrial dynamics in RPE: treat cells with small-molecule Mfn2 agonists or Drp1 inhibitors to modulate fusion/fission balance. Assays: mitochondrial network morphology (Mitotracker), mitophagy flux (mt-Keima), ROS quantification (MitoSOX), ATP production and cell viability under oxidative stress.  
4. Dissecting NLRP3 inflammasome activation in RPE upon accumulation of cholesterol crystals or A2E. Readouts: pro-Caspase-1 cleavage, IL-1β release (ELISA), pyroptotic cell death (propidium iodide uptake), gasdermin D immunoblot. Test MCC950 or other inflammasome inhibitors.  
5. Co-culture of RPE monolayers with human choroid explants to screen novel complement C3 or C5 inhibitors. Endpoints: C3b/iC3b deposition (immunofluorescence), MAC (C5b-9) formation, RPE survival (Annexin V/PI), and drusen-like sub-RPE deposits by TEM.  
6. Mechanistic study of macrophage-derived exosome transfer of miR-21 to RPE and its role in TGF-β1–driven EMT. Readouts: exosome uptake (PKH67), miRNA quantification by qPCR, EMT marker panels (E-cadherin, α-SMA), TEER and wound-healing assays.  
7. Generation of 3D retinal organoids from patient-derived iPSCs carrying CFH risk alleles to model drusen formation. Treatments: complement activation triggers. Readouts: lipid droplet accumulation (Nile Red), drusen-like deposits by confocal and EM, bulk RNA-seq for inflammatory pathways.  
8. AAV-mediated overexpression of PKCα in RPE to enhance autophagic clearance of lipofuscin precursors. Mechanistic assays: LC3-II flux (bafilomycin A1 chase), p62 degradation, lysosomal enzyme activities (cathepsin D), long-term autofluorescence imaging.  
9. High-content phenotypic screen of a small-molecule library in hypoxic choroidal endothelial cells to identify HIF-1α pathway modulators. Readouts: tube formation, VEGF secretion (ELISA), HIF-1α stabilization (Western blot), cell proliferation under hypoxia.  
10. Activation of SIRT6 in RPE to bolster oxidative stress defenses. Treat with SIRT6 activators and measure NRF2 nuclear translocation, downstream antioxidant gene expression (HO-1, NQO1), glutathione levels, and cell survival under H2O2 challenge.  
11. Evaluation of bispecific antibodies simultaneously targeting VEGF-A and PDGF-BB in RPE-endothelial-pericyte tri-culture. Endpoints: endothelial sprout length, pericyte coverage index, vascular permeability (permeability to 70 kDa dextran) and pro-angiogenic cytokine panels.  
12. Development of a microfluidic retina-on-a-chip incorporating RPE, photoreceptor precursors and choroidal microvessels under pulsatile flow and diurnal light cycles. Test neuroprotective small molecules or gene therapies; readouts: photoreceptor outer segment integrity, RPE phagocytosis, fluid transport.  
13. High-throughput siRNA knockdown of epigenetic regulators (e.g., HDACs, methyltransferases) in senescent RPE to suppress the SASP (senescence-associated secretory phenotype). Measures: SA-β-gal staining, multiplex cytokine arrays (IL-6, IL-8), proliferation rescue and chromatin accessibility (ATAC-seq).  
14. Pharmacological activation of retinoic acid receptors in RPE to restore photoreceptor outer segment turnover. Assays: MERTK and MerTK ligand (Gas6) expression, phagolysosomal activity (DQ-BSA assay), outer segment shortening by time-lapse microscopy, cell viability under lipofuscin stress.  
15. Engineering naringenin-loaded lipid nanoparticles targeted to RPE (via RGD peptides) to reduce lipofuscin accumulation. Readouts: nanoparticle uptake (fluorescent tagging), lysosomal pH measurement (LysoSensor), autofluorescence quantification, cathepsin D activity and RPE survival under photo-oxidative stress.
