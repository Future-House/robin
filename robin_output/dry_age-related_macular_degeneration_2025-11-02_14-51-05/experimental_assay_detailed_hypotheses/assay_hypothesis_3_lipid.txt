Assay Overview: This assay models RPE waste-clearance failure by preloading ARPE‑19 or iPSC‑RPE with bis‑retinoid A2E or fluorescent fatty acids to generate lipofuscin-like autofluorescent granules (AFGs) and lipid droplets, then quantifies compound‑induced clearance as a function of autophagy/lipophagy and lysosomal competence. Primary readouts include total cellular autofluorescence by plate reader or flow cytometry, and lipid droplet burden by lipid‑dye high‑content imaging; mechanistic QC readouts (optional) include lysosomal pH, cathepsin activities (e.g., CTSD), and TFEB nuclear translocation. POS/UVPOS pulse–chase AFG formation can be run in parallel as a physiological orthogonal assay and to benchmark against rapamycin or TFEB activation controls (falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 26-29, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 13-18, falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 5-7, escrevente2021formationoflipofuscinlike pages 26-30).

Biomedical Evidence: Impaired autophagy/lysosomal processing in RPE is a central feature of AMD; failure to fully degrade POS promotes lipofuscin that inhibits lysosomal acidification, impairs proteolysis, and amplifies mitochondrial dysfunction and inflammation. Drusen are lipid‑rich, lipid droplet accumulation is characteristic of AMD, and lipophagy is a core RPE function linking photoreceptor lipid handling to waste clearance. Clinically, fundus AF increases with age/disease and RPE mtDNA damage correlates with AMD severity, supporting the biological relevance of AF/lipid readouts to pathophysiology, while acknowledging incomplete validation as predictive clinical biomarkers (kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3, kaarniranta2019fattyacidsand pages 6-10, lenin2023dysfunctionalautophagyproteostasis pages 2-4, si2023theroleof pages 5-7, pinelli2025retinalautophagyfor pages 11-12, pinelli2025retinalautophagyfor pages 8-11).

Previous Use: In ARPE‑19 and primary hfRPE, mTORC1 inhibition (rapamycin) reduces A2E and POS‑dependent AF by inducing autophagy/lysosomal biogenesis, with efficacy contingent on active lysosomal proteases/lipases; nuclear TFEB induction accompanies activity, and constitutively active TFEB reduces POS‑dependent AF. Small‑molecule screening has used UVPOS‑AF reductions against rapamycin benchmarks (e.g., INK128, Drug H), and pharmacologic lysosomal acidification decreases AF, whereas lysosomal pH elevation or CTSD inhibition robustly increases AF—validating AF as a sensitive lysosomal function readout; note UVPOS can increase cytokine secretion, a potential confounder (falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 13-18, falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 5-7, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 26-29, escrevente2021formationoflipofuscinlike pages 26-30).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths: mechanistically anchored to AMD biology; scalable, quantitative readouts; sensitive to lysosomal/autophagy modulation; prior use in discovery and mechanistic studies; orthogonal POS‑AFG module and genetic perturbations (e.g., CTSD KO) enable validation of on‑pathway effects (escrevente2021formationoflipofuscinlike pages 26-30, escrevente2021formationoflipofuscinlike pages 6-10, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 13-18). Limitations: A2E/FFA loading does not capture full POS‑derived lipofuscin complexity; AF endpoints are influenced by lysosomal pH and substrate quality independent of true clearance; diurnal autophagy rhythms and UVPOS‑induced cytokines can confound signal; and AF/lipid readouts lack qualification as predictive clinical biomarkers. Mitigate with orthogonal endpoints (lysosomal pH, cathepsin activity, TFEB localization), matched POS‑based assays, and time‑of‑day and inflammatory controls (kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3, escrevente2021formationoflipofuscinlike pages 1-6, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 26-29, si2023theroleof pages 5-7, escrevente2021formationoflipofuscinlike pages 26-30).

This assay is well‑justified mechanistically and practical for early-stage screening of autophagy/lysosome modulators in dry AMD. It should be paired with POS‑based AFG assays and mechanistic QC to ensure on‑pathway effects and reduce confounding. AF/lipid endpoints are translationally relevant but not validated clinical surrogates, so hits require in vivo confirmation (falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 13-18, escrevente2021formationoflipofuscinlike pages 26-30, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3).
