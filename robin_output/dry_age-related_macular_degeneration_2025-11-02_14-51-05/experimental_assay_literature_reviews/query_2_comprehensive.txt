Query: Comprehensive survey of in vitro cell culture assay platforms for dry AMD drug discovery, including primary and iPSC-derived RPE cells, 3D organoid and co-culture systems, complemented by quantitative assays for oxidative stress, mitochondrial respiration, phagocytosis, complement activation, inflammasome signaling, lipid droplet quantification, biomarker expression and high-content imaging readouts

Cell platforms span 2D RPE monocultures and advanced multicellular models. ARPE-19, primary human RPE, and ESC/hiPSC-derived RPE are widely used; automation can standardize donor-specific iPSC-RPE production and enable downstream functional testing (e.g., Seahorse) (boutin20193dengineeringof pages 14-17, truong2021automatinghumaninduced pages 14-20, wang2020d609protectsretinal pages 8-9). 3D retinal organoids/optic cups and engineered reporter lines (e.g., CRX-GFP; hiPSC GFP/YFP) support live imaging and focused screens; kinetic readouts (IncuCyte) and the 3D-ARQ platform have been validated in plate formats, though 3D systems are best suited for hit validation due to scalability limits (boutin20193dengineeringof pages 14-17, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 12-14). Microphysiological oBRB/retina-on-chip systems integrate RPE with endothelium/pericytes, incorporate TEER electrodes, and support permeability, secretome, and imaging assays, with guidance to substitute iPSC-RPE for higher physiological relevance (김지호2025developmentofa pages 113-117, kim2024high‐throughputmicrofluidic3d pages 12-15, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 98-103).

Quantitative assays include oxidative stress and mitochondrial endpoints: ROS by DCFH-DA; ATP/viability (CellTiter-Glo, MTT/CCK8); LDH and Annexin V/PI for cytotoxicity; mitochondrial membrane potential (TMRE/MitoTracker) and CCCP challenge; oxidative insults such as H2O2 or NaIO3 (wang2020d609protectsretinal pages 9-10, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 14-17, wang2020d609protectsretinal pages 1-2, wang2020d609protectsretinal pages 8-9). Bioenergetics is measured with Seahorse OCR/ECAR and related metabolic outputs (lactate, glucose, ATP), including cytokine-driven reprogramming (truong2021automatinghumaninduced pages 14-20, hansman2024metabolicreprogrammingof pages 1-2). Complement/drusen readouts use APOE and C5b9 immunostaining after complement-competent serum, connecting to complement–lipid–mitochondrial injury mechanisms; barrier function is quantified by TEER and 70 kDa FITC-dextran Papp, and VEGF/PEDF are measured by ELISA with polarity-sensitive secretion under oxidative stress (김지호2025developmentofa pages 74-81, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 98-103, kim2024high‐throughputmicrofluidic3d pages 12-15, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 113-117). Lipid droplet accumulation and EMT are quantified by high-content imaging and molecular assays in FFA-treated ARPE-19, enabling assessment of autophagy/lipophagy modulation (tsou2025liraglutideattenuatesffainduced pages 1-2).

High-content imaging workflows employ 384-well screening, calcein/Hoechst cytotoxicity, Operetta CLS quantification, reporter fluorescence, optical clearing to address melanin, and ML-based analysis; secretome profiling and HT gene-expression platforms extend multiplexed readouts (wang2020d609protectsretinal pages 8-9, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 14-17, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 12-14, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 17-19, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 1-3). Phagocytosis and inflammasome readouts are recognized needs but not enumerated in these excerpts (김지호2025developmentofa pages 113-117, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 12-14).

In vitro dry AMD discovery leverages 2D RPE (primary/iPSC/ARPE‑19), 3D organoids, and oBRB chips with TEER, permeability, secretome, and imaging readouts; iPSC-RPE and reporter systems improve human relevance and scalability (boutin20193dengineeringof pages 14-17, kim2024high‐throughputmicrofluidic3d pages 12-15, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 98-103). Robust quantitative assays cover ROS, viability, mitochondrial function (OCR/ECAR, TMRE), complement/drusen markers, barrier integrity, lipid droplets/EMT, and cytokine gene expression, embedded in HCS pipelines with optical clearing and ML (wang2020d609protectsretinal pages 9-10, truong2021automatinghumaninduced pages 14-20, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 74-81, wang2020d609protectsretinal pages 8-9, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 12-14). Phagocytosis and inflammasome assays remain gaps to be integrated into these platforms (김지호2025developmentofa pages 113-117).
