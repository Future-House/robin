Query: small molecules that induce mitophagy or enhance mitochondrial quality control pathways, such as PINK1/Parkin or BNIP3/NIX, in RPE cells, demonstrating improved bioenergetics, reduced oxidative stress, and protection in dry AMD preclinical models, with mechanistic confirmation

Several studies have identified promising small molecules that enhance mitophagy and mitochondrial quality control in RPE cells. For example, mitochondrial‐targeted antioxidants such as mitoTEMPO and TPP‐D‐NAC have been shown to effectively neutralize mitochondrial ROS, restore bioenergetics, and reverse EMT phenotypes in PINK1‐deficient RPE cells; mechanistic confirmation was provided through assays of LC3 lipidation, p62 dynamics, and mitochondrial PRKN recruitment (datta2023mitophagyinitiatesretrograde pages 1-2, datta2023mitophagyinitiatesretrograde pages 9-12). In addition, compounds like metformin, NAC, rapamycin, PQQ, and NMN have demonstrated increased mitochondrial function and reduced oxidative stress in RPE from AMD donors, suggesting they may act via improved mitochondrial biogenesis and enhanced autophagy/mitophagy pathways, although direct canonical pathway activation remains to be fully established (fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 10-10, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 9-10). Nutraceutical approaches employing Sirt1 activators (e.g., urolithin A, N1-methylnicotinamide, glucosamine) alongside AMPK activators (metformin, berberine), phase 2 inducers (lipoic acid, sulforaphane) and PPARα agonists (astaxanthin), as well as melatonin, also offer additional avenues to modulate either PINK1/Parkin or BNIP3/NIX-mediated mitophagy in RPE (lujan2022nutraceuticalsdrugspromotingmitophagy pages 5-7). Furthermore, notoginsenoside R1 has been shown to increase both PINK1 and Parkin expression with mechanistic reversal by siRNA, providing further evidence of its therapeutic potential (skeie2021mitophagyanemerging pages 9-11).

These findings indicate that targeting mitochondrial quality control pathways improves bioenergetics and reduces oxidative stress in dry AMD preclinical models. These small molecules hold significant potential for mitigating RPE degeneration and warrant further investigation.
