Query: emerging molecular pathways regulating RPE mitochondrial quality control, such as DJ-1, PINK1/Parkin, mitofusin, and Drp1, that are dysregulated in dry AMD, with validation of target expression, functional assays, and potential small-molecule modulators

Mitochondrial quality control (MQC) is perturbed in RPE-relevant AMD models, with the strongest direct evidence for PINK1/Parkin-mediated mitophagy. PINK1 deficiency in mice induces impaired mitophagy, disturbed mitochondrial function, and EMT in the RPE, and models resembling dry AMD show reduced PINK1/Parkin-dependent mitophagy (hyttinen2023noncodingrnasregulating pages 2-4, hyttinen2023noncodingrnasregulating pages 10-11). miRNAs that downregulate PINK1 or Parkin (e.g., miR-27a/b, miR-34a, miR-181a) suppress mitophagy and increase oxidative stress in non-RPE systems, with miR-181a effects mirrored in a dAMD mouse model, supporting pathway vulnerability in AMD-like contexts (hyttinen2023noncodingrnasregulating pages 10-11). In RPE, knockdown of miR-494-3p reduces ATP and membrane potential, and oxidative stress drives release of miR-494-3p in extracellular vesicles, indicating MQC-linked functional readouts in human RPE (hyttinen2023noncodingrnasregulating pages 10-11).

Fusion/fission regulators are implicated mechanistically: miR-19b suppresses MFN1, suggesting impaired fusion; Drp1 dysregulation is mechanistically relevant but not directly demonstrated in the provided RPE/dAMD data (hyttinen2023noncodingrnasregulating pages 10-11). DJ-1 (PARK7) is discussed primarily in PD; Open Targets lacks curated dry AMD associations for PARK7, PINK1, PRKN, MFN1/2, or DNM1L, underscoring limited target validation in dry AMD datasets (OpenTargets Search: dry age-related macular degeneration-PARK7,PINK1,PRKN,MFN1,MFN2,DNM1L). Methodologically, validated mitophagy reporters (mitoQC, mtKeima), stress triggers (CCCP, H2O2, rotenone), and transport/fission readouts (Miro turnover; Drp1 overexpression rescue) offer assay frameworks to functionally interrogate these pathways in RPE models (bernardo2023inibizionediusp14a pages 23-27).

Multiple small molecules modulate these nodes, albeit with no reported testing in RPE or dry AMD in the excerpts. PINK1/Parkin modulators include kinetin/kinetin triphosphate, kaempferol, rhapontigenin, SR3677, pifithrin-α, VL-004, T-271, and USP30 inhibitors (MF-094; 15-oxospiramilactone), as well as Parkin-independent iron chelators (deferiprone) and siderophores (1,10-phenanthroline) that drive DRP1-dependent fragmentation; curcumin and sulforaphane activate upstream mitophagy programs (hong2024targetingmitochondrialquality pages 13-14, hong2024targetingmitochondrialquality pages 17-18, palikaras2018mitophagymodulators pages 5-7, lu2023cellularmitophagymechanism pages 16-18). Fusion/fission modulators include MFN agonists (S3, S89, MASM7), OPA1-targeting paeonol, and DRP1 inhibitors (Mdivi-1, P110, dynasore); phenanthroline and flubendazole induce DRP1-dependent mitophagy but raise specificity/toxicity concerns (hong2024targetingmitochondrialquality pages 13-14, hong2024targetingmitochondrialquality pages 17-18, hong2024targetingmitochondrialquality pages 14-17, lu2023cellularmitophagymechanism pages 16-18). Neurodegeneration studies corroborate MFN/OPA1 engagement and DRP1 inhibition (e.g., SS-31, BGP-15, Mdivi-1), again without RPE/dAMD testing here (choi2024targetingmitochondrialdysfunction pages 12-14).

Current data support PINK1/Parkin impairment and miRNA-driven MQC disruption in AMD-relevant settings, with limited direct validation for DJ-1, MFN1/2, and Drp1 in human RPE/dry AMD. Numerous small-molecule MQC modulators exist, but the excerpts report no RPE or dry AMD efficacy data. Focused RPE assays using mitoQC/mtKeima, mitochondrial bioenergetics, and fusion/fission readouts are warranted to translate these pathways into dry AMD therapeutics (hyttinen2023noncodingrnasregulating pages 2-4, hyttinen2023noncodingrnasregulating pages 10-11, hong2024targetingmitochondrialquality pages 17-18, hong2024targetingmitochondrialquality pages 14-17, OpenTargets Search: dry age-related macular degeneration-PARK7,PINK1,PRKN,MFN1,MFN2,DNM1L, bernardo2023inibizionediusp14a pages 23-27).

PINK1/Parkin mitophagy shows the clearest AMD-relevant dysregulation in RPE models, whereas direct RPE/dry AMD validation for DJ-1, MFN1/2, and Drp1 remains sparse in the provided sources. Multiple preclinical small molecules target these nodes, but none are reported here as tested in RPE or dry AMD models. Prioritized RPE functional assays and reporter systems should be applied to these targets to establish translatability (hyttinen2023noncodingrnasregulating pages 2-4, hong2024targetingmitochondrialquality pages 13-14, hong2024targetingmitochondrialquality pages 17-18, OpenTargets Search: dry age-related macular degeneration-PARK7,PINK1,PRKN,MFN1,MFN2,DNM1L, bernardo2023inibizionediusp14a pages 23-27).
