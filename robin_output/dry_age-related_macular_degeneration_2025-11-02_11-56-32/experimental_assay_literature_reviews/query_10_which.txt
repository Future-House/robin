Query: Which quantitative high-throughput screening assay formats (96-, 384-, or 1536-well plates) have been adapted to measure dry AMD–relevant phenotypes—such as RPE viability, TEER barrier function, mitochondrial health, and fluorescent or luminescent reporter signals—and what validation metrics (Z′ factor, signal-to-background, reproducibility) ensure assay robustness?

- 96-well: A multiplex bead/TaqMan gene-expression assay was implemented in 96-well plates to quantify RPE lineage, disease, and functional markers; validation included correlation to qRT–PCR (e.g., r = 0.687 in iPS cells; r = 0.751 in primary RPE) and characterization of linear dynamic range via serial dilutions, but no Z’/S/B/CV were reported in the excerpt (ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 9-11, ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 7-8).

- 384-well: The same multiplex gene-expression assay was optimized to 384-well plates with reduced volumes and halved bead loads, yielding near-identical performance and high concordance with qRT–PCR (Pearson r = 0.91 high-bead, 0.89 low-bead; LODs defined; linear response demonstrated) (ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 11-12, ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 8-9, ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 7-8). RPE viability assays (CellTiter-Glo ATP readout) and GFP reporter-based primary/secondary chemical screens were run in 384-well plates with control-based normalization and 4-parameter dose–response/CRC scoring; Z’/S/B/CV were not provided in the excerpt (sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 16-16, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 16-17, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 15-16). A 384-well Gaussia luciferase reporter assay reported robust plate Z’ = 0.71–0.78 across plates, supporting HTS-quality luminescent reporter measurements (hulleman2013ahighthroughputcellbased pages 20-20).

- 1536-well: Not reported in these excerpts (ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 2-3, ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 11-12).

- TEER barrier and mitochondrial health: TEER was measured electrophysiologically on transwells (not in 96/384 HTS format); no mitochondrial-health HTS assays were described (ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 4-5, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 15-16).

Validation metrics ensuring robustness:
- Z’ factor: robust Z’ ~0.71–0.78 for 384-well Gaussia luciferase; a separate RPE cytotoxicity screen used Z’≥0.7 as a plate-acceptance threshold (plate format not specified) (hulleman2013ahighthroughputcellbased pages 20-20, schustak2024phenotypichighthroughputscreening pages 11-13).
- Orthogonal concordance and dynamic range: multiplex gene-expression assays showed high Pearson correlations to qRT–PCR in 96- and 384-well formats, with defined LODs and linear dilution-series performance; bead-load halving did not degrade data quality (ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 11-12, ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 8-9, ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 9-11, ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 7-8).
- Control-based normalization and curve-fitting: 384-well viability/GFP assays used positive/negative controls, normalized readouts, and 4-parameter curve fitting with CRC scoring; explicit Z’/S/B/CV were not reported (sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 16-16, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 16-17).

In summary, 96- and 384-well formats are documented: multiplex gene expression in both, and 384-well viability and fluorescence/luminescence reporters; 1536-well is not reported. Robustness metrics include Z’≈0.7–0.8 for a 384-well luciferase reporter and strong qRT–PCR concordance and dilution linearity for multiplex gene expression; Z’/S/B/CV for the iRPE 384-well viability/GFP assays are not provided in these excerpts (hulleman2013ahighthroughputcellbased pages 20-20, ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 11-12, ferrer2014amultiplexhighthroughput pages 8-9, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 16-16).
