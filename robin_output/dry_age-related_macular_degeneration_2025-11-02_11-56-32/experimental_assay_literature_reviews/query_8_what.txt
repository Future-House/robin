Query: What classes of small molecules and biologics targeting complement cascade components, mitochondrial bioenergetics, autophagy regulators, and anti-oxidative pathways have been tested in RPE cell assays for dry AMD, and what assay designs elucidated their mechanisms of action?

Complement-directed agents tested in RPE-centric systems include C1-pathway inhibitors (plasma C1-esterase inhibitor, anti‑C1s mAb), C3 inhibitors (compstatin analogs), C3a receptor antagonists, and C5 inhibition. Assays that elucidated mechanism used primary human RPE or RPE lines exposed to anaphylatoxins C3a/C5a to quantify rapid VEGF‑A and MMP‑2/‑9 induction; RPE cultures modeling drusen with C1q‑depleted serum with terminal complement (C5b‑9) readouts; AMD‑genotype (CFH Y402H) RPE where compstatin restored lysosomal function; and fetal RPE in which C3a drove sub‑RPE deposit formation that was blocked by a C3aR antagonist, including a human RPE high‑throughput model validated by compstatin inhibition (kleinman2016complementactivationand pages 4-6, ong2024thecomplementsystem pages 7-9).

Mitochondrial bioenergetics modulators span NAD+ boosters (NMN, nicotinamide), PGC‑1α activators (PU‑91, ZLN005), mitochondria‑targeted niacin (TPP‑Niacin), mitochondria‑targeted antioxidants (SkQ1), the cardiolipin‑binding peptide elamipretide, and AMPK/mitophagy enhancers (metformin, AICAR, trehalose). Assays used ARPE‑19 oxidative‑insult paradigms (H2O2, oxLDL, NaIO3) with viability, ROS, mitochondrial membrane potential, complex activities, and antioxidant gene expression; Seahorse/respirometry in iPSC‑RPE to capture basal/maximal respiration, spare capacity, and ATP; mitochondrial biogenesis markers (PGC‑1α, mtDNA copy number) and AMD cybrid models; hydroquinone‑challenged RPE for the integrin antagonist risuteganib; and membrane‑potential/ATP measurements for photobiomodulation targeting cytochrome c oxidase (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21, lenin2023dysfunctionalautophagyproteostasis pages 11-13, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 15-17).

Autophagy/regulated‑cell‑death modulators include AMPK activators (metformin, AICAR), trehalose, and mTOR inhibition (rapamycin), plus necrostatin‑1 (RIPK1/RIPK3) and the zinc ionophore clioquinol (restoring Aβ autophagic clearance). Mechanistic designs quantified autophagic flux (LC3‑II/LC3‑I, p62), autophagosome–autolysosome imaging, lysosomal pH/V‑ATPase function and MTORC1 signaling, alongside necroptosis/pyroptosis markers (RIPK1/RIPK3, NLRP3, procaspase‑1) under oxidative stress in RPE (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16).

Anti‑oxidative/inflammasome modulators tested in RPE include zinc (Nrf2 upregulation, NOX inhibition), idebenone (reducing ROS/senescence and shifting BAX/Bcl‑2), dimethyl fumarate (Keap1–Nrf2 activation), entacapone (A2E anti‑aggregation and blue‑light ROS reduction), and fluoxetine (direct NLRP3 binding to block inflammasome assembly), assessed via A2E aggregation/phototoxicity, ROS, senescence/apoptosis markers, and Nrf2/ARE transcriptional readouts (marchesi2024differenttherapeuticapproaches pages 19-20, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21). A mitochondrial‑derived peptide, humanin, increased mitochondrial biogenesis/mtDNA, activated STAT3, suppressed caspase‑3, and prevented oxidative apoptosis in ARPE‑19, including nanoparticle delivery formats (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21).

Multiple therapeutic classes—complement inhibitors, mitochondrial bioenergetics enhancers, autophagy/necrosis modulators, and antioxidants—have been functionally profiled in RPE assays. Designs span anaphylatoxin stimulation, oxidative‑insult models, Seahorse bioenergetics, autophagic flux/lysosomal pH, inflammasome assays, and deposit‑formation paradigms to link target engagement to RPE phenotypes (kleinman2016complementactivationand pages 4-6, ong2024thecomplementsystem pages 7-9, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21, marchesi2024differenttherapeuticapproaches pages 19-20).
