Query: How can co-culture systems combining RPE cells with microglia, macrophages, or choroidal endothelial cells be established to investigate cell-cell interactions driving chronic inflammation and complement-mediated damage in dry AMD, and what quantitative readouts indicate pathological crosstalk?

Build complementary architectures to capture contact-dependent and paracrine crosstalk. For microglia–RPE, isolate retinal microglia (e.g., CX3CR1+/GFP), activate with LPS, then co-culture directly with primary RPE or embed microglia in Matrigel for in vitro co-culture; transplant into the subretinal space for in vivo validation and CNV assessment by DiI perfusion and flatmounts (quantify CNV area, RPE morphological disruption, junctional protein loss, and decreased RPE65) (ma2009microgliainthe pages 9-10, ma2009microgliainthe pages 1-2). For macrophage–RPE, condition bone marrow–derived macrophages by co-culturing with damaged RPE (autophagy-impaired with WORT/CQ or Beclin‑1 siRNA; mitochondrial insult with ROT; oxidative stress with H2O2/PQ); macrophages phagocytose dying RPE within 60 min, unengulfed debris is removed at 2 h, and readouts collected up to 24 h (contact controls via transwells, heat‑killed RPE as negative control) (liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 2-4, liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 4-5). For RPE–endothelial coupling, use long‑term polarized transwells with ARPE‑19 on the top and HUVEC on the underside, or microfluidic dual‑channel devices with a porous membrane; quantify barrier function (TEER/FITC‑dextran), ECM deposition (collagen IV/fibronectin/fibrillin), endothelial migration/tube formation, and VEGF/PlGF secretion under defined stressors (e.g., low glucose, CoCl2) (spencer2017identificationofa pages 1-2, chen2017microfluidiccoculturesof pages 2-3). Organotypic porcine retina–RPE co‑cultures and transwells enable polarized sampling, complement assays, and TLR-driven epithelial responses (fietz2023cocultivationofprimary pages 16-16, wagner2022impactofprimary pages 12-15).

Quantitative indicators of pathological crosstalk include:
- Macrophage inflammasome activation: cleaved caspase‑1 p20, mature IL‑1β (IL‑18 often low), NO; dependency on phagocytosis; localization of caspase‑1 to infiltrating macrophages in vivo (liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 4-5, liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 9-10, liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 7-9).  
- Pro‑angiogenic programming: CXCL1, CCL2, proliferin, Serpin E1/PAI‑1, TIMP‑1 (± IGFBP‑9/PlGF‑2); choroidal explant sprouting and endothelial proliferation/sprouting with conditioned media; VEGF may be unchanged in macrophage supernatants (liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 4-5, ma2009microgliainthe pages 9-10).  
- RPE stress/viability: OCR/ECAR, LC3B puncta, LDH release, caspase‑3, mitoSOX; IL‑1β cytotoxicity and low‑dose IL‑6 protection; downregulation of IRAK3/IL‑1Ra increases IL‑1β susceptibility (liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 2-4, liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 9-10).  
- Complement/inflammasome: NLRP3, PYCARD, pro/cleaved caspase‑1, IL‑1β/IL‑18, IRAK3/4, Otx2, and complement components/regulators in media/tissues (C1q, C3/C3a, C4, sC5b‑9, C5aR, CFH/CFI/C4BP); MAC deposition and C3/CFH mRNA in organotypic systems (wong2022exploringthepathogenesis pages 13-15, mohlin2018amodelto pages 1-2, wagner2022impactofprimary pages 12-15).  
- Barrier/morphology: TEER/permeability, ZO‑1/claudin‑1 localization, phalloidin‑based RPE architecture metrics, and fractional area of abnormal RPE distribution (ma2009microgliainthe pages 9-10, chen2017microfluidiccoculturesof pages 2-3, fietz2023cocultivationofprimary pages 16-16).

Oxidative adducts such as CEP can be applied to RPE and myeloid cells to model antigen‑specific, IFN‑γ/IL‑17‑producing T‑cell–driven M1 polarization; measure M1 markers, RPE‑derived M1 factors, and retinal pro‑inflammatory gene induction (cruzguilloty2014tcellsand pages 1-2).

Establish direct and separated co-cultures pairing RPE with microglia/macrophages or endothelial cells using primary or ARPE‑19 RPE on transwells, microfluidics, or organotypic explants. Induce RPE stress (autophagy blockade, oxidative/mitochondrial injury, CEP) and quantify macrophage inflammasomes/cytokines, complement activation, RPE stress/barrier defects, and endothelial angiogenic behavior as objective readouts of pathological crosstalk (liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 2-4, liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 4-5, ma2009microgliainthe pages 9-10, spencer2017identificationofa pages 1-2, chen2017microfluidiccoculturesof pages 2-3, wong2022exploringthepathogenesis pages 13-15, mohlin2018amodelto pages 1-2).
