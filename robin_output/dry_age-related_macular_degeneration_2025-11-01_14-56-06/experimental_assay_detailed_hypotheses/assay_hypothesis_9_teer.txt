Assay Overview: A transepithelial electrical resistance (TEER) assay measures tight‑junction–dependent barrier integrity of a polarized retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) monolayer cultured on a permeable support (Transwell or chip). Resistance is recorded with standard chopstick electrodes (e.g., EVOM/STX4) and reported as Ω·cm² after blank subtraction; complementary readouts include ZO‑1 junctional immunostaining and macromolecular tracer permeability (e.g., 70 kDa FITC‑dextran) to validate paracellular leak. Overall Evaluation: Strengths—mechanistically proximal, quantitative, and sensitive to early, reversible tight‑junction dysfunction; correlates with permeability; scalable for screening; compatible with co‑culture and molecular readouts (VEGF/PEDF, complement).
