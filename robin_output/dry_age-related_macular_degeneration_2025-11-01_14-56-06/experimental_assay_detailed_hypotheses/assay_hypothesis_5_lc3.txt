Assay Overview:  
The LC3‐II autophagic flux immunoblot assay quantifies the ratio of lipidated LC3‐II to non‐lipidated LC3‐I by Western blot, employing lysosomal inhibitors (E64d/pepstatin A) to block degradation and reveal active autophagosome formation (golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 5-9). This assay is applied in retinal pigment epithelial (RPE) cell cultures, a relevant in vitro model for dry age‐related macular degeneration (AMD), to monitor autophagic flux dynamics under stress conditions such as nutrient starvation or oxidative injury.

Biomedical Evidence:  
Defective autophagy is central to dry AMD pathogenesis, with RPE cells displaying impaired induction of autophagy, accumulation of autophagic structures, and lysosomal dysfunction (golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 5-9, golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 1-2). Numerous studies have linked decreased autophagic flux with mitochondrial dysfunction, altered energy production, and elevated oxidative stress, all of which contribute significantly to AMD progression (kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3, kaarniranta2017autophagyregulatesdeath pages 4-7). The assay’s ability to report on LC3 processing makes it a critical readout to understand the underlying autophagy deficits driving cellular degeneration in AMD.

Previous Use:  
This assay has been widely used to elucidate autophagic impairments in RPE cells, demonstrating that AMD RPE fail to increase LC3‐II/LC3‐I ratios under stress conditions (golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 5-9, golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 1-2). Additionally, it has served as a platform to test the reversal of autophagy blockade by candidate compounds, where restoration of normal flux has been correlated with improvements in cellular homeostasis (gupta2023increasedlcn2(lipocalin pages 4-5, golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 10-11).

Overall Evaluation:  
Strengths include its quantitative nature and the robustness of detecting dynamic autophagic flux, particularly when paired with complementary markers such as p62 and lysosomal imaging (szatmaritoth2016clearanceofautophagyassociated pages 10-11, szatmaritoth2016clearanceofautophagyassociated pages 11-12). However, reliance on LC3‐II alone can be ambiguous without orthogonal readouts, and the assay is sensitive to experimental timelines and inhibitor efficacy (ueno2020monitoringautophagyflux pages 1-2).  
This assay is a well‐validated method to assess autophagic flux in RPE cells and provides a crucial measure of a disease‐relevant mechanism in dry AMD. Its integration with additional endpoints makes it a valuable tool for early-stage drug discovery in this context.
