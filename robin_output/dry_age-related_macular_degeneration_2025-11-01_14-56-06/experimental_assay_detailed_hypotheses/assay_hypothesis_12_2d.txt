Assay Overview: Long-term, polarized RPE monolayers cultured on permeable inserts deposit sub-RPE, drusen-like material that can be driven and modulated by human serum and oxidized lipids. Readouts include: neutral lipid burden (Oil Red O or Nile red), mineralization (Von Kossa or HAP probes), and immunofluorescence for drusen markers (APOE, TIMP3, vitronectin, CFH, collagen V). Whole-well tiled imaging with semi-automated segmentation yields deposit count, area, and size distributions longitudinally; TEER and tight-junction (ZO-1) morphology index monolayer integrity. Primary RPE (porcine or human) or hiPSC-RPE on porous supports are suitable substrates for these measurements (shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 6-7, shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 1-2, shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 3-6, chen2020unstimulatedserumfreecultures pages 5-6, curcio2018softdrusenin pages 7-8, curcio2018softdrusenin pages 11-12, mesch2021…foragerelated pages 74-77, mesch2021…foragerelateda pages 77-79, chen2020unstimulatedserumfreecultures pages 6-6).

Biomedical Evidence: Soft drusen are lipid-rich with protein and hydroxyapatite components, originate from polarized RPE lipoprotein secretion toward Bruch’s membrane, and are dynamic in patients—features reproduced in long-term transwell RPE cultures (curcio2018softdrusenin pages 7-8, curcio2018softdrusenin pages 11-12, shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 6-7). Aging Bruch’s membrane and restricted permeability favor basal retention, justifying permeable-insert models; serum exposure increases complement content, matching clinical inflammatory signatures (chen2020unstimulatedserumfreecultures pages 6-6, mesch2021…foragerelateda pages 77-79). In vitro deposits contain APOE, vitronectin, CFH, collagen V, and hydroxyapatite, aligning with human drusen composition (shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 6-7, chen2020unstimulatedserumfreecultures pages 5-6, curcio2018softdrusenin pages 7-8, chen2020unstimulatedserumfreecultures pages 6-6).

Previous Use: Multiple groups have shown autonomous formation of drusen-like basal deposits by 8–24+ weeks with quantifiable growth in number and size, modulation by serum, and complement enrichment; genotype sensitivity has been noted in hiPSC-RPE (curcio2018softdrusenin pages 8-9, shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 6-7, shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 1-2, mesch2021…foragerelateda pages 77-79). While these platforms have been explicitly proposed for screening and map to druggable pathways (e.g., lipid-handling “oil-spill” strategies), published pharmacologic screens and assay performance metrics are sparse or absent in these excerpts (shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 6-7, curcio2018softdrusenin pages 11-12, chen2020unstimulatedserumfreecultures pages 5-6).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths—disease-relevant biology (RPE-driven, polarized secretion), multi-parametric readouts (lipid, mineral, protein, dynamics), and semi-automated quantification suitable for phenotypic screening (shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 6-7, shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 1-2, curcio2018softdrusenin pages 7-8). Limitations—lack of native Bruch’s membrane/choroid and vascular clearance, deposit retention by pores, inter-preparation variability, declining RPE health after ~16–20 weeks, and dye-specific biases (e.g., Nile red misses protein/mineral) that constrain temporal resolution and may underestimate burden; apical serum exposure in some systems diverges from physiology (curcio2018softdrusenin pages 8-9, shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 6-7, shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 3-6, mesch2021…foragerelateda pages 77-79, chen2020unstimulatedserumfreecultures pages 6-6). Net: valuable for ranking deposit-modulating agents and mechanistic dissection, but requires careful controls and complementary models to de-risk translational predictions.

This assay models a central AMD phenotype—RPE-driven, lipid/protein/mineral sub-RPE deposit formation—with validated molecular signatures and quantifiable kinetics. It is well-suited for phenotypic discovery and mechanism testing but lacks full tissue context and validated screening benchmarks; pairing with orthogonal models is advised (shaw2024characterizationofdrusen pages 6-7, curcio2018softdrusenin pages 8-9, curcio2018softdrusenin pages 11-12, chen2020unstimulatedserumfreecultures pages 5-6).
