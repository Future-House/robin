Query: What is the detailed biochemical composition of drusen deposits in dry AMD, including lipids, proteins, and advanced glycation end products, and how can synthetic or cell-derived drusen-like aggregates be engineered and quantified in RPE culture for use in drug screening?

Drusen in dry AMD are extracellular, insoluble aggregates in the RPE basal labyrinth/Bruch’s membrane, with approximately 40% lipid content, hence the “oil leak on BrM” descriptor (basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 2-4). The lipid fraction is dominated by neutral lipids—esterified and unesterified cholesterol and phosphatidylcholine—with contributions from RPE-secreted apoB/MTP-dependent lipoproteins and retained/chemically modified circulating lipoproteins that yield lipoprotein-derived debris (algvere2016drusenmaculopathya pages 2-3, tang2024aberrantlipidmetabolism pages 5-6). Oxidative and glycation chemistry is prominent: peroxidized lipids, carboxyethyl pyrrole adducts, and oxidized proteins coexist with advanced glycation end products (e.g., carboxymethyllysine) and lipid peroxidation adducts (MDA, 4‑HNE) (basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 2-4, algvere2016drusenmaculopathya pages 2-3, zhang2021drusenandpachydrusen pages 7-8, baek2018quantitativeproteomicanalysis pages 12-13). Proteins are extensive (>120), including apolipoproteins (apoE, apoB), inflammatory/immune proteins (vitronectin, amyloid P, CRP), amyloid‑β, immunoglobulin light chains, coagulation/complement factors (factor X; C3, C5, C5b‑9, C9), clusterin, serum albumin, crystallins, TIMP3, and ECM proteins (collagens I–VI, elastin, EFEMP1, thrombospondin‑1, MFG‑E8) (algvere2016drusenmaculopathya pages 2-3, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 2-4, zhang2021drusenandpachydrusen pages 7-8). Drusen also contain PAS‑positive glycoconjugates (glycogen, glycoproteins, proteoglycans, mucopolysaccharides), zinc, and calcific mineral, including hydroxyapatite/calcified nodules (zhang2021drusenandpachydrusen pages 7-8, algvere2016drusenmaculopathya pages 2-3, zhang2021drusenandpachydrusen pages 11-12).

Engineering and quantification in vitro:
- 2D RPE on permeable supports form basal laminar/sub‑RPE deposits over months; albumin/retinal homogenate or complement-competent human serum drive APOE, vitronectin, clusterin, serum amyloid P, and C5b‑9 deposition, with complement‑induced mitochondrial injury proposed to nucleate aggregates (bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 16-17). Deposit polarity is tunable with oxidized lipids; quantification includes Oil Red O, Von Kossa/hydroxyapatite fluorescence, IF for APOE/vitronectin/clusterin/SAP/MAC/C3 fragments, mass spectrometry, complement gene panels, TEER, permeability assays, and organ‑on‑chip imaging (bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 16-17, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 14-16).
- 3D RPE spheroids generate a BrM‑like surface and drusen-like deposits enriched for APP, CFH, vitronectin, and apoE; glycoxidized “artificial lipofuscin” loading and oxidative/smoking mimetics increase deposit frequency; readouts include live imaging, autofluorescence, TEM, and marker quantification (usui2019invitrodrusen pages 4-7, usui2019invitrodrusen pages 7-10, usui2019invitrodrusen pages 1-4).
- RPE organoids enable long‑term autonomous deposits; aging/stress increase TIMP3/APOE and C3/CFH, with lipid (Nile Red), hydroxyapatite (Osteosense), serum‑modulated complement, and POS/A2E/blue‑light/smoke‑induced acceleration as readouts (mesch2021…foragerelated pages 77-79).
- Mineral/lipid nucleators (synthetic hydroxyapatite or cholesterol particles) are supported by primary culture deposition of hydroxyapatite; hiPSC‑RPE platforms and automation support scalable, patient‑specific drug screening (mesch2021…foragerelated pages 92-95, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 28-29).

Drusen comprise lipid-rich (cholesterol- and phosphatidylcholine-dominated) deposits intermixed with oxidized/glycated proteins, abundant complement/apolipoproteins, ECM/glycoconjugates, zinc, and calcific hydroxyapatite (algvere2016drusenmaculopathya pages 2-3, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 2-4, zhang2021drusenandpachydrusen pages 7-8, zhang2021drusenandpachydrusen pages 11-12). Robust in vitro models include long-term 2D RPE, 3D spheroids, and organoids, augmented by serum, oxidized lipids, lipofuscin mimetics, or POS/A2E, with multiparametric quantification (lipid/mineral stains, IF/proteomics, complement gene panels, TEER/permeability, live/TEM imaging) suitable for drug screening (bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 16-17, usui2019invitrodrusen pages 4-7, mesch2021…foragerelated pages 77-79, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 14-16).
