Query: What are the key molecular and cellular mechanisms driving retinal pigment epithelial cell death in dry age-related macular degeneration, including oxidative stress pathways, mitochondrial dysfunction, endoplasmic reticulum stress responses, and caspase-dependent or ‑independent apoptotic cascades, and how can each be reliably modeled in vitro to test therapeutic candidates?

Dry AMD RPE loss arises from converging oxidative, mitochondrial, lysosomal/proteostatic, and ER-stress pathways that culminate in apoptosis and, in some contexts, alternative regulated cell death programs (necroptosis, pyroptosis, ferroptosis) (chen2023oxidativestressin pages 1-2, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 5-7, bilbaomalave2021mitochondrialdysfunctionand pages 22-23, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 10-11).

Oxidative stress: Endogenous mitochondrial ROS (complexes I–IV), phagocytosis-associated respiratory bursts, photo-oxidation of bisretinoids/lipofuscin (A2E), lipid peroxidation of PUFA-rich POS, and lifestyle oxidants (smoking, diet) elevate ROS beyond antioxidant capacity; aging blunts NRF2-driven responses, increasing vulnerability (chen2023oxidativestressin pages 1-2, lenin2023dysfunctionalautophagyproteostasis pages 15-16, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 5-7, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 2-3, kaarniranta2019roleofmitochondrial pages 13-15). In vitro, expose ARPE-19 or human RPE to H2O2, load A2E and apply blue light, feed isolated POS, or treat with cigarette smoke extract to model oxidative/photo-oxidative and phagocytic stress, then quantify ROS, antioxidant gene induction, and viability/apoptosis (lenin2023dysfunctionalautophagyproteostasis pages 15-16, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 5-7, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 10-11).

Mitochondrial dysfunction: AMD RPE exhibit mtDNA damage, cristae loss, reduced respiration/ATP, excess ROS, imbalanced fusion/fission, impaired biogenesis and mitophagy; modulators include OPA1/MFN/DRP1, PGAM5, and PINK1–Parkin (kaarniranta2019roleofmitochondrial pages 3-5, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 10-11, si2023theroleof pages 16-17). Model by genetic/chemical perturbation of fusion/fission or PINK1–Parkin, or by chronic oxidative/CSE exposure; readouts include mitochondrial membrane potential, ATP/respiration, mtDNA damage, and mitophagy markers, ideally in iPSC/primary RPE from AMD vs control donors (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 10-11, kaarniranta2019roleofmitochondrial pages 3-5, si2023theroleof pages 16-17).

Proteostasis and lysosome–autophagy failure: High POS load with defective MerTK/αvβ5 signaling and lysosomal protease insufficiency (e.g., CTSD) promotes lipofuscin/drusen, impairs proteasome, and suppresses autophagic flux via mTOR/AMPK dysregulation and p62–Keap1–Nrf2 disruption (si2023theroleof pages 4-5, lenin2023dysfunctionalautophagyproteostasis pages 2-4, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 5-7). Model with POS-feeding assays, MerTK pathway perturbation, lysosomal/autophagy modulation, and chronic oxidative stress while measuring phagosome–lysosome fusion, autophagic flux, and protein/lipid aggregates (si2023theroleof pages 4-5, lenin2023dysfunctionalautophagyproteostasis pages 2-4).

ER stress: Oxidative/mitochondrial injury drives maladaptive UPR signaling that shifts from protective to pro-apoptotic and pro-inflammatory states, reinforcing mitochondrial damage (bilbaomalave2021mitochondrialdysfunctionand pages 22-23). Model by applying sustained oxidative/mitochondrial stress and assaying ER-stress/UPR markers alongside apoptosis and inflammatory outputs to evaluate ER-targeted candidates (bilbaomalave2021mitochondrialdysfunctionand pages 22-23).

Cell-death execution: Intrinsic apoptosis proceeds via mitochondrial outer-membrane permeabilization (BAX/BAK), release of cytochrome c/SMAC/Omi, and caspase-9 activation; H2O2 robustly triggers RPE apoptosis in vitro (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 10-11, lenin2023dysfunctionalautophagyproteostasis pages 15-16). Non-apoptotic programs can be engaged: mitochondrial changes accompany necroptosis/pyroptosis/ferroptosis in some models, and mtDNA/NLRP3 inflammasome and CD36–oxLDL axes link mitochondrial/oxidative stress to inflammatory death signaling; model by inducing oxidative/mtDNA release or oxLDL uptake and measuring cytochrome c/caspases versus inflammasome activation and cell death modality markers (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 10-11, si2023theroleof pages 16-17, si2023theroleof pages 9-10).

RPE death in dry AMD is driven by ROS overload, mitochondrial dysfunction, lysosome–autophagy failure, and maladaptive ER stress, converging on intrinsic apoptosis and occasionally other regulated death programs (chen2023oxidativestressin pages 1-2, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 10-11, bilbaomalave2021mitochondrialdysfunctionand pages 22-23). Reliable in vitro modeling uses H2O2, A2E+blue light, POS feeding, CSE, and genetic manipulation of mitochondrial dynamics/mitophagy and phagolysosomal pathways, with readouts spanning redox, bioenergetics, autophagy/lysosome, UPR, and apoptotic execution (lenin2023dysfunctionalautophagyproteostasis pages 15-16, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 10-11, si2023theroleof pages 4-5). These platforms enable mechanism-aligned testing of antioxidant, mitochondrial, lysosomal/autophagy, and ER-stress–targeted therapeutics (bilbaomalave2021mitochondrialdysfunctionand pages 22-23, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 5-7).
