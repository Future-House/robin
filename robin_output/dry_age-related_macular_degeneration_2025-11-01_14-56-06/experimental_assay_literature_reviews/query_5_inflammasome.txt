Query: Inflammasome activation and inflammatory cytokine release in dry AMD pathobiology: identifying cell culture methods to assess NLRP3 assembly, caspase-1 activation, IL-1β/IL-18 secretion, and effects of small-molecule inhibitors in primary RPE models

Model and controls. Use primary human fetal RPE (hfRPE; passages 3–5) and/or hiPSC‑RPE, maintained at 37°C, 5% CO2; serum‑starve 24 h and perform treatments in serum‑free media to synchronize cultures (ARPE‑19 conditions are transferable to primary RPE) (kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 7-8). Include rigorous positive immune‑cell controls (THP‑1: LPS 10 μg/mL + ATP 5 mM; RAW‑264.7: LPS 10 ng/mL, defined loads and induction) and negative controls (Nlrp3−/− tissue; iPSC‑RPE lacking NLRP3) to benchmark assay performance and specificity (kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 2-3, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 7-8). Given that NLRP3 mRNA/protein are near or below detection in RPE even after diverse “priming/activation” regimens, rely on orthogonal functional readouts and validated reagents to avoid false positives (RT‑PCR Ct ≈36; <1 copy/50 ARPE‑19 cells; only CST D4D8T/15101 passed specificity; use long exposures and IP as needed) (kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 7-8, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 3-4, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 1-2, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 2-3).

Stimulation paradigms. AMD‑relevant stimuli include Aβ oligomers (AβOs; prepare from HFIP‑treated Aβ1‑40), which prime/assemble NLRP3 in RPE in vivo via P2RX7; and previously used RPE conditions (e.g., LPS ± ATP, IL‑1α ± 7‑KC, TNFα, IL‑17α, Pam3CSK4, oxLDL) for cross‑study comparability, recognizing weak/absent NLRP3 induction in RPE (narendran2021nucleosidereversetranscriptase pages 6-7, narendran2021nucleosidereversetranscriptase pages 1-3, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 7-8, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 6-7).

Assay endpoints and methods. For “assembly,” quantify ASC specks by immunofluorescence as a hallmark of inflammasome polymerization, using immune‑cell positive controls in parallel (narendran2021nucleosidereversetranscriptase pages 1-3, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 2-3). For caspase‑1, detect p20 cleavage by immunoblot and/or use enzymatic activity assays; for secretion, measure mature IL‑1β/IL‑18 by ELISA from cell‑free supernatants; include GSDMD cleavage and LDH as pyroptosis readouts and consider EV‑associated IL‑1β quantification when relevant (sekar2023impairinggasdermindmediated pages 19-21, zhao2025theroleof pages 5-7, zheng2023themechanismsof pages 9-10, sekar2023impairinggasdermindmediated pages 1-2).

Small‑molecule interventions. Test upstream P2RX7/NLRP3 blockers with AMD relevance: NRTIs (e.g., 3TC, AZT) and Kamuvudines (K‑8, K‑9) that inhibit AβO‑induced RPE degeneration in vivo, applying them during priming/activation and quantifying reductions in ASC specks, caspase‑1 cleavage, and IL‑1β/IL‑18 release (narendran2021nucleosidereversetranscriptase pages 1-3, narendran2021nucleosidereversetranscriptase pages 6-7). Additional tool compounds include vinpocetine (suppresses Aβ‑induced NF‑κB/NLRP3 and cytokines in RPE), NLRP3 inhibitors (MCC950, glyburide), caspase‑1 inhibitors (Ac‑YVAD derivatives), ROS scavenger NAC, GSDMD inhibitor necrosulfonamide, and EV inhibitor GW4869, with explicit caution about off‑target liabilities and compensatory pathways; implement dose‑response with immune‑cell benchmarks to confirm on‑target effects (kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 10-11, zheng2023themechanismsof pages 9-10, sekar2023impairinggasdermindmediated pages 19-21).

A primary RPE inflammasome workflow should therefore pair AMD‑relevant stimuli (e.g., AβOs) with orthogonal functional readouts, pharmacologic/gene controls, and immune‑cell benchmarks, acknowledging that endogenous RPE NLRP3 may be minimal and necessitates stringent validation to interpret low‑amplitude signals (narendran2021nucleosidereversetranscriptase pages 1-3, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 7-8, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 2-3).

Concise answer: Primary RPE assays should use serum‑starved hfRPE/hiPSC‑RPE, AMD‑relevant stimuli (AβOs; or LPS‑based paradigms for comparability), and orthogonal readouts (ASC specks, caspase‑1 p20/activity, IL‑1β/IL‑18 ELISA, GSDMD/LDH), with THP‑1/RAW positives and Nlrp3−/−/NLRP3‑null RPE negatives to ensure specificity (kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 7-8, narendran2021nucleosidereversetranscriptase pages 1-3, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 2-3, sekar2023impairinggasdermindmediated pages 19-21). Consider NRTIs/Kamuvudines (P2RX7–NLRP3 blockade), vinpocetine, MCC950/glyburide, caspase‑1 inhibitors, NAC, necrosulfonamide, and GW4869 in dose–response formats, while recognizing off‑target risks and the low endogenous NLRP3 in RPE (narendran2021nucleosidereversetranscriptase pages 6-7, narendran2021nucleosidereversetranscriptase pages 1-3, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 10-11, zheng2023themechanismsof pages 9-10, sekar2023impairinggasdermindmediated pages 19-21).
