Query: Current methods for differentiating human induced pluripotent stem cells into functional RPE monolayers or 3D retinal organoids for dry AMD modeling, including protocols, phenotypic validation, scalability, and assay reproducibility considerations

Current protocols employ either direct adherent differentiation methods or three‐dimensional retinal organoid approaches to yield functional RPE cells for dry AMD modeling. Direct adherent differentiation involves exposing hiPSCs to a defined cocktail of growth factors and pathway modulators (IGF, FGF‐2, TGF‐β and WNT modulators) during the first 14 days to drive RPE identity, followed by selection of hexagonal, cobblestone‐like cells that are dissociated, replated, and matured (typically analyzed at passage 2 after 28 days) (mesch2021…vitromodel pages 47-52, mesch2021…vitromodela pages 47-52). In contrast, 3D retinal organoid protocols initiate with embryoid body formation, transition to neural retinal induction in N2 medium, and yield retinal fields that are detached by approximately day 24; subsequent self‐assembly leads to RPE organoids that can be maintained for extended periods (up to 300–360 days) to recapitulate age‐related phenotypes, including drusen‐like deposits (mesch2021agedhumanipscrpe pages 1-4, mesch2021agedhumanipscrpe pages 9-12, mesch2021…vitromodel pages 74-77). Phenotypic characterization employs morphological assessment (hexagonality and pigmentation), immunohistochemistry for RPE markers (RPE65, LRAT, BEST1, MITF, PMEL, Ezrin, and ZO‐1), ultrastructural analyses (TEM for microvilli, basal infoldings, Bruch’s membrane components), and functional assays such as phagocytosis and TEER/diffusion measurements (mesch2021…vitromodel pages 47-52, mesch2021…vitromodel pages 74-77, mesch2021…vitromodel pages 43-47, mesch2021…vitromodela pages 43-47).  

Scalability is addressed by using small-format suspension cultures (e.g., 96-well V-bottom plates) for retinal organoids and standardized media and seeding protocols for adherent RPE, although manual steps (such as selection or microdissection) can limit throughput (cheng2023humanretinalorganoids pages 3-4, mesch2021…vitromodel pages 20-24, andreazzoli2021induciblepluripotentstem pages 9-10). Reproducibility is enhanced by employing consistent culture conditions, defined differentiation timelines, normalization of gene expression to GAPDH and RPS9, and statistically rigorous analysis frameworks (two-sided t-tests, ANOVA with Bonferroni corrections), with hiPSCs from the same donor minimizing inter-donor variation (mesch2021…vitromodel pages 47-52, mesch2021…vitromodela pages 74-77, tsikandelova2024investigationintothe pages 75-77, mesch2021agedhumanipscrpe pages 15-17).  

Both adherent and 3D-derived hiPSC–RPE models offer complementary advantages: the adherent method is faster and simpler, while organoid-derived RPE provides advanced maturation and AMD-related phenotypes. These approaches enable nuanced studies of AMD pathobiology and facilitate early drug development.
