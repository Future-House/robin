Query: Characterization of small molecules targeting tight junction proteins (e.g., claudin family, occludin, ZO-1) to enhance RPE monolayer barrier integrity in high-content imaging and TEER assays, including evidence of direct binding or expression modulation, functional efficacy in AMD models, and preliminary safety profiles

Below are 15 distinct small-molecule–based mechanistic hypotheses and associated cell-culture assay strategies for enhancing RPE tight junction integrity (readouts: high-content imaging (HCI), TEER, binding/engagement assays, AMD stress models, cytotoxicity). Each idea ends with a note on preliminary safety profiling.

1. Allosteric Claudin-3 Stabilizer  
Mechanism: Small molecule A binds the first extracellular loop (ECL1) of claudin-3, locking strands into a high-order assembly.  
Assays: SPR/ITC for direct binding; CETSA in ARPE-19; HCI for claudin-3 strand continuity; TEER gain.  
AMD model: A2E + blue-light photooxidative stress—measure barrier collapse rescue.  
Safety: LDH release, caspase-3 activity at 0.1–10 μM.

2. PTP1B Inhibitor to Sustain ZO-1 Tyrosine Phosphorylation  
Mechanism: Molecule B inhibits PTP1B phosphatase that dephosphorylates ZO-1 Tyr-398, promoting scaffold stability.  
Assays: Western blot/phospho-ZO-1 antibody; PLA ZO-1–actin proximity; TEER improvement.  
AMD model: Complement C5a treatment—test barrier protection.  
Safety: MTT viability, off-target PTP profiling.

3. Occludin PDZ-Motif Modulator  
Mechanism: Compound C binds to occludin’s C-terminal PDZ-binding motif, strengthening ZO-1 recruitment.  
Assays: Fluorescence polarization with synthetic PDZ peptide; co-IP occludin–ZO-1; HCI junction continuity; TEER.  
AMD model: H2O2 oxidative injury.  
Safety: MIC cytotoxicity, HepG2 cross-screen.

4. BET Bromodomain Inhibitor to Upregulate CLDN5  
Mechanism: Inhibitor D selectively displaces BRD4 from the CLDN5 promoter, boosting transcription.  
Assays: ChIP-qPCR for BRD4 occupancy; qRT-PCR/Western for claudin-5; HCI/TEER.  
AMD model: Drusen-mimetic lipoprotein overload.  
Safety: Cell-cycle analysis, Annexin V.

5. SENP1 Inhibitor to Enhance ZO-1 SUMOylation  
Mechanism: Molecule E blocks SENP1, elevating SUMOylated ZO-1 and reducing its proteasomal degradation.  
Assays: SUMO1 pull-down Western; junctional ZO-1 intensity by HCI; TEER.  
AMD model: TNF-α–induced barrier leak.  
Safety: Proteasome activity, global SUMO profiling.

6. RhoA GTPase Agonist for Actin-Driven TJ Assembly  
Mechanism: Small molecule F allosterically activates RhoA, promoting actomyosin contractility that tightens junctions.  
Assays: G-LISA for RhoA-GTP; phalloidin staining (HCI); TEER; junctional continuity.  
AMD model: VEGF-induced permeability.  
Safety: Phospho-MLC off-target check, MTT.

7. S1P1 Receptor Agonist to Boost Occludin Expression  
Mechanism: Compound G agonizes S1P1, triggering downstream ERK/AKT pathways that upregulate occludin.  
Assays: qRT-PCR/Western; immunofluorescence HCI; TEER.  
AMD model: A2E + blue-light.  
Safety: ERK-selectivity profiling, LDH release.

8. Cholesterol-Mimetic Lipid Raft Stabilizer  
Mechanism: Cyclodextrin derivative H inserts into the membrane, enriching cholesterol at tight junction microdomains and clustering claudins.  
Assays: FRAP of raft markers; HCI co-cluster analysis; TEER.  
AMD model: Oxidized LDL challenge.  
Safety: Membrane fluidity (Laurdan), viability.

9. MMP-9 Active-Site Inhibitor to Prevent Occludin Cleavage  
Mechanism: Compound I selectively blocks MMP-9 secreted by stressed RPE, sparing occludin from proteolysis.  
Assays: Gelatin zymography; occludin fragment Western; HCI; TEER.  
AMD model: VEGF + MMP-9 induction.  
Safety: MMP selectivity panel; calcein AM.

10. HDAC8 Inhibitor to Induce CLDN19  
Mechanism: Inhibitor J reduces HDAC8 at the CLDN19 locus, enhancing histone acetylation and claudin-19 expression.  
Assays: ChIP-acetyl-H3; qRT-PCR/Western; HCI; TEER.  
AMD model: Lipofuscin precursor (A2E) stress.  
Safety: Global acetyl-proteome, apoptosis markers.

11. ZO-1 PDZ1 Domain Binder  
Mechanism: Small molecule K engages the PDZ1 pocket of ZO-1, promoting multimerization and junctional scaffolding.  
Assays: ITC for direct interaction; fluorescence microscopy—ZO-1 puncta count; TEER.  
AMD model: C5a-mediated junctional disassembly.  
Safety: PDZ-domain selectivity, Annexin V.

12. AKAP-PKA Targeting Agent to Phosphorylate Occludin  
Mechanism: Compound L enhances AKAP anchoring of PKA at the membrane, increasing occludin Ser338 phosphorylation.  
Assays: Proximity ligation AKAP-PKA; phospho-occludin Western; HCI; TEER.  
AMD model: Oxidative insult.  
Safety: cAMP off-target screen, cell viability.

13. 3′UTR Binder to Stabilize CLDN1 mRNA  
Mechanism: Molecule M binds a G-quadruplex in CLDN1 3′UTR, preventing miRNA-mediated decay and boosting translation.  
Assays: RNA pulldown; luciferase-UTR reporter; qRT-PCR/protein; TEER.  
AMD model: Inflammatory cytokines.  
Safety: Transcriptome microarray, cytotoxicity.

14. Mitochondria-Targeted Antioxidant for Indirect TJ Preservation  
Mechanism: Lipophilic antioxidant N concentrates in mitochondria of RPE, lowers ROS, and secondarily prevents junctional protein oxidation.  
Assays: MitoSOX; HCI for claudin/occludin oxidation markers; TEER.  
AMD model: Photo-oxidation + A2E.  
Safety: Mitochondrial membrane potential (JC-1), proliferation.

15. miR-155 Antagonist Mimetic to Elevate ZO-1  
Mechanism: Small molecule O disrupts miR-155-AGO2 interaction, de-repressing ZO-1 translation.  
Assays: AGO2 RIP-qPCR; miR reporter assay; ZO-1 by Western/HCI; TEER.  
AMD model: Cytokine-induced miR-155 up-regulation.  
Safety: miR-off-target panel, cell viability.

Each proposal can be triaged through a tiered screening cascade: direct binding/engagement → junctional localization and expression → functional TEER/HCI readouts under AMD-relevant stress → preliminary cytotoxicity and selectivity profiling.
