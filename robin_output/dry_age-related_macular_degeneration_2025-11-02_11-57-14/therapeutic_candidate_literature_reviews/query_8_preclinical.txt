Query: Preclinical evidence for integrin or adhesion molecule modulators improving RPE barrier integrity in TEER and paracellular flux assays, with mechanistic analyses of focal adhesion complex regulation, efficacy in RPE-specific AMD models, and ocular bioavailability and safety data

1. Small-Molecule FAK Activator (SM-FAK01)  
   • Hypothesis: Low-dose activation of focal adhesion kinase (FAK) via integrin β1 clustering strengthens RPE barrier.  
   • Assays: TEER and FITC-dextran flux in ARPE-19 and primary human RPE under H2O2 stress.  
   • Mechanism: Increased FAK Y397 and paxillin Y118 phosphorylation; enhanced vinculin recruitment at focal adhesions (immunofluorescence, co-IP).  
   • AMD Model: NaIO3-induced RPE atrophy in C57BL/6 mice; fundus imaging and RPE flat-mount ZO-1 staining.  
   • Ocular PK & Safety: Topical ocular drops; LC-MS/MS quantitation in vitreous/retina; ERG and histology for toxicity.

2. α5β1 Integrin Agonist Peptide (A5P-Ag)  
   • Hypothesis: Selective activation of α5β1 enhances fibronectin engagement and RPE adhesion.  
   • Assays: TEER, Lucifer Yellow flux in iPSC-derived RPE monolayers exposed to drusen-like deposits.  
   • Mechanism: Src and FAK dual phosphorylation; increased talin-β1 binding (PLA assay); stabilization of focal adhesion plaques.  
   • AMD Model: iPSC-RPE carrying CFH Y402H variant, challenged with complement-activated serum; barrier rescue readout.  
   • Ocular PK & Safety: Subconjunctival injection; peptide levels in subretinal space; slit lamp exam and inflammatory cytokine panel.

3. αVβ3-Stabilizing Monoclonal Antibody (mAb-V3Stab)  
   • Hypothesis: Preventing αVβ3 internalization sustains focal adhesion integrity and barrier function.  
   • Assays: TEER and paracellular flux in ARPE-19 under TNFα challenge.  
   • Mechanism: Reduced MMP-2 secretion (zymography); enhanced talin and vinculin deposition at adhesion sites (confocal).  
   • AMD Model: Laser-induced CNV in rats; OCT measurement of lesion size and fluorescein angiography.  
   • Ocular PK & Safety: Intravitreal administration; antibody distribution by ELISA; IOP monitoring and retinal histopathology.
