Query: Investigations of mitochondrial-targeted antioxidants or metabolic modulators that preserve RPE barrier integrity in TEER and permeability assays under AMD-like stressors, with mechanistic confirmation of mitochondrial function improvement, efficacy in animal models of geographic atrophy, and pharmacokinetic and toxicity data

1. MitoQ Derivatives for RPE Barrier Preservation  
   • Mechanistic hypothesis: A novel MitoQ analog reduces mitochondrial ROS, stabilizes Δψm and ATP production, and prevents tight-junction protein (ZO-1, occludin) loss under H₂O₂ or A2E+blue-light stress.  
   • Cell-culture assays: TEER and FITC-dextran permeability, MitoSOX staining, JC-1 membrane potential, Seahorse OCR/ECAR.  
   • Animal studies: NaIO₃-induced geographic atrophy in C57BL/6 mice—fundus autofluorescence, OCT lesion volume.  
   • PK/toxicity: LC-MS/MS quantification in plasma, retina/choroid; ocular histopathology, liver/kidney panels.

2. SkQ1 Combined with SIRT3 Activation  
   • Hypothesis: Mitochondrial–targeted plastoquinone (SkQ1) plus a SIRT3 allosteric activator synergize to boost antioxidant defenses and deacetylate mitochondrial enzymes, preserving RPE barrier under lipofuscin accumulation.  
   • Assays: TEER, FITC-Dextran, NAD⁺/NADH ratio, SOD2 acetylation Western blots, respirometry.  
   • In vivo: Cfh⁻/⁻ mice with age-related complement dysregulation; GA lesion scoring.  
   • PK/Tox: Ocular microdialysis, cytokine panels, body-weight and organ-weight toxicity.

3. PGC-1α Overexpression via AAV2  
   • Hypothesis: AAV2-mediated PGC-1α upregulation enhances mitochondrial biogenesis, preserves barrier integrity under chronic low-grade inflammation (TNFα challenge).  
   • Assays: TEER, paracellular flux, mtDNA copy number, citrate synthase activity, TOM20 immunofluorescence.  
   • Animal model: Subretinal TNFα-injection GEAP mice, GA lesion progression.  
   • PK/Tox: AAV biodistribution, anti-AAV neutralizing antibodies, hepatic toxicity.

4. SS-31 (Elamipretide) in Combination with Catalase Overexpression  
   • Hypothesis: SS-31 stabilizes cardiolipin and, together with adenoviral catalase targeted to mitochondria, reduces H₂O₂-mediated barrier breakdown.  
   • Assays: TEER, H₂O₂ permeability, mitochondrial catalase activity, MitoPY1 ROS probe, ATP levels.  
   • In vivo: NaIO₃ rat model of dry AMD—ERG, fundus imaging.  
   • PK/Tox: Ocular half-life by ELISA, systemic cytokines, histology of off-target tissues.

5. NAD⁺ Precursor NMN Analog with PARP1 Inhibitor

   • Animal testing: Abca4⁻/⁻ mice under chronic light stress; GA progression by OCT.  
   • PK/Tox: Plasma/ocular NMN analog levels, complete blood count, liver enzymes.

   • Model: Cfh⁻/⁻ mice challenged with A2E/blue light; GA lesion quantification.  
   • PK/Tox: Retina bioavailability, motor function tests, histopathology of major organs.
