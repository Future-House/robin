Query: Survey mechanistic insights into chronic sterile inflammation in dry AMD, mapping the crosstalk between RPE cells and innate immune cells, cytokine profiles such as IL-1β, IL-18, TNFα, microglial activation, and develop cell co-culture or conditioned media assays to study inflammatory mediator release and RPE barrier disruption

Chronic sterile inflammation in dry AMD is initiated by RPE-intrinsic stress (mitochondrial loss/mtDNA damage, ROS, impaired autophagic flux, senescence/SASP) that licenses cytosolic DNA sensing (cGAS–STING, ZBP1) and inflammasome activation, while releasing mtDNA-bearing extracellular vesicles that directly activate microglia/macrophages to a proinflammatory state (dabouz2024neuroimmuneinteractionsin pages 34-38). Downstream, two-signal NLRP3 activation integrates priming (NF-κB) with triggers including ROS/TXNIP, lysosomal membrane permeabilization from lipofuscin/A2E, ATP–P2X7-driven ion flux, amyloid-β, and complement fragments (C3a/C5a/C1q), culminating in caspase‑1–dependent maturation of IL‑1β/IL‑18 and pyroptosis; autophagy restrains this axis (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14, wong2022exploringthepathogenesis pages 13-15). RPE and infiltrating MPs produce IL‑1β; IL‑1α primes NLRP3 and can switch death from apoptosis to pyroptosis; IL‑18 shows context‑dependent roles; TNFα is induced with hypoxia/oxidative stress and regulates VEGF/MMP programs and chemokines (MCP‑1/IL‑8) via NF‑κB/MAPK (wooff2019il1familymembers pages 3-4, wooff2019il1familymembers pages 2-3, tan2020theroleof pages 1-2, kaur2023inflammationandretinal pages 1-2). Aging disrupts immune privilege (loss of CX3CL1/CD200 restraint, CCL2 upregulation), enabling persistent subretinal microglia/macrophage accumulation that both supports clearance and drives RPE dysfunction; activated microglia disorganize the RPE monolayer and potentiate CNV in vivo (dabouz2024neuroimmuneinteractionsin pages 48-51, ma2009microgliainthe pages 1-2, ma2009microgliainthe pages 10-11). The RPE shifts from immunosuppressive to proinflammatory, increasing IL‑1α/IL‑1β/IL‑6/IL‑8/TNF‑α and expressing NLRs with barrier breakdown preceding late disease (wong2022exploringthepathogenesis pages 6-7).

Experimental assays:
- Direct-contact versus transwell RPE–monocyte/microglia co-cultures to quantify contact-amplified CCL2/IL‑8 and iNOS; add thrombin to model BRB breach (ELISA/qPCR) (wong2022exploringthepathogenesis pages 12-13).  
- RPE-conditioned media after TLR3/4, ATP–P2X7, A2E photo-oxidation, blue light, or POS phagocytosis; apply to microglia to measure IL‑8, TNFα, COX‑2, chemotaxis and neurotoxicity; time-course acute/chronic stimulation (klettner2021retinalpigmentepithelium pages 6-8, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14, dabouz2024neuroimmuneinteractionsin pages 45-48).  
- NF‑κB/ERK/PI3K pathway dissection in hRPE (p65 nuclear translocation, EMSA; selective inhibitors) with readouts MCP‑1/IL‑8; co-culture with monocytes ± IL‑1β/TNFα (dacosta2015investigationsofthe pages 131-136).  
- Inflammasome readouts in RPE/MPs after drusen components/Aβ/complement: caspase‑1 activity, IL‑1β/IL‑18, ASC specks, cathepsin leakage, K+ efflux/Ca2+ influx (wong2022exploringthepathogenesis pages 13-15, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14).  
- RPE EV–microglia transfer assays: isolate EVs after mitochondrial stress, quantify mtDNA cargo, assess microglial ZBP1 activation and proinflammatory gene induction (dabouz2024neuroimmuneinteractionsin pages 34-38).  
- Barrier integrity: TEER and FITC–dextran permeability across differentiated RPE; ZO‑1/occludin immunostaining after H2O2/blue light/RAGE ligands; VEGF/MMP outputs ± NF‑κB blockade (dacosta2015investigationsofthe pages 67-72, dabouz2024neuroimmuneinteractionsin pages 45-48).  
- Ex vivo porcine neuroretina–RPE co-culture to confirm multicellular cytokine amplification (IL‑1β/IL‑6/IL‑8) and cell death (fietz2023cocultivationofprimary pages 1-2).

Dry AMD inflammation arises from RPE mitochondrial/lysosomal stress that activates cGAS–STING and NLRP3, producing IL‑1 family cytokines and exporting mtDNA to license microglial activation (dabouz2024neuroimmuneinteractionsin pages 34-38, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14). Microglia/macrophages reciprocally disrupt RPE architecture and amplify chemokine/VEGF programs; IL‑1β, IL‑18, and TNFα are central outputs (ma2009microgliainthe pages 1-2, wooff2019il1familymembers pages 3-4, tan2020theroleof pages 1-2). The proposed co-culture/conditioned-media and barrier assays provide tractable, mechanistically anchored platforms to quantify mediator release, inflammasome engagement, and RPE barrier failure (wong2022exploringthepathogenesis pages 12-13, klettner2021retinalpigmentepithelium pages 6-8, dacosta2015investigationsofthe pages 67-72).
