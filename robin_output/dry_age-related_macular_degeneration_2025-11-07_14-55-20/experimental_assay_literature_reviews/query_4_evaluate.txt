Query: Evaluate the biochemical basis of oxidative stress-induced damage in RPE cells in dry AMD, focusing on sources of reactive oxygen species, mitochondrial dysfunction, antioxidant defense mechanisms, and in vitro assays measuring ROS generation, mitochondrial membrane potential, and lipid peroxidation in relevant cell culture models

Biochemical sources of ROS in RPE include mitochondrial electron transport (dominant intracellular source; complexes I and III > II/IV), phagocytosis-coupled H2O2 generation via NADPH oxidase, peroxisomal β‑oxidation of outer segment lipids, and photo-oxidation driven by lipofuscin/A2E under high oxygen and light exposure; exogenous amplifiers include smoking, UV, and high‑fat diet that enhance lipid peroxidation burden in the macula (hernandez2025roleofoxidative pages 4-6, toma2021oxidativeandnitrosative pages 4-5, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 1-2, kushwah2023oxidativestressand pages 7-8, hernandez2025roleofoxidative pages 2-4). Mitochondrial DNA is especially vulnerable; accumulated mtDNA lesions and ETC impairment promote a feed‑forward “mitochondrial vicious cycle” of ROS overproduction, with aging RPE showing fewer/smaller mitochondria, cristae loss, decreased Δψm, increased proton leak, and impaired mitophagy that further elevates ROS and triggers SASP/inflammatory signaling (kaarniranta2019roleofmitochondrial pages 1-3, bilbaomalave2021mitochondrialdysfunctionand pages 2-4, toma2021oxidativeandnitrosative pages 4-5). Oxidative injury activates inflammasome/NF‑κB pathways and can be exacerbated by loss of mitochondrial SOD2 in RPE, whereas NADPH oxidase contributes additional enzymatic ROS in retinal vascular/immune compartments linked to AMD phenotypes (terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 2-4, toma2021oxidativeandnitrosative pages 4-5).

Antioxidant defenses in RPE comprise small molecules (glutathione, micronutrients) and an NRF2‑directed enzymatic network (SOD, catalase, peroxidases); with aging and smoking these defenses are blunted (failure to upregulate SOD, impaired NRF2 signaling), permitting ROS accumulation and complement‑lipid peroxidation interactions (e.g., CFH–MDA) that potentiate inflammation (chen2023oxidativestressin pages 2-3, nebbioso2022oxidativestressimplication pages 3-5, domenech2020therelevanceof pages 9-11, toma2021oxidativeandnitrosative pages 13-15). Dysregulated autophagy/mitophagy further permits retention of damaged mitochondria and lipofuscin, sustaining oxidative damage and RPE dysfunction (nebbioso2022oxidativestressimplication pages 3-5).

In vitro RPE models (primary/iPSC‑RPE, ARPE‑19, D407, hTERT‑RPE1) use oxidants such as H2O2, t‑BHP, NaIO3, paraquat, and lipid peroxidation products (4‑HNE) to quantify endpoints: total/mitochondrial ROS increases, Δψm depolarization with cytochrome c release and reduced ATP/respiration, glutathione redox shifts, and lipid peroxidation adducts (MDA, 4‑HNE); chronic oxidative paradigms also activate MAPK/NF‑κB and NLRP3 as mechanistic readouts (kushwah2023oxidativestressand pages 13-14, toma2021oxidativeandnitrosative pages 4-5). Cigarette smoke–based paradigms robustly increase lipid peroxidation and mitochondrial superoxide, providing complementary validation of assay responsiveness (abokyi2021antioxidativeroleof pages 37-41).

Dry AMD RPE pathology reflects convergent mitochondrial and enzymatic ROS sources, mtDNA-centric vicious cycles, and failure of NRF2/autophagy defenses. Validated culture models quantify ROS, Δψm, and lipid peroxidation to mechanistically link oxidant load to mitochondrial failure, inflammation, and RPE degeneration (chen2023oxidativestressin pages 1-2, bilbaomalave2021mitochondrialdysfunctionand pages 2-4, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 2-3, toma2021oxidativeandnitrosative pages 4-5).
