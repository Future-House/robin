Query: Investigate comprehensive mechanistic studies on the role of local complement system dysregulation in the subretinal space during dry AMD pathogenesis, detailing complement factor expression in RPE, drusen biogenesis, MAC pore formation, and potential in vitro assays to quantify complement deposition, cell lysis, and cytokine release in RPE monolayer cultures

Mechanistic studies demonstrate that human RPE constitutively express and secrete a complement repertoire (C3, C5, CFB, CFH, CFI, CD46, CD59, CLU, VTN), with CFH/CFI released apically/basolaterally and functionally converting C3b to iC3b in vitro; expression is inducible by IFN-γ, A2E, and hypoxia, and CFH knockdown elevates C3 and C3/C3b in supernatants, while iPSC-RPE with AMD-risk genotypes show increased CFI/CFH/FHL-1 and reduced C3 (bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 16-17, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 17-19). Oxidative and toxic exposures (H2O2, cigarette smoke) increase surface C3 and reduce CD55/CD59, and CFH deficiency drives IL-6/IL-8, linking complement dysregulation to inflammatory outputs (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 14-15). Surface-restricted amplification is further promoted by ARMS2-mediated properdin recruitment and impaired FH402H regulation under oxidative damage (lin2018theroleof pages 67-70).

Drusen biogenesis is RPE-driven: complement-competent human serum or purified C3a/C5a produces extracellular deposits enriched for APOE, vitronectin, clusterin, and SAP co-localized with C5b-9; C3a/C5a signaling (C3aR1/C5aR1) activates NF-κB, suppresses autophagy and elevates mTOR/Ca2+ signaling, reprogramming RPE metabolism toward lipid/APOE deposition; complement-induced mitochondrial injury promotes APOE aggregation that can nucleate drusen-like material (bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 17-19, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 16-17).

MAC assembles on RPE but is typically sub-lytic due to CD59, which blocks C9; standardized sub-lytic paradigms (10% NHS or 60 μg/mL C9 in 10% C9-depleted serum for 10 min) yield measurable C5b-9 without >5% LDH release and drive mitochondrial fragmentation and a pro-inflammatory secretome (tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 76-80, tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 84-89, tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 106-116). Two rapid defenses—accelerated CD59 recycling and lysosome exocytosis—limit MAC persistence; A2E/cholesterol derail these responses via ASMase/tubulin acetylation, sustaining Ca2+ loads and mitochondrial damage (tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 76-80, tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 71-76, tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 106-116).

Validated in vitro assays include: immunostaining for C5b-9/C3d/APOE; surface C3b opsonization quantification; C3/C3b ELISA in supernatants; functional CFH/CFI C3b→iC3b assays using RPE-conditioned media; LDH release for lysis; MAC counting (e.g., cells with ≥10 MACs/cell); PI-PLC CD59 cleavage–recovery under cycloheximide; live Ca2+ imaging and mitochondrial morphology; β-hexosaminidase release for lysosome exocytosis; and ELISAs for VEGF and cytokines (IL-6, IL-8, MCP-1) after NHS or C3a/C5a exposure (bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 17-19, lin2018theroleof pages 67-70, tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 106-116, tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 84-89, tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 71-76, lin2018theroleof pages 1-4).

Local complement dysregulation in the subretinal/RPE niche arises from RPE-derived complement, oxidative stress–biased regulation, and surface-restricted amplification, culminating in sub-lytic MAC signaling that drives metabolic reprogramming, APOE/lipid deposition, and cytokine release (bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 17-19, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 16-17, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 14-15). A reproducible assay panel combining C5b-9/C3d/APOE imaging, C3b and CFH/CFI function, LDH cytotoxicity, and cytokine ELISAs robustly quantifies complement deposition, lysis potential, and inflammatory outputs in RPE monolayers (bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 17-19, tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 106-116, lin2018theroleof pages 1-4).
