Query: Compile mechanistic evidence on impaired autophagy and mitophagy pathways in RPE cells under chronic oxidative stress conditions; include details on key regulators like mTORC1, AMPK, PINK1/Parkin, LC3-II lipidation, and protocols for fluorescence-based autophagic flux assays in RPE cell culture models

Chronic oxidative stress in RPE triggers autophagy/mitophagy but with persistent burden the system becomes dysfunctional, evidenced by rapid autophagosome induction by A2E (LC3 puncta, LC3‑II, Beclin‑1 within 15 min at 25 µM) followed by delayed fusion (∼6 h) and late-stage defects such as giant LAMP1+ autophagolysosomes and lysosomal membrane destabilization under lipofuscin photooxidation, promoting extracellular deposit formation when degradation fails (drusen/drusenoid) (nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 11-13, pinelli2023autophagyactivationpromoted pages 6-7, nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 5-7). ROS modulate core machinery bidirectionally—Atg4 oxidation sustains LC3-II, whereas oxidation of Atg7/Atg10/Atg3 and upstream pathway shifts can suppress autophagy—and aging/AMD correlate with decreased mitophagy and proteostasis failure in RPE (nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 18-19).

mTORC1 is a principal negative regulator; its hyperactivation drives RPE dedifferentiation/degeneration, whereas rapamycin and A2E suppress Akt/mTOR signaling and trigger autophagy with anti-inflammatory effects (reduced VEGF-A) (nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 26-27, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16, nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 11-13). AMPK antagonizes mTORC1 and activates ULK1, NFE2L2, and PGC‑1α, enhancing autophagy/mitophagy and antioxidant programs; in RPE models AMPK activation increases LC3-II and decreases p62, aligning LC3 lipidation with AMPK↑/mTORC1↓/ULK1↑ signaling (zhou2024roleofampkregulated pages 3-4, zhou2024roleofampkregulated pages 2-3, nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 19-20). The p62/KEAP1–NFE2L2 feedback links selective autophagy to antioxidant gene induction and directly impacts mitophagy via NFE2L2-driven PINK1 expression (nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 19-20, chang2022theinterplayof pages 6-8).

Mitophagy is initiated by PINK1 stabilization on depolarized mitochondria, activation of Parkin via PINK1-dependent ubiquitin phosphorylation, and Parkin-mediated ubiquitination of OMM substrates (VDAC1, Mfn1/2, Miro1, Drp1), which recruit LC3-binding receptors (p62, NDP52, OPTN) and RAB GTPases to assemble autophagosomes; receptor-mediated BNIP3/NIX/FUNDC1 routes and ATM→AMPK→ULK1 signaling integrate ROS with organelle clearance (chang2022theinterplayof pages 6-8, huang2018protectiverolesof pages 9-9). With chronic stress and aging, mitophagy becomes sluggish in RPE, amplifying ROS and inflammasome activation that promote angiogenic remodeling (nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 18-19, nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 27-29).

Fluorescence-based autophagic flux in RPE culture: (1) LC3-based methods specific to RPE are established (“Using LC3 to monitor autophagy flux in the RPE”) and should be paired with LC3 immunoblotting best practices to quantify LC3‑II with/without lysosomal blockade (nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 26-27, huang2018protectiverolesof pages 9-9). (2) Use tandem LC3 reporters (CAG‑mRFP‑EGFP‑MAP1LC3B/mCherry‑GFP‑LC3) to score yellow (autophagosomes) versus red-only (autolysosomes) puncta; validate flux modulation with lysosomal inhibitors (bafilomycin A1/chloroquine) (pinelli2023autophagyactivationpromoted pages 23-24, pinelli2023theessentialrole pages 22-23). (3) In oxidative paradigms, A2E photooxidation provides a defined time course (15 min autophagosomes; ∼6 h fusion) for imaging LC3 puncta and lysosomal colocalization, alongside ROS quantification by DCFDA flow cytometry (nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 11-13, huang2018protectiverolesof pages 9-9). The excerpts flag these tools but do not include stepwise parameters; consult the cited methods papers for detailed protocols (nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 26-27, pinelli2023theessentialrole pages 22-23).

Chronic oxidative stress in RPE disrupts autophagy and mitophagy at multiple tiers: mTORC1 hyperactivity, insufficient AMPK/NFE2L2–PGC‑1α signaling, and compromised lysosomal turnover lead to LC3-II/p62 abnormalities, defective PINK1/Parkin execution, and accumulation of damaged mitochondria (nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 11-13, zhou2024roleofampkregulated pages 3-4, chang2022theinterplayof pages 6-8, pinelli2023autophagyactivationpromoted pages 6-7). Robust flux readouts in RPE rely on LC3 immunoblotting and tandem LC3 reporters with lysosomal inhibition, timed to oxidative stressors such as A2E, with ROS measured by DCFDA (huang2018protectiverolesof pages 9-9, pinelli2023autophagyactivationpromoted pages 23-24, pinelli2023theessentialrole pages 22-23).
