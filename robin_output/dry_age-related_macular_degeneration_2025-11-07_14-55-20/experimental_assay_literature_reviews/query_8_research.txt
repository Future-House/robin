Query: Research the mechanisms of photoreceptor outer segment phagocytosis by RPE cells in healthy and AMD contexts, focusing on MerTK, integrin signaling, opsonins, and lysosomal degradation, and propose quantitative in vitro assays using fluorescently labeled rod outer segments to assess RPE phagocytic capacity under stress conditions

Photoreceptor outer segment (POS) clearance by RPE cells is a cyclic, diurnally regulated process that depends on coordinated receptor‐mediated recognition, internalization, and lysosomal degradation. In healthy RPE, exposure of phosphatidylserine on distal POS tips is recognized by opsonins (e.g., MFG‑E8), which bridge to the apically localized αvβ5 integrin. This engagement activates focal adhesion kinase (FAK) and, in conjunction with receptor tyrosine kinase MerTK activation via ligands such as Gas6 and Protein S, drives Rac1‐mediated actin remodeling and formation of a phagocytic cup necessary for POS uptake (kwon2020phagocytosisbythe pages 2-3, lieffrig2023clearancephagocytosisby pages 11-12, moran2022regulationofthe pages 9-11). Subsequent trafficking to acidic lysosomal compartments enables degradation by proteases such as cathepsin D (olchawa2024theeffectof pages 32-35).

In AMD, evidence suggests that perturbations in mitochondrial function, autophagy, and lysosomal efficacy compromise these fundamental mechanisms. Altered integrin/MerTK signaling and reduced availability of opsonins may further impede efficient phagocytosis, leading to lipofuscin accumulation and increased oxidative damage (stahl2021evaluatingthephagocytosis pages 12-16, lieffrig2023clearancephagocytosisby pages 1-3).

To quantitatively assess RPE phagocytic capacity in vitro, fluorescently labeled rod outer segments (e.g., FITC-tagged) can be employed in cultured RPE monolayers. Uptake can be monitored through flow cytometric analysis and high-resolution confocal microscopy to discriminate between surface-bound and internalized POS, while co-localization with lysosomal markers will enable quantitative assessment of degradation kinetics. Stress conditions (e.g., induced by H2O2) can be applied to simulate AMD-associated oxidative challenges, enabling evaluation of phagocytic efficiency under pathological states (olchawa2024theeffectof pages 29-32, voisin2022proteinsassociatedwith pages 13-13).

Fluorescently labeled rod outer segments offer a robust quantitative measure to assess RPE phagocytic function under defined stress conditions. This assay system facilitates a mechanistic dissection of how AMD-associated alterations in MerTK, integrin signaling, opsonin bridging, and lysosomal degradation compromise outer segment clearance.
