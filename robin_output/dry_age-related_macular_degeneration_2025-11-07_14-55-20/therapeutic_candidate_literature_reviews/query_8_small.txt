Query: Small molecule inhibitors of CCR3 or CX3CR1 that paradoxically enhance RPE phagocytosis in vitro, with evidence for mechanism confirmation, therapeutic window determination, and pharmacodynamic readouts in rodent dry AMD models

None of the available excerpts provide evidence that small molecule inhibitors of CCR3 or CX3CR1 paradoxically enhance RPE phagocytosis in vitro with confirmed mechanisms, a defined therapeutic window, or pharmacodynamic (PD) readouts in rodent dry AMD models. The study in (kuse2017ccr3isassociated pages 6-8) investigates CCR3 antagonists in a photoreceptor-derived cell line, demonstrating ROS reduction and cell protection via CCR3 inhibition, but it explicitly notes that there is no data supporting enhanced RPE phagocytosis or in vivo PD/therapeutic window metrics. Similarly, (nagai2015novelccr3antagonists pages 9-9) reports on the CCR3 antagonist GW766994X in mouse models of choroidal neovascularization (a wet AMD phenotype) and provides PK/PD data in that context, but it does not assess RPE phagocytosis enhancement or evaluate any effects in a dry AMD framework. Finally, (pankova2016innateimmuneresponse pages 156-158) describes a NaIO₃-based rodent model focusing on macrophage polarization and interventions with bindarit to affect RPE atrophy without investigating CCR3 or CX3CR1 inhibitors in the context of RPE phagocytosis. 

The provided context lacks mechanistic confirmation, therapeutic window determination, and PD readouts for small molecule inhibitors that paradoxically enhance RPE phagocytosis in rodent dry AMD models. Further studies are necessary to validate such an approach.
