Query: Cross-talk between autophagy, unfolded protein response, and inflammasome activation in RPE cells during dry AMD progression, with emphasis on how this interplay impairs phagocytosis of photoreceptor outer segments in vitro and in vivo, including molecular regulators such as Beclin1, CHOP, NLRP3, and functional rescue experiments by pathway-specific inhibitors

In RPE, LC3-associated phagocytosis (LAP) and lysosomal autophagy are integral to daily degradation of photoreceptor outer segments (POS). LC3 recruitment to phagosomes depends on Atg5 and Beclin1; Rubicon promotes LAP with diurnal regulation coincident with peak POS degradation, and Atg5 is required for phagosome–lysosome transit. Genetic disruption of LC3B eliminates LAP (while sparing canonical autophagy), yielding lipid dyshomeostasis and accumulation of pro-inflammatory 7-ketocholesterol that further undermines POS clearance and promotes inflammation (markitantova2025retinalpigmentepithelium pages 9-11, liton2023autophagyinthe pages 21-23). Lysosomal acidification/mTORC1 coupling (V-ATPase; CRYBA1/βA3/A1-crystallin) is a key control point for phagolysosomal digestion of POS (kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 10-11, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3, markitantova2025retinalpigmentepithelium pages 9-11).

ER stress/UPR intersects this axis. Chronic oxidative stress with impaired autophagy coincides with UPR activation in AMD; suppressing ER stress–related autophagy attenuates retinal light injury, and POS-related stress (high glucose + oxidized POS) shifts LC3, GRP78, and caspase-8, indicating ER stress–autophagy coupling during compromised POS processing. Direct CHOP data are not provided in these excerpts (kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 11-12, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 5-8, markitantova2025retinalpigmentepithelium pages 9-11).

Autophagy restrains inflammasome-dependent IL-1 family secretion, whereas autophagy inhibition activates inflammasomes. NLRP3 is present in RPE and activated in AMD; Aβ drives NLRP3/caspase-1 and IL-1β/IL-18 in vivo, linking proteostasis/lysosomal stress to inflammatory amplification that further impairs POS renewal (kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 11-12, liton2023autophagyinthe pages 23-24, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 5-8, wang2023potentialregulatoryroles pages 32-37, wang2023potentialregulatoryroles pages 37-38).

Beclin1 cooperates at mitochondria-associated membranes to promote autophagosome formation; its complex nucleates autophagic vesicles, and Beclin1 increases with Aβ exposure in vivo, aligning with BECN1 diurnal expression in retina and RPE autophagic demand (kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 11-12, wang2023potentialregulatoryroles pages 7-9, wang2023potentialregulatoryroles pages 32-37, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3). In vivo RPE-specific loss of core autophagy components (Atg5, Atg7, RB1CC1) leads to AMD-like degeneration, underscoring the requirement of autophagy for POS handling (kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 10-11, wang2023potentialregulatoryroles pages 37-38).

Functional rescue evidence includes enhancement of lysosomal competence, which improves POS degradation and reduces lipofuscin-like autofluorescence in RPE, and protection by suppressing ER stress–related autophagy; autophagy activation can also blunt IL-1β/IL-18–driven RPE swelling. However, these sources do not provide inhibitor-based restoration of POS phagocytosis via Beclin1, CHOP, or NLRP3 targeting (wang2023potentialregulatoryroles pages 32-37, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 11-12, liton2023autophagyinthe pages 23-24, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 5-8).

Autophagy/LAP, UPR, and NLRP3 inflammasomes form a feed-forward network in RPE that degrades lysosomal capacity and POS phagocytosis under oxidative/proteostatic stress. Beclin1- and Atg5-dependent machineries, lysosomal V-ATPase–mTORC1 control, and Aβ/NLRP3-driven inflammation are central nodes. Direct CHOP involvement and inhibitor-based rescue of POS phagocytosis via Beclin1/CHOP/NLRP3 are not demonstrated in these excerpts (markitantova2025retinalpigmentepithelium pages 9-11, liton2023autophagyinthe pages 21-23, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 10-11, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 11-12, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 5-8, wang2023potentialregulatoryroles pages 32-37, wang2023potentialregulatoryroles pages 37-38, liton2023autophagyinthe pages 23-24).
