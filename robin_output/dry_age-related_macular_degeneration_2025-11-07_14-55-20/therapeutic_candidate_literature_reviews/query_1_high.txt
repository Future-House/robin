Query: High-throughput screening studies identifying small molecules that enhance RPE-mediated photoreceptor outer segment phagocytosis in iPSC-derived RPE cells from dry AMD patients, including evidence of target engagement, pathway specificity, and preliminary ADME/safety profiles

High-throughput screens using iPSC-/hESC-derived RPE from dry AMD patients have identified small molecules that enhance photoreceptor outer segment phagocytosis. Notably, ramoplanin (RM) was confirmed as a potent hit in multiple assays, including confocal microscopy, FACS, SDS‐PAGE, and SEM imaging, demonstrating dose-dependent increases in both POS internalization and ensheathment (karlUnknownyearsvenschreiterkaterina pages 10-11, schreiter2020ahumanretinal pages 3-5, schreiter2020ahumanretinal pages 5-7, karlUnknownyearsvenschreiterkaterina pages 1-2). Transcriptomic studies revealed that RM upregulates genes such as ERMN, MFSD2A, and S1PR5, and pharmacologic inhibition experiments showed that blocking S1PR5 (but not MFSD2A) abrogates RM’s effect, providing evidence for target engagement and pathway specificity (karlUnknownyearsvenschreiterkaterina pages 10-11, schreiter2020ahumanretinal pages 9-11). In addition, RM-treated RPE were shown to maintain key epithelial functions including a high transepithelial resistance and a well-preserved basal/apical VEGF secretion ratio, supporting a preliminary in vitro safety profile (schreiter2020ahumanretinal pages 3-5, schreiter2020ahumanretinal pages 5-7, schreiter2020ahumanretinal pages 7-9). Complementary screens in complement-challenged iRPE models identified compounds such as L745,870 and ACA that also restore phagocytosis while modulating NF-κB and autophagy pathways, although these studies did not provide formal ADME or systemic toxicology data (sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 10-11, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 13-14).

Ramoplanin and related compounds enhance RPE phagocytosis by engaging specific pathways and preserving RPE function. However, detailed pharmacokinetic and comprehensive safety profiles remain to be established.
