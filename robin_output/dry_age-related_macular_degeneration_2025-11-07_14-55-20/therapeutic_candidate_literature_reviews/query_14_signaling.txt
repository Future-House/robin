Query: Signaling pathways downstream of integrin αvβ5 and MerTK that regulate cytoskeletal rearrangements and phagosome formation in RPE, with functional data from dry AMD patient–derived models showing pathway downregulation or mutation, including CRISPR-engineered RPE cell lines and pharmacological modulators to dissect individual pathway nodes

Integrin αvβ5 and MerTK coordinate discrete, yet interlinked, signaling cascades that modulate the cytoskeletal dynamics required for photoreceptor outer segment (POS) engulfment by RPE. Engagement of αvβ5 by the PS‐bridging ligand MFG‑E8 initiates an adhesion‐dependent pathway that activates Rac1 and focal adhesion kinase (FAK), thereby driving localized actin polymerization and cup formation (lieffrig2023clearancephagocytosisby pages 12-14, lieffrig2023clearancephagocytosisby pages 11-12). In parallel, MerTK, upon binding Gas6 or Protein S, undergoes ligand‐induced autophosphorylation and recruits Src. Src in turn phosphorylates the Cdc42 guanine nucleotide exchange factor Dbl3, which activates Cdc42. Activated Cdc42 signals via two effectors: one branch involves N‑WASP to promote actin‐driven pseudopod extension, while the other employs MRCKβ to stimulate myosin‑II–mediated contractility, thereby refining cup morphology and facilitating phagosome internalization (zihni2022spatiotemporalcontrolof pages 24-32, zihni2022spatiotemporalcontrolof pages 7-9, zihni2022spatiotemporalcontrolof pages 1-3).

Pharmacological inhibitors such as blebbistatin and MRCKβ inhibitor BDP disrupt actomyosin contractility and attenuate FAK phosphorylation, highlighting the role of force transduction in cup maturation (zihni2022spatiotemporalcontrolof pages 24-32, zihni2025phagocytosisbythe pages 10-11). Moreover, CRISPR‐mediated depletion of MRCKβ produces defects in apical F‑actin organization and POS internalization in vivo (zihni2022spatiotemporalcontrolof pages 24-32). Despite these detailed mechanistic insights, the available excerpts uniformly note that no experimental data from dry AMD patient–derived RPE models or CRISPR‐engineered human RPE cell lines have been reported to link pathway downregulation or mutation to disease phenotypes (zihni2022spatiotemporalcontrolof pages 24-32, zihni2022spatiotemporalcontrolof pages 1-3, zihni2022spatiotemporalcontrolof pages 15-17).

Integrin αvβ5 and MerTK converge to regulate actin remodeling and phagosome formation via distinct Rac1/FAK and Src/Dbl3/Cdc42/MRCKβ modules. Additional studies employing dry AMD patient–derived models and CRISPR‐engineered RPE lines, alongside targeted pharmacological modulation, are needed to directly assess pathway alterations in disease.
