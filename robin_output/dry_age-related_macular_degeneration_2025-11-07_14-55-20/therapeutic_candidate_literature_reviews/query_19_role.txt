Query: Role of epigenetic alterations (DNA methylation patterns, histone modifications, specific microRNA changes) in controlling expression of RPE phagocytosis-related genes such as MERTK, ITGAV, and LAMP1 in dry age-related macular degeneration pathogenesis, including studies in patient-derived cells, organoids, and analysis of postmortem macular tissue epigenomes

Multiple lines of evidence indicate that epigenetic alterations substantially impact RPE cell function in AMD, yet current studies do not provide direct evidence linking DNA methylation, histone modifications, or microRNA changes to specific regulation of phagocytosis-related genes such as MERTK, ITGAV, or LAMP1. Studies using pharmacological inhibitors of DNA methyltransferases and HDACs demonstrate modulation of RPE gene expression and secretory mediator production, highlighting the plausibility that similar epigenetic mechanisms could affect the phagocytic machinery (nashine2020furtherunderstandingof pages 13-14, li2020anupdatedreview pages 4-6). In parallel, altered expression of noncoding RNAs and lncRNAs in patient-derived RPE models has been correlated with dedifferentiation and impaired phagocytosis, implying ceRNA networks may indirectly influence phagocytic function even though MERTK, ITGAV, and LAMP1 have not been specifically interrogated (zhang2023rolesandmechanisms pages 4-6, saptarshi2022investigatingtheepigenetic pages 62-65). Genome-wide methylation profiling from RPE/choroid and blood analyses further supports a role for epigenomic dysregulation in AMD pathogenesis but stops short of mapping changes at loci encoding core phagocytic genes (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 13-14, nashine2020furtherunderstandingof pages 8-10). Similarly, investigations incorporating patient-derived cells, organoids, and postmortem macular tissues have so far not reported locus-specific epigenetic changes at these genes, underscoring a critical gap in the field (li2020anupdatedreview pages 8-9, nashine2020furtherunderstandingof pages 1-3). 

Epigenetic dysregulation likely contributes to broader RPE dysfunction in dry AMD, indirectly affecting phagocytic capacity. Direct epigenetic control of MERTK, ITGAV, and LAMP1 remains to be established in targeted patient-derived systems.
