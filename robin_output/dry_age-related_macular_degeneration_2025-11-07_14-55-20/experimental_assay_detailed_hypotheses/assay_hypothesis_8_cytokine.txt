Assay Overview: This assay models RPE–myeloid paracrine crosstalk that drives retinal parainflammation in dry AMD by collecting conditioned media from stressed RPE (e.g., autophagy inhibition, oxidative stress, or basolateral TLR activation) and applying it to retinal microglia or monocytes in a transwell/Boyden system to quantify cytokine release and chemotaxis. Primary or iPSC-derived RPE are preferred; stress paradigms that impair autophagy or mitochondrial function (wortmannin/chloroquine, rotenone, H2O2) or TLR2/3/4 agonists yield polarized IL-6/IL‑1β/TNFα secretion and barrier loss (TEER) that can be compartmentally harvested (apical/basal) for downstream immune-cell assays (ELISA, qPCR) and chemotaxis (Boyden chambers) (liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 10-11, terheyden2021basolateralactivationwith pages 1-2). Microglial/monocyte activation readouts include IL‑1β/IL‑6 release, caspase‑1 p20 (inflammasome), nitric oxide, and migration toward RPE-conditioned media; optional RPE readouts include junctional proteins and VEGF (liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 4-5, ma2009microgliainthe pages 6-7, fietz2023cocultivationofprimary pages 3-4).

Biomedical Evidence: RPE dysfunction is central to AMD; dry AMD features chronic, low-grade inflammation with activated microglia/macrophages, chemokine-driven recruitment (Ccl2), and elevated IL‑6/IL‑8 clinically and experimentally (lu2018theeffectsof pages 37-41). Inflammasome-linked IL‑1 family signaling (IL‑1α/β, IL‑18) drives chemokine production, microglial recruitment, and photoreceptor loss; IL‑1β neutralization reduces degeneration and chemokines, underscoring assay-relevant endpoints (wooff2019il1familymembers pages 3-4, wooff2019il1familymembers pages 2-3). Direct porcine RPE–retina co-culture rapidly elevates IL‑6, IL‑8, TNFα, IFN‑γ, RANTES, and VEGF, demonstrating that stressed RPE generate precisely the cytokines/chemokines to which microglia respond (fietz2023cocultivationofprimary pages 2-3, fietz2023cocultivationofprimary pages 3-4).

Previous Use: Conditioned-media/transwell paradigms are established: RPE supernatants (after microglial exposure) drive microglial chemotaxis; co-culture induces RPE IL‑1β, TNFα, IL‑6, GM‑CSF, CCL2/5, SDF‑1, MMPs, and VEGF, with readouts by ELISA/RT‑PCR/Western (ma2009microgliainthe pages 6-7, ma2009microgliainthe pages 5-6). Damaged RPE condition macrophages to activate inflammasomes (caspase‑1 p20) and secrete IL‑1β/IL‑6/NO and pro‑angiogenic proteins; conditioned macrophage media enhance choroidal sprouting ex vivo (liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 4-5, liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 1-2). Polarized TLR stimulation provides compartment-specific RPE supernatants rich in IL‑6/IL‑1β/TNFα and reproducible TEER disruption, enabling standardized inputs for immune assays (terheyden2021basolateralactivationwith pages 1-2). RPE secretome assays have been used to pharmacologically modulate inflammatory and VEGF outputs, supporting screening utility (terheyden2021basolateralactivationwith pages 11-11).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths: mechanistically aligned with AMD parainflammation; quantitative, multiplexable readouts (IL‑6/IL‑8/TNFα/IL‑1β, chemotaxis, inflammasome markers); compatible with primary/iPSC RPE and human myeloid cells; scalable for early screening (lu2018theeffectsof pages 37-41, terheyden2021basolateralactivationwith pages 1-2, liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 4-5). Limitations: acute and contact‑free; misses complement, ECM/drusen, and chronic remodeling; some macrophage responses require phagocytic contact; cytokine outputs are stressor- and cell-source–dependent (primary vs immortalized; passage), impacting translatability (liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 9-10, fietz2023cocultivationofprimary pages 8-9, liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 4-5). Use as a first-line functional screen, followed by validation in chronic/co-culture organotypic systems (e.g., porcine retina–RPE) and complement-inclusive models (fietz2023cocultivationofprimary pages 2-3, fietz2023cocultivationofprimary pages 9-11).

This cytokine-release/chemotaxis transwell assay captures a core AMD mechanism—RPE-driven microglial activation and recruitment—with robust, clinically anchored readouts (IL‑6/IL‑8/TNFα/IL‑1β, CCL2-driven migration). It is well supported for early therapeutic screening, provided results are confirmed in more chronic, tissue-complex models.
