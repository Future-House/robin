Overall Evaluation: Strengths and weaknesses of this assay for testing therapeutics for dry age-related macular degeneration.

Assay Overview: The assay quantifies mitophagy in RPE by: (1) flux-sensitive mitochondrial reporters (tandem mCherry–eGFP–COX8A or mKeima-mito) to score delivery of mitochondria to lysosomes (red-only puncta or 561/458 nm ratio), and (2) PINK1/Parkin pathway reporters (e.g., GFP‑Parkin translocation, pS65‑Ub) to monitor ubiquitin‑dependent mitophagy initiation, with lysosomal inhibitors (leupeptin/bafilomycin) to resolve flux versus accumulation (mito-QC also applicable with pH control) (datta2023mitophagyinitiatesretrograde pages 2-4, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 7-9, rosignol2020themitoqcreporter pages 11-13). Primary human RPE or iPSC‑RPE are preferred; ARPE‑19 can be used for throughput with orthogonal validation (Seahorse, ATP, TMRM, MitoSOX; LAMP2 and Rab7 colocalization; TOMM20/COX subunits; RNA‑seq/EMT markers) (datta2023mitophagyinitiatesretrograde pages 9-12, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 9-10, gurubaran2020mitophagyinthe pages 1-3).

Biomedical Evidence: AMD RPE exhibit decreased PINK1, altered PINK1/Parkin signaling, and impaired mitophagy, with downstream mitochondrial dysfunction, EMT reprogramming via NFE2L2→TXNRD1→PI3K–AKT→ZEB1/SNAI1, and death-resistant dysmorphic phenotypes, establishing mitophagy failure as a driver of RPE pathology (datta2023mitophagyinitiatesretrograde pages 9-12, datta2023mitophagyinitiatesretrograde pages 1-2). Primary AMD RPE show ~50% lower PINK1 and diminished mitophagy capacity to depolarization, while receptor-mediated pathways (BNIP3/NIX) can partially compensate, indicating pathway-selective deficits relevant to disease (fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 7-9, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 9-10). Clinically, AMD RPE display reduced mitochondrial number/cristae, mtDNA damage, and lysosomal/autophagic dysfunction with lipofuscin that can stall mitophagic flux, making flux readouts mechanistically central (brooks2023roleofmitophagy pages 5-6, golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 10-11, gurubaran2020mitophagyinthe pages 7-10).

Previous Use: Flux reporters have demonstrated impaired mitophagy with PINK1 loss in RPE and defined RMNS‑to‑EMT signaling, while primary AMD RPE quantified mitophagy with mKeima under FCCP/CoCl2, revealing stressor‑specific and modest dynamic responses (datta2023mitophagyinitiatesretrograde pages 2-4, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 7-9, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 9-10). In related retinal models, mito‑QC enabled pharmacologic induction in tissue, underscoring utility for compound testing with attention to pH and bystander autophagy (rosignol2020themitoqcreporter pages 11-13). Multiple studies emphasize that initiation markers (PINK1/Parkin/LC3) can rise while lysosomal clearance is blocked, necessitating flux‑sensitive reporters plus LAMP2/Rab7 colocalization (gurubaran2020mitophagyinthe pages 1-3, gurubaran2020mitophagyinthe pages 7-10).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths—direct measurement of a causal AMD mechanism in human RPE; flux-capable, live-cell compatible readouts; sensitivity to AMD‑specific deficits; translatable secondary endpoints (mitochondrial function, EMT/inflammation) for pharmacology triage (datta2023mitophagyinitiatesretrograde pages 2-4, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 7-9, datta2023mitophagyinitiatesretrograde pages 9-12). Weaknesses—initiation-only reporters can mislead when lysosomes are compromised; mKeima/mito‑QC require careful pH/flux controls and may show limited dynamic range to mild stress; PINK1/Parkin captures only one mitophagy route; model variability and diurnal/heterogeneity effects demand replication and multiparameter validation (gurubaran2020mitophagyinthe pages 7-10, rosignol2020themitoqcreporter pages 11-13, pinelli2025retinalautophagyfor pages 16-18, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3). Recommended implementation pairs flux reporters with lysosomal competence, mitochondrial function, and EMT/inflammatory readouts under both depolarization and hypoxia‑mimetic paradigms (fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 3-5, datta2023mitophagyinitiatesretrograde pages 9-12, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 7-9).

Mitophagy reporter assays are suitable, mechanistically grounded tools to test dry AMD therapeutics in RPE, provided they measure flux and incorporate lysosomal and mitochondrial function controls (datta2023mitophagyinitiatesretrograde pages 2-4, gurubaran2020mitophagyinthe pages 1-3). They detect AMD‑specific mitophagy deficits and reveal pathway‑selective pharmacology but must be embedded in a multiparameter workflow to avoid false positives from blocked clearance (fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 7-9, gurubaran2020mitophagyinthe pages 7-10).
