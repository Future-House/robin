Assay Overview:  
This assay quantifies apoptotic caspase‐3/7 activity in retinal pigment epithelial (RPE) cells exposed to oxidative or complement insults. It models the intrinsic apoptotic pathway—specifically Bax‐mediated mitochondrial dysfunction that leads to cytochrome c release and caspase activation—which is implicated in dry age‐related macular degeneration (AMD) (kim2021molecularmechanismsof pages 6-7, ruan2021agerelatedmaculardegeneration pages 6-7). The measurement employs luminogenic substrates that emit light upon cleavage by active caspase‑3/7, with RPE cells such as ARPE‐19, primary human RPE, or hiPSC‑derived RPE serving as the biological material (borras2019cfhexertsantioxidant pages 6-8).

Biomedical Evidence:  
Oxidative stress and complement-derived insults are key inducers of mitochondrial dysfunction, leading to Bax‐triggered intrinsic apoptosis and caspase‑3 activation in RPE cells, a pathway central to RPE atrophy and geographic atrophy seen in dry AMD (somasundaran2020retinalpigmentepithelium pages 4-5, somasundaran2020retinalpigmentepithelium pages 8-10). Clinical studies have demonstrated active caspase‑3 in the RPE of AMD patients, reinforcing the assay’s relevance to disease pathogenesis (somasundaran2020retinalpigmentepithelium pages 4-5).

Previous Use:  
This luminogenic caspase‑3/7 assay has been used to monitor apoptosis in RPE cell culture models exposed to oxidative agents, such as NaIO₃ and tert‐butyl hydroperoxide, and to evaluate protective effects of compounds like quercetin, ginsenoside Rg3, and full‐length CFH (chang2021protectiveeffectof pages 12-14, hu2024ginsenosiderg3improved pages 10-11, sreekumar2022oxidativestressand pages 4-5).

Overall Evaluation:  
Strengths of this assay include its quantitative readout and direct measurement of a key apoptotic pathway implicated in dry AMD. Weaknesses include its inability to differentiate apoptosis from other cell death modalities such as necrosis or pyroptosis, necessitating complementary assays to fully validate therapeutic mechanisms (tong2020notallstressors pages 8-9, kim2021molecularmechanismsof pages 14-15).

This assay is valuable for early-stage drug screening in dry AMD due to its focus on the pivotal caspase‐mediated apoptotic pathway. However, incorporating additional markers to distinguish cell death modes will enhance therapeutic evaluation.
