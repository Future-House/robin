Query: Survey mechanistic insights into inflammasome activation by Alu RNA or other endogenous nucleic acid species in RPE cells in dry AMD, detailing involvement of NLRP3, caspase-1, IL-1β release, and relevant cell-culture assay readouts amenable to small molecule intervention screening.

Below are 15 distinct mechanistic hypotheses or therapeutic‐screening strategies—each formulated for human RPE monolayer or polarized cultures challenged with Alu RNA (or related endogenous RNAs/DNAs)—that interrogate steps in NLRP3 inflammasome activation (priming, assembly, caspase-1 activation, IL-1β release) and are compatible with medium-throughput small-molecule screening.

1. RIG-I–NF-κB Priming Blockade  
   • Hypothesis: Cytosolic Alu RNA engages RIG-I, driving NF-κB–dependent transcription of NLRP3 and pro-IL-1β.  
   • Assay Readouts: qRT-PCR for NLRP3/pro-IL-1β transcripts; NF-κB reporter (luciferase); IL-1β ELISA; caspase-1 FLICA.  
   • Intervention Screen: RIG-I antagonists, TBK1/IKKε inhibitors.

2. EndoRNA Uptake via Scavenger Receptors → Lysosomal Rupture  
   • Hypothesis: Alu RNA is endocytosed via CD36/SR-B1, causing lysosomal membrane permeabilization, cathepsin B release and NLRP3 assembly.  
   • Assay Readouts: Lysotracker destabilization imaging; Magic Red cathepsin B activity; ASC speck formation (immunofluorescence); IL-1β ELISA.  
   • Intervention Screen: Cathepsin B inhibitors; SR-B1 blocking antibodies; lysosomal stabilizers.

3. P2X7-Mediated K+ Efflux Trigger  
   • Hypothesis: Alu RNA induces autocrine ATP release to activate P2X7, prompting K+ efflux and NLRP3 oligomerization.  
   • Assay Readouts: Extracellular ATP (luminescence); intracellular K+ (fluorescent dye); caspase-1 cleavage (western blot); IL-1β release.  
   • Intervention Screen: P2X7 antagonists; pannexin-1 channel blockers; ATP scavengers (apyrase).

4. Mitochondrial ROS Amplification  
   • Hypothesis: Alu RNA drives mitochondrial dysfunction and ROS generation, providing the second signal for NLRP3 activation.  
   • Assay Readouts: MitoSOX fluorescence; Δψm (JC-1 assay); ASC oligomerization (cross-linking); IL-18/IL-1β ELISA.  
   • Intervention Screen: Mitochondria-targeted antioxidants (MitoQ); electron transport chain modulators.

5. cGAS-STING–Dependent Type I IFN Cross-Talk  
   • Hypothesis: Nuclear Alu DNA byproducts activate cGAS–STING, upregulating IRF3/IFNβ and priming NLRP3.  
   • Assay Readouts: cGAMP levels (LC-MS); p-STING, p-TBK1 western; IFNβ ELISA; NLRP3 mRNA.  
   • Intervention Screen: cGAS or STING inhibitors; TBK1 inhibitors.

6. TLR9/CpG DNA Engagement  
   • Hypothesis: Endogenous Alu-derived DNA engages endosomal TLR9, contributing to pro-IL-1β transcription.  
   • Assay Readouts: TLR9 reporter assay; NF-κB nuclear translocation (immunofluorescence); pro-IL-1β qPCR.  
   • Intervention Screen: TLR9 antagonistic oligonucleotides; endosomal acidification blockers (chloroquine).

7. Autophagy Dysfunction and Inflammasome Regulation  
   • Hypothesis: Alu RNA impairs RPE autophagy flux, allowing accumulation of damaged mitochondria and NLRP3 activation.  
   • Assay Readouts: LC3-II/ p62 by western; tandem mCherry-GFP-LC3 reporter; ROS and IL-1β.  
   • Intervention Screen: Autophagy inducers (rapamycin analogs); TFEB activators.

8. SIRT1/SIRT3 Modulation of NLRP3  
   • Hypothesis: Alu RNA downregulates SIRT1/SIRT3, elevating acetylation of NF-κB/NLRP3 and boosting inflammasome activation.  
   • Assay Readouts: NLRP3 acetylation (IP-western); SIRT activity assay; IL-1β ELISA.  
   • Intervention Screen: SIRT1/SIRT3 activators (resveratrol analogs); nicotinamide phosphoribosyltransferase (NAMPT) enhancers.

9. TXNIP as a ROS Sensor Linking to NLRP3  
   • Hypothesis: Alu RNA–induced ROS dissociates TXNIP from thioredoxin permitting TXNIP–NLRP3 binding.  
   • Assay Readouts: TXNIP–NLRP3 co-IP; ROS (DCFDA); caspase-1 activity.  
   • Intervention Screen: TXNIP inhibitors; thioredoxin mimetics.

10. HMGB1 Release and Autocrine TLR4 Priming  
    • Hypothesis: Alu RNA causes HMGB1 secretion, which via TLR4 primes NLRP3 and pro-IL-1β expression.  
    • Assay Readouts: HMGB1 ELISA; TLR4 reporter; pro-IL-1β qPCR; IL-1β ELISA.  
    • Intervention Screen: HMGB1 neutralizing antibodies; TLR4 antagonists (TAK-242).

11. Caspase-8 Crosstalk with Inflammasome  
    • Hypothesis: Noncanonical cleavage of IL-1β by caspase-8 augments NLRP3 activation in response to Alu RNA.  
    • Assay Readouts: Cleaved caspase-8 and caspase-1 (western); IL-1β processing; FLICA probes.  
    • Intervention Screen: Caspase-8 selective inhibitors; pan-caspase inhibitors.

12. Ca2+‐Dependent NLRP3 Activation  
    • Hypothesis: Alu RNA elevates cytosolic Ca2+ via IP3R or TRPM2, promoting NLRP3 inflammasome assembly.  
    • Assay Readouts: Fluo-4 Ca2+ imaging; IP3 levels; ASC speck count; IL-18 release.  
    • Intervention Screen: IP3R blockers; TRPM2 antagonists; intracellular Ca2+ chelators.

13. Lipid‐Mediator Modulation of Inflammasome  
    • Hypothesis: Alu RNA alters sphingosine‐1‐phosphate or ceramide balance, affecting NLRP3 activation threshold.  
    • Assay Readouts: Lipidomics (LC‐MS); ASC speck formation; IL-1β/IL-18 readouts.  
    • Intervention Screen: S1P receptor modulators; ceramide synthase inhibitors.

14. Ubiquitination/Deubiquitination Control of NLRP3  
    • Hypothesis: Alu RNA shifts activity of E3 ligases (TRIM31) or DUBs (BRCC3), changing NLRP3 ubiquitination status and activation.  
    • Assay Readouts: NLRP3 ubiquitination assay (IP); proteasome activity; IL-1β ELISA.  
    • Intervention Screen: DUB inhibitors; proteasome modulators; E3 ligase enhancers.

15. Small‐Molecule Direct NLRP3 Inhibitor Profiling  
    • Hypothesis: Targeting NLRP3 NACHT domain prevents ATP‐induced conformational change and inflammasome assembly in Alu RNA–primed RPE.  
    • Assay Readouts: BRET‐based NLRP3 oligomer assay; ASC-GFP speck quantification; caspase-1 FLICA; IL-1β ELISA.  
    • Intervention Screen: MCC950 analogs; covalent NACHT binders; novel allosteric inhibitors.

Each idea pairs a defined mechanistic step with RPE‐adapted readouts (qPCR, ELISA, imaging, activity probes, western blot, reporter assays) and suggests small‐molecule or biologic intervention classes for library screening.
