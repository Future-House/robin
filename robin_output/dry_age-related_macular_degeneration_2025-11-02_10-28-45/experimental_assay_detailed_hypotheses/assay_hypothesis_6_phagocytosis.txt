Overall Evaluation: Strengths and weaknesses of this assay for testing therapeutics for dry age-related macular degeneration.

Assay Overview: This assay models the RPE’s apical recognition, engulfment, and lysosomal degradation of photoreceptor outer segments (POS), a pathway compromised in dry AMD. ARPE-19 or hiPSC-RPE monolayers are fed FITC-labeled POS; uptake is quantified by high-content imaging and orthogonal flow cytometry with POS-only and cell-only gates. Maturation/processing are read out by lysosomal colocalization and acidification metrics (e.g., Rab5→Rab7/LC3 tandem reporters, LysoSensor), and CTSD activity assays; opsonins (MFG-E8, Protein S) standardize binding/engulfment. ARPE-19 require prolonged culture to approach native-like phenotypes; hiPSC-RPE provide human genetics with robust POS handling (when differentiated) (baileysteinitz2021developmentofin pages 96-99, baileysteinitz2021developmentofin pages 87-91, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 37-40, bullock2020theroleof pages 18-22, lynn2018aconvenientprotocol pages 12-15, rastoin2020experimentalmodelsin pages 3-5).

Biomedical Evidence: RPE daily phagocytosis is essential; defects across binding, engulfment, maturation, or lysosomal degradation promote lipofuscin accumulation and AMD pathology. Human AMD donor RPE exhibit reduced phagocytosis; environmental stressors (iron, cigarette smoke) reduce POS uptake, lower active CTSD, delay degradation, and increase autofluorescence, aligning with clinical observations of lipofuscin and GA progression. A2E/lipofuscin directly impairs lysosomal acidification and phagolysosomal degradation, mechanistically linking POS processing defects to AMD (bullock2020theroleof pages 18-22, inana2018rpephagocyticfunction pages 1-2, dalvi2019environmentalstressimpairs pages 1-2, zhang2023improvedlipofuscinmodels pages 20-22).

Previous Use: FITC-POS assays in hiPSC-RPE detected pharmacologic responsiveness: the ROCK inhibitor Y-27632 increased uptake and RPE65; MFG-E8/Protein S further boosted engulfment. Conditioned media and defined RTK ligands/bridging molecules rescued phagocytic deficits in AMD RPE, demonstrating therapeutic modulation. Oxidative stress paradigms revealed impaired uptake and pathway dependencies (FAK/mTORC2), while complementary imaging quantified phagosome maturation, acidification, and lysosomal protease activity (baileysteinitz2021developmentofin pages 87-91, baileysteinitz2021developmentofin pages 96-99, inana2018rpephagocyticfunction pages 1-2, voisin2022proteinsassociatedwith pages 1-2, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 37-40).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths—central disease biology; human-cell context; multiplexed, mechanism-resolved endpoints (uptake, maturation, acidification, CTSD); pharmacologic sensitivity; compatibility with high-content imaging and flow. Limitations—ARPE-19 require long maturation and have weaker barrier properties; hiPSC-RPE show line-to-line variability; opsonin dependence can mask intrinsic recognition defects; fluorescent readouts are confounded by lipofuscin/autofluorescence and residual POS, necessitating spectral/lifetime masking; some reports have small n/variability; AMD is multifactorial, so this assay should be paired with oxidative stress, complement, and lipofuscin models for broader coverage (lynn2018aconvenientprotocol pages 12-15, rastoin2020experimentalmodelsin pages 3-5, baileysteinitz2021developmentofin pages 87-91, zhang2023improvedlipofuscinmodels pages 14-16, zhang2023improvedlipofuscinmodels pages 18-19, lynn2018aconvenientprotocol pages 23-25, dalvi2019environmentalstressimpairs pages 1-2, zhang2023improvedlipofuscinmodels pages 20-22).

This FITC-POS phagocytosis kinetics assay is biologically central, methodologically mature, and drug-responsive, making it suitable for phenotypic screening and MoA studies in dry AMD. Use hiPSC-RPE where possible, standardize opsonization and maturation readouts, and incorporate imaging strategies to manage autofluorescence. Pair with complementary stress and lipofuscin assays to capture broader AMD mechanisms (baileysteinitz2021developmentofin pages 96-99, inana2018rpephagocyticfunction pages 1-2, zhang2023improvedlipofuscinmodels pages 14-16).
