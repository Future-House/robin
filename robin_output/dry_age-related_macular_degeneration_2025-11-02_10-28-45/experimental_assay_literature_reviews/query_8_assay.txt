Query: Assay development strategies for modeling complement-mediated inflammation in dry AMD, integrating RPE-microglia co-culture, pro-inflammatory cytokine profiling, NF-κB reporter systems, high-content imaging of inflammatory phenotypes, characterization of inflammasome activation dynamics and multiplexed imaging of cytokine secretion microenvironments

Assay development for complement-mediated inflammation in dry AMD should integrate cellular co-culture systems with advanced cytokine profiling and signaling assays to faithfully recapitulate the retinal microenvironment. RPE-microglia co-cultures are critical for modeling the in vivo interaction between retinal pigment epithelial cells and immune effectors, simulating the complement activation cascade and immune-mediated stress observed in AMD (boyce2022microglia–neutrophilinteractionsdrive pages 4-5, gao2018evidenceforthe pages 1-2). Pro-inflammatory cytokine profiling, including quantification of IL-6, MCP-1, IL-1β, and IL-18, is essential to define the inflammatory milieu and verify complement pathway activation (ebeling2022inflammasomeactivationin pages 7-9, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 76-79).

Incorporating NF-κB reporter systems—monitoring RelA/p65 activation—enables sensitive detection of NF-κB-driven transcriptional responses that underlie inflammatory signaling in the diseased retina (ridley2025modulationofretinal pages 12-13). High-content imaging of inflammatory phenotypes, paired with multiplexed imaging of cytokine secretion microenvironments, allows for spatial and temporal assessment of cell-specific inflammatory responses. Moreover, characterization of inflammasome activation dynamics via live-cell imaging and complementary biochemical assays provides insight into the progression of pyroptotic and apoptotic pathways in RPE cells (gao2018evidenceforthe pages 1-2, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 76-79).

These strategies together establish a robust framework to model, dissect, and eventually target complement-mediated inflammation in dry AMD. The integrated approach facilitates both mechanistic investigations and therapeutic screening for delaying retinal degeneration.
