Query: Detailed review of RPE autophagy impairment mechanistic pathways in dry AMD, including lysosomal dysfunction, drusen-associated lipofuscin accumulation, key autophagic flux markers, and optimized cell culture assays for measuring autophagic activity

RPE autophagy impairment in dry AMD is driven by lysosomal dysfunction that compromises the degradation of photoreceptor outer segments, leading to accumulation of autofluorescent lipofuscin granules and subsequent drusen formation (falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 1-6, falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 2-4). Lysosomal dysfunction in AMD involves defective lysosome‐phagosome fusion and reduced catalytic activity of key enzymes such as Cathepsin D, which exacerbates oxidative damage and disrupts normal proteostasis (falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 1-6, wang2023potentialregulatoryroles pages 1-3). The resultant accumulation of lipofuscin not only serves as a marker of impaired autophagy but also contributes to inflammation and cellular stress by interfering with lysosomal proton pump function, thereby intensifying the autophagic defect (falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 2-4, si2023theroleof pages 5-7).

Key autophagic flux markers such as the LC3-II/LC3-I ratio and p62/SQSTM1 levels are consistently used to assess the competence of the autophagy machinery, with failure to downregulate p62 under starvation conditions indicative of blocked autophagosome clearance (golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 1-2, golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 5-9, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 3-5). In addition, deficits in essential autophagy components including ATG5 and ATG7 further underscore the breakdown of autophagic flux (kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3).

Optimized cell culture assays employing primary human fetal RPE and ARPE-19 cells use photoreceptor outer segment (POS) pulse-chase protocols, including UV-irradiated POS to mimic oxidative damage, along with lysosomal inhibitors (e.g., E64d, pepstatin A) to measure autophagic flux and lysosomal function via LAMP1 immunostaining and western blot analysis of autophagy markers (falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 13-18, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 1-6, golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 10-11, golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 1-2).

RPE autophagy impairment in dry AMD is characterized by lysosomal dysfunction, lipofuscin accumulation, and defective autophagic flux. Robust in vitro models permit detailed mechanistic studies and evaluation of therapeutic strategies targeting the mTOR/TFEB pathway.
