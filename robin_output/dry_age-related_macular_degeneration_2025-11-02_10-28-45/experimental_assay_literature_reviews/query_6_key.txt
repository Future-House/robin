Query: Key signaling pathways driving RPE apoptosis and senescence in dry AMD, including p53, NF-κB, and MAPK cascades, crosstalk with oxidative stress, and standardized in vitro assays for quantifying apoptotic and senescent cell populations

Oxidative stress is the proximate trigger that engages p53, NF-κB, and MAPK nodes to drive senescence and apoptosis in RPE relevant to dry AMD (terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 13-14, terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 5-7). p53 enforces senescence via the p53→p21Cip1→RB axis downstream of DNA damage signaling (ATM/ATR) and also executes apoptosis; in A2E/blue light models, c-Abl and JNK cooperate with p53 to drive cell death, while pharmacologic p53 inhibition (pifithrin-α) or TP53 blockade suppresses apoptosis (lee2021cellularsenescencein pages 7-8, fietz2023bluelightdamage pages 19-21, fietz2023bluelightdamage pages 1-2). Oxidative stress increases p53 acetylation (K382), limiting MDM2 binding and sustaining caspase-3/7 activation, BAX mobilization, and TUNEL+ death, establishing a mechanistic route from ROS to p53-dependent apoptosis (fietz2023bluelightdamage pages 15-17).

NF-κB is a primary inducer of the SASP under oxidative stress, driving IL-6, IL-8, and CXCL1; its activation can be initiated by oncogenic signals or GATA4 released from ATM/ATR control, and NF-κB inhibition suppresses H2O2-induced SASP and can bypass p53/p21-driven senescence (terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 5-7, lee2021cellularsenescencein pages 7-8). mTOR, Notch, and p38 MAPK further potentiate SASP output; rapamycin suppresses oxidative-stress-induced p16/p21, SA-β-gal and SASP without requiring NRF2, linking nutrient sensing to senescence phenotype (terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 5-7, terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 13-14).

MAPK cascades integrate stress and inflammatory signaling: p38 drives senescence/SASP (including MAPKAPK2/IL-1α coupling), JNK participates in blue light/A2E apoptosis, and upstream kinases (TAK1) modulate senescence–apoptosis balance; oxidative stress–induced BMP4 enforces p53–p21–RB senescence, and DUSP4 restrains p38/p53/NF-κB activation to limit RPE senescence (blasiak2020senescenceinthe pages 5-7, fietz2023bluelightdamage pages 19-21, lee2021cellularsenescencein pages 7-8, liu2025theprotectiverole pages 12-14). Redox crosstalk is reinforced by age-related NRF2 decline and stabilization of NRF2 by p21; antioxidant interventions (e.g., NAC) partially rescue A2E photosensitization, whereas blue light induces mitochondrial fragmentation and telomere damage that amplify DDR/p53 signaling (terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 5-7, terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 13-14, blasiak2020senescenceinthe pages 10-12, fietz2023bluelightdamage pages 1-2).

Standardized in vitro readouts include senescence: SA-β-gal staining; p16INK4a, p21Cip1, p53 expression; γH2AX foci, DNA-SCARS/TIFs; and SASP profiling (IL‑6, IL‑8, CXCL1, VEGF, TNF‑α, MMPs) in ARPE‑19 under H2O2, A2E+blue light, oxidized POS, or cigarette smoke (terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 5-7, terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 13-14, blasiak2020senescenceinthe pages 5-7, blasiak2020senescenceinthe pages 3-4). Apoptosis quantification uses caspase‑3/7 activity assays, TUNEL, free BAX detection, and immunodetection of acetyl‑p53 (K382); caspase‑3 activation is a common endpoint in blue‑light injury models (fietz2023bluelightdamage pages 15-17, fietz2023bluelightdamage pages 19-21).

Oxidative stress engages p53 (DDR→p21 and apoptotic programs), NF‑κB (SASP), and MAPKs (p38/JNK) to determine RPE fate in dry AMD. Nutrient/redox modifiers (mTOR, NRF2) and phosphatases (DUSP4) tune these outputs. Robust in vitro quantification leverages SA‑β‑gal, p16/p21/p53, γH2AX/SASP panels, and apoptosis assays (caspase‑3/7, TUNEL, BAX, acetyl‑p53) in oxidative stress models such as A2E+blue light and H2O2 (terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 5-7, terao2022oxidativestressinducedcellular pages 13-14, blasiak2020senescenceinthe pages 5-7, fietz2023bluelightdamage pages 15-17).
