Drusen-related accumulation: quantify lipid droplets (BODIPY), glycogen, and APOE puncta burden/intensity; immunostain amyloid β/clusterin/complement/VEGFA and compute puncta counts and Pearson co-localization with lysosomes/exosomes; in 3D-oBRB, induce sub-RPE APOE-positive deposits with complement-competent serum to benchmark deposit detection (golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 11-11, golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 2-2, golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 1-2, handa2019asystemsbiology pages 5-6, song2023bioprinted3douter pages 19-23, buchser2014assaydevelopmentguidelinesa pages 15-17).
