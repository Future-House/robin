Query: Methodologies for measuring mitochondrial dynamics and bioenergetic alterations in dry AMD RPE cell culture, covering mitochondrial membrane potential probes, Seahorse assays, live-cell imaging of fission/fusion events, and mechanistic modulators of mitochondrial quality control

The investigation of mitochondrial dynamics and bioenergetic alterations in dry AMD RPE cell culture relies on a combination of complementary methodologies. Mitochondrial membrane potential is assessed using fluorescent probes such as TMRE, which accumulates in mitochondria based on their electrochemical gradient, thereby providing high-resolution insights into mitochondrial functional heterogeneity (tan2022analysisofmitochondrial pages 6-8). Seahorse assays are employed to measure key bioenergetic parameters, including oxygen consumption rate (OCR) and extracellular acidification rate (ECAR), which together evaluate mitochondrial respiration, ATP production, and glycolytic activity (rosales2019lossofpgc1α pages 1-2, rosales2019lossofpgc1α pages 12-13).

In addition, high-speed live-cell imaging using mitochondrial-targeted reporters such as mKeima facilitates real-time visualization of fission and fusion events, allowing for dynamic assessment of mitochondrial network integrity (fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 2-3, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 3-5, tan2022analysisofmitochondrial pages 3-6). Mechanistic modulators of mitochondrial quality control are also incorporated; FCCP is used to induce rapid mitochondrial depolarization and stimulate fission and mitophagy, while CoCl2 mimics hypoxic conditions to trigger receptor-mediated mitophagy. Further, compounds such as metformin, N-acetyl-L-Cysteine, rapamycin, pyrroloquinoline, and nicotinamide mononucleotide are applied to modulate mitochondrial biogenesis, autophagy, and oxidative phosphorylation, thereby elucidating therapeutic targets in AMD (fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 1-2, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 10-10, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 9-10, fisher2022investigatingmitochondrialfission pages 3-5).

These methodologies provide a multifaceted approach to characterizing mitochondrial function in dry AMD RPE models. They collectively reveal critical insights into mitochondrial quality control and bioenergetic integrity, informing potential targeted interventions.
