Query: Biochemical characterization of RPE lysosomal membrane composition and proton pump assembly changes in dry AMD, with structural lipidomics, cryo-EM analysis of v-ATPase complexes, functional pH sensor assays, lipid peroxidation and cholesterol accumulation effects on vesicle acidification, and correlation with drusen component infiltration in patient tissues and in vitro RPE cultures

In dry AMD, RPE lysosomal dysfunction is mechanistically linked to oxidative stress–induced alterations in membrane composition and v‑ATPase assembly. Structural lipidomics can quantify and spatially resolve shifts in phospholipid classes, the accumulation of oxidized lipid species (including A2E and lipid–protein adducts from photoreceptor outer segment oxidation), and changes in fatty acid content that occur during disease progression (markitantova2025retinalpigmentepithelium pages 20-21). Cryo‑EM analysis is optimally suited to visualize lysosomal ultrastructure and the near‑atomic organization of v‑ATPase complexes. This technique can detect alterations such as subunit disassembly, misalignment of membrane‐associated domains, or aberrant interactions with oxidatively modified lipids that may impair proton pump efficiency (barral2022currentmethodsto pages 27-28, markitantova2025retinalpigmentepithelium pages 20-21).

Functional pH sensor assays provide dynamic readouts of lysosomal acidification. These assays indicate that lipid peroxidation and cholesterol accumulation disrupt vesicle acidification by altering membrane lipid microdomains and compromising v‑ATPase assembly or stability (barral2022currentmethodsto pages 8-10, barral2022currentmethodsto pages 27-28). Correlative studies in patient tissues and in vitro RPE cultures further link drusen component infiltration to specific lipidomic signatures and v‑ATPase dysfunction, supporting a model where oxidative lipid modifications drive lysosomal failure in dry AMD (markitantova2025retinalpigmentepithelium pages 24-25, markitantova2025retinalpigmentepithelium pages 20-21).

Oxidative modifications in RPE lysosomal membranes diminish v‑ATPase complex integrity, resulting in impaired vesicle acidification. These multi‑modality approaches establish a clear correlation between drusen lipid infiltration and lysosomal proteolytic failure in dry AMD.
