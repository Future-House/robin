Query: Studies reporting high-throughput screening of diverse small-molecule libraries targeting lysosomal acidification and cathepsin D activity in retinal pigment epithelium cells under dry AMD-like stress conditions, including validation in patient-derived iPSC-RPE and animal geographic atrophy models

No study in the provided corpus reports a high-throughput screen of a diverse small‑molecule library explicitly targeting lysosomal acidification and/or cathepsin D (CTSD) activity in RPE under dry AMD-like stress with subsequent validation in both patient-derived iPSC-RPE and animal GA models. The closest HTS precedent in iPSC-RPE used the LOPAC library with phenotypic endpoints (drusen/APOE and survival under A23187 or anaphylatoxin stress), not lysosomal pH or CTSD activity, and did not include animal GA validation of lysosome-targeted hits (sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 1-2, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 8-9). Multiple studies establish the disease-relevant iPSC-RPE (CFH H/H vs Y/Y), robust lysosome/autophagy readouts (CTSD/CTSL protein and enzymatic activity, TFEB/TFE3 phosphorylation/localization, CLEAR gene expression, LC3/p62), and positive controls (trehalose; TFEB-S467A) that are suitable for HTS assay design and for in vivo follow-up in Akt2 KI, Cryba1 KO, PGC-1α KO, and Sirt5 KO mice, but these were applied in hypothesis-driven experiments rather than library-scale screening (ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 10-11, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 6-6, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 14-15, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 6-8, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 1-2). Assay conditions compatible with medium/high throughput exist, including 96‑well lysosomal pH (LysoSensor Yellow/Blue DND‑160 with ionophore calibration) and CTSD active-site labeling (BODIPY‑pepstatin A), further supporting feasibility of an HTS focused on lysosomal acidification and CTSD rescue in iPSC‑RPE (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 37-40). Importantly, two summaries explicitly note the absence of a published example meeting all requested criteria within these sources (ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 17-18, fisher2022mitochondrialdysfunctionin pages 167-170).

There are no reported HTS studies in the cited literature that specifically target lysosomal acidification/CTSD activity in RPE under dry AMD-like stress and validate hits in both patient iPSC-RPE and animal GA models. Feasibility and assay components are well established (iPSC-RPE CFH models, CTSD/TFEB/CLEAR readouts, trehalose/TFEB controls, Akt2 KI/Cryba1 KO mice), but a single integrated HTS-to-in vivo pipeline has not been documented here (sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 1-2, sharma2021epithelialphenotyperestoring pages 8-9, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 10-11, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 6-6, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 14-15, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 37-40, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 17-18, fisher2022mitochondrialdysfunctionin pages 167-170).
