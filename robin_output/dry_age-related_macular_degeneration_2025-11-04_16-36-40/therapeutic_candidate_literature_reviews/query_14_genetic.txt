Query: Genetic and functional validation of lysosomal acidification modulators as therapeutic targets in dry AMD: CRISPR knockout or overexpression of v-ATPase subunits, cathepsin D, mTOR-TFEB regulators in RPE models, with readouts including lysosomal pH sensors, cathepsin D activity assays, RPE survival, phagocytosis efficiency, and rescue by candidate small molecules

Genetic perturbations. Implement CRISPR/Cas9 loss-of-function and CRISPRa/overexpression in RPE for: (i) v-ATPase subunits (e.g., ATP6VOA1; TFEB activation increases ATP6VOA1 transcripts, supporting their functional linkage to lysosomal acidification), (ii) cathepsin D (CTSD; KO lines in ARPE‑19 are established and show lysosomal dysfunction), and (iii) mTOR–TFEB pathway nodes (Raptor/mTOR, TFEB, TFEB‑CA), with genetic rescue (re‑expression) to confirm on‑target effects (falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 5-7, mesquita2021purificationandcharacterization pages 61-66, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 10-13, falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 2-4). Use LysoIP via TMEM192 tagging (HA/FLAG or endogenous KI) to enrich lysosomes and resolve organelle‑level effects otherwise masked in whole‑cell lysates (mesquita2021purificationandcharacterization pages 61-66, mesquita2021purificationandcharacterization pages 72-74).

Primary readouts. Quantify lysosomal pH using a calibrated ratiometric protocol (LysoSensor Yellow/Blue DND‑160, 330/380 nm excitation; nigericin/monensin pH clamp, MES buffers, 4.0–6.0 calibration curve), and avoid over‑interpreting LysoTracker; use minimal LT exposure or add only before acquisition, and control for pigmentation quenching (ng2024impairedcathepsind pages 5-6, cardoso2018stemcellderivedretinal pages 164-167). Measure CTSD protein processing by immunoblot and proteolytic activity with fluorogenic substrates; CTSD maturation/activity tracks with acidification and is reduced in disease models (ng2024impairedcathepsind pages 5-6, ng2024impairedcathepsind pages 10-12). Monitor autophagy and lysosome biogenesis/TFEB status (LC3, p62, LAMP1; TFEB phosphorylation/localization), and quantify lysosome mass/number (falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 10-13, falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 5-7). Assess POS handling with defined pulse–chase assays (e.g., 4 h, 200 µg/mL POS; Alexa647‑POS plus anti‑rhodopsin to separate bound vs internalized) to derive phagocytosis efficiency and cargo clearance; quantify AFGs/AF as lipofuscin proxies (falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 13-18, ng2024impairedcathepsind pages 5-6, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 10-13). Include lysosomal exocytosis/extracellular deposit formation where relevant and cell health/survival metrics under chronic POS/oxidative stress challenge (cardoso2018stemcellderivedretinal pages 164-167, falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 2-4).

Rescue/controls. Validate pharmacologic rescue of acidification and clearance with mTOR inhibitors (rapamycin, INK128, Drug H) and P2Y12 antagonism (ticagrelor), and test dependence on lysosomal hydrolases using Lalistat‑2 or leupeptin/pepstatin and CTSD KO backgrounds; rapamycin/ticagrelor reduce AFG/AF and restore pH/CTSD maturation in a hydrolase‑dependent manner (falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 10-13, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 13-18, ng2024impairedcathepsind pages 10-12, falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 5-7). Use BafA1 and CQ as positive controls for alkalinization to benchmark assay dynamic range (mesquita2021purificationandcharacterization pages 53-56, cardoso2018stemcellderivedretinal pages 164-167).

This framework couples targeted CRISPR perturbations of v‑ATPase, CTSD, and mTOR–TFEB with organelle‑resolved lysosomal pH, CTSD maturation/activity, POS degradation kinetics, AFG burden, and survival. Orthogonal genetic and pharmacologic rescues establish causality and therapeutic relevance for lysosomal acidification in dry AMD RPE models (ng2024impairedcathepsind pages 5-6, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 10-13).
