Query: Analysis of small-molecule inhibitors or activators affecting endolysosomal trafficking pathways, evaluating their impact on lysosomal pH normalization, cathepsin D activation, and cellular debris degradation in RPE cells derived from dry AMD patients, including mechanistic interrogation of Rab GTPases and ESCRT machinery, endosome-lysosome fusion assays, patient iPSC-RPE validation, and preliminary hit compound physicochemical profiling

Current studies in iPSC‐RPE models have elucidated the importance of Rab GTPase prenylation and mTORC1‐mediated autophagy in maintaining lysosomal function (raeker2024reducedretinalpigment pages 17-18, raeker2024reducedretinalpigment pages 1-2). Small-molecule agents, such as the mTOR inhibitor torin and the prenylation inhibitor compactin, have been employed to modulate these pathways; torin restored autophagic flux and reduced mTORC1 signaling in CHM−/− RPE cells, while compactin served as a chemical probe to assess global Rab prenylation and membrane localization (raeker2024reducedretinalpigment pages 12-14, raeker2024reducedretinalpigment pages 4-5, raeker2024reducedretinalpigment pages 14-16). However, none of the current reports provide explicit data on compounds that directly achieve lysosomal pH normalization, cathepsin D activation, or enhanced debris degradation in RPE cells derived from dry AMD patients (li2025ipscderivedretinalpigment pages 2-4, li2025ipscderivedretinalpigment pages 6-7). Mechanistic dissection has largely focused on Rab12 activity, yet targeted interrogation of ESCRT machinery or endosome–lysosome fusion assays remains unaddressed (raeker2024reducedretinalpigment pages 5-6, raeker2024reducedretinalpigment pages 1-2, raeker2024reducedretinalpigment pages 16-17). Moreover, while iPSC-RPE disease models have been leveraged for drug screening, there is an absence of reported hit compounds with comprehensive ADME or physicochemical profiling validated in a dry AMD context (li2025ipscderivedretinalpigment pages 5-6, li2025ipscderivedretinalpigment pages 7-7, raeker2024reducedretinalpigment pages 16-17).

The current evidence indicates promising targets via Rab-dependent modulation of autophagy but does not provide complete small-molecule validation for lysosomal function restoration in dry AMD iPSC-RPE. Additional studies addressing these mechanistic gaps and compound profiling are needed.
