Query: Evidence on combination screening strategies coupling lysosomal pH measurement with cathepsin D activity assays to identify synergistic or multi-target small molecules capable of reversing RPE lysosomal dysfunction in geographic atrophy models, including optimized dual-readout high-throughput assay design, pilot screening results, synergy quantification methods, hit validation workflows, and translation of leads to in vivo efficacy studies

The available evidence supports a dual‐readout screening approach that couples lysosomal pH measurements with cathepsin D activity assays to identify multi‐target small molecules capable of reversing RPE lysosomal dysfunction in geographic atrophy. Elevated lysosomal pH in RPE models, which impairs proteolytic function, is complemented by quantifiable reductions in cathepsin D activity, making these two endpoints highly relevant for detecting therapeutic rescue (egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 26-32). Optimized high‐throughput assays integrate pH‐sensitive dyes (e.g., Lysotracker DND 99) and fluorogenic substrates for cathepsin D in live-cell platforms, including FRET-based imaging configurations that provide dose- and time-dependent kinetic readouts (egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 32-36, egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 36-41). Pilot studies using interventions such as trehalose, AKT2 inhibitors, and constitutively active TFEB (TFEB-S467A) have demonstrated restoration of lysosomal hydrolase activity and normalization of lysosomal markers in iPSC-derived CFH (H/H) RPE models (ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 10-11, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 6-6). Synergy quantification is performed using robust statistical methods (e.g., One-way ANOVA followed by Tukey’s post-hoc test) across multiple replicates to identify compounds that simultaneously modulate both pH and cathepsin D activity (ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 14-15). Hits are validated through secondary workflows incorporating orthogonal readouts such as CLEAR gene expression, autophagy flux assays, and in vivo efficacy studies in relevant mouse models using established dosing regimens and retinal function outcomes (ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 6-8, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 29-32).

This screening strategy effectively identifies synergistic small molecules that restore lysosomal function in RPE models of geographic atrophy. It offers a comprehensive workflow from dual-readout high-throughput screening to in vivo validation confirming the translational potential of candidate treatments.
