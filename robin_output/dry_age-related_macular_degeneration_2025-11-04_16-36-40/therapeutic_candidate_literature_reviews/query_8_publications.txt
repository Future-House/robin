Query: Publications detailing high-content microscopy or fluorometric assays optimized for lysosomal pH and cathepsin D activity screening in RPE cultures, including assay validation metrics, dynamic range, reproducibility, pilot compound screening results, chromatographable dyes or genetically encoded pH reporters, plate format recommendations, image analysis pipelines, compound library integration, and statistical hit selection thresholds

The publications describe high-content microscopy and fluorometric workflows that use the genetically encoded ratiometric lysosomal pH sensor FIRE-pHLy (mTFP1/mCherry) and fluorogenic probes such as BODIPY FL–Pepstatin A for cathepsin D activity. These studies report that FIRE-pHLy has an appropriate pKa (~4.4) for lysosomal pH sensing and is stably expressed in cell systems that include neuronal and RPE‐like models, with protocols readily adaptable to RPE cultures (chin2022phenotypicscreeningusing pages 1-2, chin2022phenotypicscreeningusing pages 11-11). They detail plate format recommendations—typically 96‑well microplates with defined cell seeding densities and a 6‑hour compound treatment to capture rapid pH modulation—with automated liquid handlers ensuring consistent well-to-well reproducibility (chin2022phenotypicscreeningusing pages 2-4, chin2022phenotypicscreeningusing pages 9-10).

The image analysis pipelines are constructed around two complementary strategies. Population‑based analysis computes average FIRE-pHLy ratios per well while object‑based analysis segments individual lysosomes with histogram binning and defined thresholds (e.g., ±3 SD for nucleus or ratio fold‐changes) to exclude fluorescence artifacts and cytotoxic compounds (chin2022phenotypicscreeningusing pages 4-5, chin2022phenotypicscreeningusing pages 7-9). Pilot compound screening data show dynamic range differences (e.g., narrower shifts in differentiated cells) and dose–response profiles with EC50 values ranging from 2 to 35 µM (chin2022phenotypicscreeningusing pages 5-7).  

These publications provide a robust framework with explicit assay validation metrics, dynamic range, reproducibility statistics, and clear statistical hit selection thresholds. They enable reliable, high-throughput screening of lysosomal pH and cathepsin D activity that is readily adapted for RPE cultures.
