Therapeutic Candidate 1:
Candidate: Trehalose (Sigma T9531)
Hypothesis: Trehalose will restore lysosomal acidification and enhance cathepsin D–mediated autophagy in RPE by promoting TFEB nuclear translocation, thereby improving clearance of lipofuscin-like debris and slowing geographic atrophy progression in dry AMD.
Reasoning: Trehalose is a disaccharide autophagy enhancer that activates TFEB via AMPK‐dependent mTORC1 inhibition, increasing CLEAR network gene expression (including V-ATPase subunits and CTSD). Its favorable safety (GRAS status), water solubility, and blood–retinal barrier penetration support rapid repurposing. Mechanistically specific TFEB activation distinguishes trehalose from nonspecific mTOR inhibitors, offering preclinical efficacy in RPE stress models and facilitating clinical translation.

Therapeutic Candidate 54:
Candidate: SH-BC-893 (v-ATPase activator; WuXi AppTec)
