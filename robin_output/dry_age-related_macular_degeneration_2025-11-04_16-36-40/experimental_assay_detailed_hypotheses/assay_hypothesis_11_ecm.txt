Overall Evaluation: Strengths and weaknesses of this assay for testing therapeutics for dry age-related macular degeneration.

Assay Overview: This assay measures ECM protease balance at the RPE–Bruch’s membrane (BrM) interface by quantifying pro- and active gelatinases (MMP‑2/MMP‑9) in RPE-conditioned medium via gelatin zymography, optionally coupled to measurements of MT1‑MMP (MMP‑14) and TIMP‑2 that control pro‑MMP‑2 activation at the RPE surface (immunoblotting/ELISA; cell-surface localization) (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 5-8, liva2019agerelatedmaculardegeneration pages 15-18). Primary human RPE or iPSC‑RPE cultured on BrM-like substrates or altered ECM (e.g., EFEMP1‑R345W or nitrite-modified matrices) would be used to better model aged/AMD BrM, with readouts including zymography band densitometry (pro/active MMP‑2/9), MMP‑14/TIMP‑2 levels, and downstream ECM degradation markers (e.g., collagen IV fragments) (biasella2023theextracellularmicroenvironment pages 9-11, liva2019agerelatedmaculardegeneration pages 9-12).

Biomedical Evidence: MMP‑2/9 and TIMPs are present in IPM, BrM and RPE; dysregulated gelatinase activity and TIMP balance correlate with BrM thickening, drusen composition (TIMP‑3-enriched), and early deposit formation in AMD, implicating impaired MMP‑2 activation in sub‑RPE material accumulation and collagen IV deposition (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 5-8, dewing2019thediverseroles pages 5-6). MMP‑2 activation requires an RPE cell-surface MT1‑MMP/TIMP‑2 complex that is sensitive to oxidative stress, anchoring disease-relevant regulation to cell-surface events rather than bulk secretion (liva2019agerelatedmaculardegeneration pages 15-18, caban2022theroleof pages 5-7). Aging reduces BrM permeability and MMP accessibility, reinforcing the importance of local activation state over total MMP levels (dewing2019thediverseroles pages 5-6). Human and experimental data show compartment- and stage-specific alterations (e.g., increased MMP‑2 in RPE-associated IPM in AMD; mixed plasma/serum findings), supporting biological plausibility but heterogeneity (liva2019agerelatedmaculardegeneration pages 9-12, caban2022theroleof pages 5-7).

Previous Use: RPE models under hypoxia/VEGF or abnormal ECM show altered MMP‑2/9 activity, and EFEMP1‑R345W and complement C3a exposure increase MMP‑2 activation, indicating utility of activity assays in mechanistic studies, though most evidence is preclinical and not assay-validated for clinical prediction in dry AMD (biasella2023theextracellularmicroenvironment pages 9-11, liva2019agerelatedmaculardegeneration pages 15-18). Reviews note activity-based changes in AMD tissues/models but do not provide definitive validation that conditioned-medium zymography predicts dry AMD progression (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 8-10, liva2019agerelatedmaculardegeneration pages 9-12).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths: mechanistically anchored to BrM ECM turnover; sensitive to activation state; can incorporate regulators (MMP‑14/TIMP‑2) and aged/AMD-like ECM to increase relevance (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 5-8, biasella2023theextracellularmicroenvironment pages 9-11). Weaknesses: activation is cell-surface and context-dependent; conditioned-medium zymography may misrepresent in situ proteolysis, is confounded by pro‑forms/HMW complexes, and shows compartment- and stage-specific variability with stronger ties to neovascular than dry AMD (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 5-8, caban2022theroleof pages 5-7). Recommendation: pair zymography with readouts of MMP‑14/TIMP‑2 localization, collagen IV degradation on BrM-like matrices, and functional transport metrics to better model macular, age-related constraints (liva2019agerelatedmaculardegeneration pages 15-18, dewing2019thediverseroles pages 5-6).

Measuring MMP‑2/9 activity addresses a plausible, disease-relevant node of BrM remodeling and can mechanistically rank agents that normalize MMP‑14/TIMP‑2‑dependent activation of MMP‑2. However, conditioned-medium gelatin zymography alone is an incomplete surrogate for macular in situ activity and should be embedded in ECM- and surface-activation–aware assays to be informative for dry AMD therapeutics (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 5-8, biasella2023theextracellularmicroenvironment pages 9-11).
