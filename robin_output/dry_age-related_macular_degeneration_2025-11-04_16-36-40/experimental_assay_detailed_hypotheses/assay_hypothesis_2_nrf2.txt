Overall Evaluation: Strengths and weaknesses of this assay for testing therapeutics for dry age-related macular degeneration.

Assay Overview: The assay places a luciferase or GFP reporter under an antioxidant response element (ARE) to quantify NRF2 transcriptional activity as a surrogate for RPE antioxidative capacity under AMD-relevant stress. Measurements include fold induction kinetics, EC50/efficacy of test agents, and NRF2 dependence (e.g., siNRF2 or KEAP1 modulation), recorded as luminescence/fluorescence with cell viability and redox counterscreens. The most relevant cell systems are ARPE-19, primary human RPE, and hiPSC-RPE, with physiologic stressors such as H2O2, blue/LED light, cigarette smoke extract, and sodium iodate to model AMD-like oxidative/inflammatory environments. Positive controls are established NRF2 activators used in RPE studies (e.g., electrophiles such as dimethyl fumarate) and pathway benchmarks (HO-1/NQO1 induction, NRF2 nuclear translocation) for orthogonal confirmation (catanzaro2020eyelightonagerelated pages 1-2, camelo2025repurposingdimethylfumarate pages 4-6, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 5-6, webster2023nrf2associatedantioxidantstress pages 82-84, frede2017luteinactivatesthe pages 1-5).

Biomedical Evidence: NRF2 governs ARE-driven expression of phase II/antioxidant enzymes (HO-1, NQO1, SOD2, catalase, Trx1, Grx1, GCL) that determine RPE resilience to oxidative injury implicated in dry AMD/GA. NRF2 activation reduces ROS, limits NLRP3 inflammasome activity, competes with NF-κB, and suppresses IL-6/IL-1β, aligning with inflammatory features of AMD; conversely, NRF2 deficiency yields AMD-like retinal degeneration and inflammation, and human AMD RPE over drusen shows reduced NRF2 (camelo2025repurposingdimethylfumarate pages 4-6). Genetic and viral NRF2 augmentation preserves RPE and photoreceptors in NaIO3 injury, and dietary carotenoids (lutein/zeaxanthin) activate NRF2 in ARPE-19, consistent with clinical signals from AREDS/AREDS2 formulations (chen2023oxidativestressin pages 5-6, frede2017luteinactivatesthe pages 1-5, camelo2025repurposingdimethylfumarate pages 7-9).

Previous Use: While multiple RPE/retinal studies quantify NRF2 activation via nuclear translocation and HO-1/NQO1 induction, they do not explicitly report ARE luciferase/GFP reporters in RPE/AMD models. Nonetheless, transcriptional bioassays have been used to rank NRF2 activators (e.g., NQO1 induction “CD” metric in Hepa cells), enabling discovery of highly potent activators later validated in ocular models, supporting the utility of transcription-based screening cascades feeding into RPE validation (nakagami2016nrf2isan pages 3-5). In ARPE-19, phenotypic screens with siNRF2 dependence and DMF as a positive control demonstrate tractable compound triage aligned with a reporter-first strategy; in primary RPE, small-molecule NRF2 activation confers cytoprotection with siNRF2 loss-of-function confirmation (catanzaro2020eyelightonagerelated pages 1-2, liu2016thenoveltriterpenoid pages 9-10, liu2016thenoveltriterpenoid pages 1-2, frede2017luteinactivatesthe pages 1-5).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths: highly scalable, quantitative, and pathway-specific primary screen directly tied to a validated AMD-relevant mechanism in RPE; readily multiplexed with viability, ROS/GSH, and cytokine readouts; enables potency ranking before deeper RPE biology (camelo2025repurposingdimethylfumarate pages 4-6, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 5-6, liu2016thenoveltriterpenoid pages 9-10). Weaknesses: ARE output can be influenced by other stress-responsive TFs (e.g., AP-1) and by media components (e.g., nicotinamide), necessitating NRF2-dependence controls and orthogonal endpoints; electrophilic false positives and redox cycling demand cytotoxicity/reactivity counterscreens; acute reporter activation may not predict chronic RPE benefit, requiring confirmation in hiPSC-RPE and chronic stress paradigms with p62/KEAP1/autophagy integration (webster2023nrf2associatedantioxidantstress pages 82-84, hyttinen2019theregulationof pages 10-12, batliwala2017involvementofnrf2 pages 4-5). Use as a first-pass screen, followed by mechanistic and functional RPE assays under AMD-like stress, is justified.

A cell-based ARE reporter is a useful, scalable primary assay to find NRF2-pathway activators for dry AMD, provided hits are triaged with NRF2-dependence and redox/liability counterscreens. Translational relevance depends on confirming protection in RPE models under AMD-like oxidative/inflammatory stress and on demonstrating downstream enzyme induction and functional rescue (camelo2025repurposingdimethylfumarate pages 4-6, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 5-6, liu2016thenoveltriterpenoid pages 9-10, nakagami2016nrf2isan pages 3-5).
