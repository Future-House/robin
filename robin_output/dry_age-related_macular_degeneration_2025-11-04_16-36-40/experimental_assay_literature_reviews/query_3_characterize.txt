Query: Characterize impaired autophagy and lysosomal dysfunction in RPE under dry AMD–like conditions, focusing on regulators such as LC3 and p62, lysosomal pH changes, cathepsin activities, lipofuscin accumulation, and development of cell-based assays to measure autophagic flux and lysosomal degradation

Impaired autophagy in dry AMD-like RPE is marked by accumulation of autophagosomes, as defined by increased lipidated LC3 (LC3-II) levels in parallel with persistent p62/SQSTM1 accumulation, indicating a blockade in autophagosome clearance rather than enhanced initiation (cerniauskas2020complementmodulationreverses pages 2-4, golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 1-2). Lysosomal dysfunction is evident from altered pH homeostasis and diminished cathepsin activity, particularly a decrease in both the protein expression and enzymatic activity of cathepsin D, which compromises the proteolytic capacity necessary for efficient degradation of autophagic cargo (cerniauskas2020complementmodulationreverses pages 4-7). Concomitantly, accumulation of lipofuscin—exemplified by the A2E fluorophore—further impairs lysosomal function by inhibiting V-ATPase activity and perturbing cholesterol handling, creating a feed-forward loop that exacerbates proteostatic failure (jeong2019photoactivationofnretinylidenenretinylethanolamine pages 1-7, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 2-3). 

To evaluate these defects, cell-based assays have been developed that combine biochemical and imaging approaches. Immunoblotting for LC3-I/II conversion and p62 dynamics in the presence and absence of lysosomal inhibitors (e.g., bafilomycin A1 or chloroquine) distinguishes between autophagosome synthesis and degradation deficits (tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 31-35, abokyi2021targetinglysosomesto pages 4-7). Complementary live-cell assays using tandem fluorescent LC3 reporters and LysoTracker staining allow real-time monitoring of autophagosome–lysosome fusion and lysosomal acidification, thereby providing comprehensive measures of autophagic flux and lysosomal function (tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 39-44, cardoso2018stemcellderivedretinal pages 58-61).

Autophagy impairment in AMD RPE results in defective clearance of damaged proteins and organelles, while lysosomal dysfunction compounds this defect through loss of degradative capacity. These integrated cell-based assays are critical for dissecting the molecular deficits associated with dry AMD.
