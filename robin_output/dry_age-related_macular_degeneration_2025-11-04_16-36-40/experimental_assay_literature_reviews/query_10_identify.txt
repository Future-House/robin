Query: Identify and validate functional biomarkers and readouts in RPE cell culture assays for dry AMD therapeutics, including quantification of lipofuscin accumulation, drusen-like extracellular aggregates, complement deposition, pro-inflammatory cytokine panels, mitochondrial respiration metrics, and real-time cell death markers

A fit-for-purpose RPE assay panel should combine imaging, biochemical, and immunologic readouts that map to human dry AMD pathology and mechanistic validation.

Lipofuscin/bisretinoids: Quantify per-cell autofluorescence (spectral imaging) and chemically resolve bisretinoids (e.g., A2E by HPLC/LC‑MS); validate against autofluorescent granules in human RPE and cytotoxic/photo-oxidative effects of A2E on lysosomes/mitochondria (supports translational linkage and mechanism) (iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 19-20, iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 3-5, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 10-12, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 6-7).

Drusen-like extracellular aggregates: Image and quantify deposits and substructures; immunolabel ApoE/B, vitronectin, CFH, CD59, and profile cholesterol (esterified vs unesterified) to subtype deposits; OCT-defined reflective drusen substructures that predict GA provide imaging anchors; RPE on synthetic matrices can induce deposit formation for in vitro validation (iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 2-3, iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 19-20, piippo2019inflammasomesignalingand pages 59-62, iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 3-5).

Complement deposition: Measure C3 fragments and C5b‑9 on RPE after human serum exposure (IF/ELISA); complement is a major drusen component, upregulated in early AMD, and elevated in CFH risk-genotype RPE, with C1q implicated in inflammasome/pyroptosis priming (iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 19-20, piippo2019inflammasomesignalingand pages 62-67, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 6-7, hernandez2025roleofoxidative pages 10-11, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 9-10).

Cytokine/angiogenic panel and inflammasome: Use multiplex ELISA/Luminex for IL‑1β, IL‑18, IL‑6, IL‑8, MCP‑1, TNF‑α, VEGF; pair with NLRP3 assembly, caspase‑1 activation, and GSDMD cleavage to mechanistically anchor inflammation (piippo2019inflammasomesignalingand pages 62-67, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 9-10, hernandez2025roleofoxidative pages 10-11).

Mitochondrial function: Seahorse OCR (basal, ATP‑linked, spare capacity), ATP output, NAD+/NADH and PARP activity, Δψm and mitochondrial ROS; AMD RPE and cybrids show reduced basal/max respiration and ATP with NAD depletion/PARylation dysregulation, validating these endpoints (iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 19-20, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 7-8, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 10-12).

Real-time cell death: Live-cell caspase‑3/7, Annexin V externalization, and LDH release; pathway attribution via cleaved caspase‑9/3 (apoptosis), p‑RIPK1/p‑RIPK3/p‑MLKL (necroptosis), caspase‑1/cleaved GSDMD with IL‑1β/IL‑18 (pyroptosis), and lipid ROS/iron with mitochondrial shrinkage/cristae loss (ferroptosis) (iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 19-20, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 9-10, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21).

This integrated panel is justified by human donor tissue and in vivo/in vitro studies linking lipofuscin, drusen/complement, inflammatory cytokines, and mitochondrial failure to AMD progression and RPE death (iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 19-20, iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 2-3, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 7-8).

A validated RPE assay suite for dry AMD should include: autofluorescence and LC‑MS for lipofuscin/bisretinoids; imaging/immunotyping of drusen-like deposits; C3/C5b‑9 complement deposition; multiplex IL‑1β/IL‑18/IL‑6/IL‑8/MCP‑1/TNF‑α/VEGF with NLRP3 metrics; Seahorse OCR, ATP, NAD+/PARP; and live-cell caspase/Annexin V/LDH plus pathway markers. Each readout is mechanistically anchored to AMD pathology and supported by human tissue, genetic risk, and model validation (iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 19-20, iliescu2024nonneovascularagerelatedmacular pages 2-3, piippo2019inflammasomesignalingand pages 62-67, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 7-8, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 9-10, hernandez2025roleofoxidative pages 10-11, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21).
