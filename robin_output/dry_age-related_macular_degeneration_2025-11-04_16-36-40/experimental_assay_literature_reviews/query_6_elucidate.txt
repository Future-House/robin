Query: Elucidate extracellular matrix remodeling processes between RPE and Bruch’s membrane in dry AMD, including matrix metalloproteinase regulation, fibronectin and collagen cross-linking, ECM stiffness alterations, effects on RPE adhesion/migration, and assays for MMP activity and biomechanical properties

Dry AMD features progressive remodeling of the RPE–Bruch’s membrane (BrM) interface: BrM thickens, accumulates lipids and AGEs (e.g., pentosidine), loses permeability, and stiffens, collectively lowering hydraulic conductivity and undermining RPE adhesion while favoring sub‑RPE deposit/drusen formation and pathological RPE behaviors (detachment, migration) (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 25-27, schimmel2024theinfluenceof pages 5-6, shu2020emtandendmt pages 22-23). These compositional and biomechanical changes alter integrin-mediated mechanotransduction, increasing traction and focal adhesion signaling on stiffer substrates (schimmel2024theinfluenceof pages 5-6).

Matrix metalloproteinase (MMP) control is central. MMP‑2 and MMP‑9 levels/localization are altered with age/AMD, and oxidant injury reduces MMP‑2 activity secondary to downregulating MT1‑MMP (MMP‑14) and TIMP‑2, linking diminished proteolysis to collagen IV accumulation and early drusen (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 25-27, garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 8-10). Pro‑MMP‑2 activation requires an MT1‑MMP/TIMP‑2/pro‑MMP‑2 ternary complex; competitive interactions with pro‑MMP‑9 generate HMW species that lower active MMP‑2/9 in AMD tissue (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 5-8). Cytokine pathways amplify this: TGF‑β increases endothelial MMP‑9 and permeability; uPA/PAI‑1 signaling modulates invasive remodeling; MMP‑2/9 synergize in CNV (shu2020emtandendmt pages 22-23, shu2020emtandendmt pages 9-11, garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 25-27).

Collagen and fibronectin remodeling/cross‑linking exacerbate stiffness. AGE and nitrite‑mediated non‑enzymatic cross‑links accumulate in BrM, yield 3‑nitro‑tyrosine adducts, reduce collagen I/IV detectability, and lower elasticity; exogenous collagen IV rescues adhesion (eamegdool2020extracellularmatrixand pages 10-11, eamegdool2020extracellularmatrixand pages 8-10). AMD‑like ECM drives deposition of dense, entangled COL IV/VI networks and upregulates fibronectin during RPE EMT; HTRA1‑generated fibronectin fragments stimulate IL‑6, MCP‑1, MMP‑3/‑9, reinforcing proteolytic remodeling (chinchilla2021amdlikesubstratecauses pages 7-9, garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 8-10).

These matrix changes directly impair RPE function: nitrite‑crosslinked ECM reduces attachment and spreading (~30% drop), whereas collagen I/IV coatings restore adhesion and increase migration; aged/AMD BrM reduces RPE reattachment and promotes EMT with increased motility on AMD-like substrates (eamegdool2020extracellularmatrixand pages 8-10, eamegdool2020extracellularmatrixand pages 1-2, shu2020emtandendmt pages 22-23, chinchilla2021amdlikesubstratecauses pages 7-9).

Assays: MMPs are quantified by plasma/ocular fluid measurements, immunohistochemistry, drusen proteomics, and gelatin zymography for MMP‑2 activity; ECM function is evaluated by hydraulic conductivity of BrM, plus structural proxies (confocal thickness, SEM topology, directionality, branch/tortuosity metrics) and label-free two‑photon/FLIM imaging; AFM/rheology are conceptually relevant but not reported in these excerpts (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 25-27, chinchilla2021amdlikesubstratecauses pages 9-11, shu2020emtandendmt pages 22-23, eamegdool2020extracellularmatrixand pages 8-10, schimmel2024theinfluenceof pages 5-6).

In dry AMD, an altered MMP/TIMP axis (notably impaired MT1‑MMP/TIMP‑2–dependent MMP‑2 activation and HMW MMP complexes) plus AGE/nitrosative cross‑linking remodels collagen/fibronectin, stiffens BrM, and reduces hydraulic conductivity (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 5-8, garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 8-10, garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 25-27, eamegdool2020extracellularmatrixand pages 10-11). These changes weaken RPE adhesion, promote EMT and migration, and predispose to deposit formation and neovascular responses (chinchilla2021amdlikesubstratecauses pages 7-9, shu2020emtandendmt pages 22-23, garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 25-27). MMP activity (fluid assays, IHC, zymography) and ECM biomechanics/structure (conductivity, imaging-derived topology, FLIM) provide complementary readouts in these studies (garciaonrubia2020matrixmetalloproteinasesin pages 25-27, chinchilla2021amdlikesubstratecauses pages 9-11, eamegdool2020extracellularmatrixand pages 8-10).
