Query: Search for detailed molecular pathways of oxidative stress in retinal pigment epithelium cells in dry age-related macular degeneration, including sources of reactive oxygen species, antioxidant defense mechanisms, roles of NRF2, mitochondrial dysfunction, and known assayable enzyme activities

Oxidative stress in RPE in dry AMD reflects both excessive ROS production and redox-signaling rewiring; implicated species include superoxide (O2•−), singlet oxygen (1O2), hydrogen peroxide (H2O2), hydroxyl radical (HO•), nitric oxide (NO•), and peroxyl radicals (ROO•), which drive bisretinoid photodegradation, lipid peroxidation, mitochondrial/ER stress, DNA damage, inflammation, defective autophagy, and drusen deposition (basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 5-7). Principal ROS sources are mitochondria (∼90% of intracellular ROS) where ETC electron leak generates O2•− that is dismutated to H2O2 and converted via Fenton chemistry to HO•; high choroidal O2, intense light exposure, PUFA-rich outer segments, and the respiratory burst during daily RPE phagocytosis further amplify oxidative load, with additional inputs from NADPH oxidases, lipofuscin/A2E photo-oxidation, peroxisomal β-oxidation, and iron biology (ferritin buffering) (basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 7-8, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 5-7, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 1-2, toma2021oxidativeandnitrosative pages 4-5, toma2021oxidativeandnitrosative pages 17-19).

NRF2 is the central transcriptional regulator of antioxidant and detoxification genes in RPE and is integrated with p62/Keap1 feedback, AMPK–PGC‑1α signaling, and mitophagy/autophagy programs that restrain ROS by removing oxidized proteins and damaged mitochondria; loss of NRF2 or combined NRF2/PGC‑1α precipitates AMD-like RPE damage (basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 5-7, nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 18-19, nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 1-2, toma2021oxidativeandnitrosative pages 17-19). Core NRF2-dependent enzymatic defenses include SOD1/2/3 (O2•− dismutation), catalase (H2O2 to H2O/O2), glutathione peroxidases (H2O2 and lipid hydroperoxides reduction at the expense of GSH), peroxiredoxins with SRXN1 repair, HO‑1 (heme degradation yielding bilirubin as a radical trap with iron sequestered by ferritin), NQO1 (quinone detoxification), GSTs, GCLC/GSH, thioredoxin/glutaredoxin systems, metallothionein, and sirtuins (basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 7-8, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 5-7, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 2-3).

Mitochondrial dysfunction is a defining downstream and feed-forward driver: AMD RPE exhibits reduced mitochondrial density, ATP output, cristae integrity, early mtDNA damage, depolarization, cytochrome c release, and increased ROS; impaired mitophagy contributes, whereas PGC‑1α–NRF1/2–TFAM programs promote biogenesis and MQC (chen2023oxidativestressin pages 2-3, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 1-2, toma2021oxidativeandnitrosative pages 4-5, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 7-8, nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 1-2).

Assayable activities and markers widely used include enzyme activities (SOD, catalase, GPx; TRX/GRX system), GSH/GSSG and total GSH, HO‑1/NQO1 and SOD1/2 protein levels (immunoblot/qPCR), ferritin, GST activity, and serum/tissue total antioxidant/oxidant status; lipid peroxidation adducts (MDA, 4‑HNE, CEP), ROS measurements, mitochondrial respiration and membrane potential, mtDNA damage, autophagy/mitophagy flux markers (LC3‑II, BECN1, p62; ULK1 axis), AMPK and NRF2 activation, and caspase‑3 activity; catalase gene delivery has shown protection against photo‑oxidative injury in RPE/photoreceptors (basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 7-8, kushwah2023oxidativestressand pages 13-14, kushwah2023oxidativestressand pages 8-10, kushwah2023oxidativestressand pages 7-8, nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 18-19).

Dry AMD RPE endures high ROS from mitochondria, light, hyperoxia, PUFA peroxidation, phagocytic bursts, and enzymatic oxidases, overwhelming defenses and coupling to inflammation and degeneration (basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 5-7, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 7-8, toma2021oxidativeandnitrosative pages 4-5). NRF2-centered enzymatic networks and mitophagy/autophagy maintain redox balance but decline with age; their readouts and classical antioxidant enzyme activities provide robust, assayable endpoints for mechanistic studies and interventions (nita2023antioxidativeroleof pages 18-19, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 7-8, kushwah2023oxidativestressand pages 8-10).
