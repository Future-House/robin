Query: Define lipid metabolism dysregulation in RPE and Bruch’s membrane in dry AMD, exploring roles of apolipoproteins, cholesterol transporters ABCA1/ABCG1, fatty acid oxidation, lipid droplet formation, and quantitative assays for intracellular lipid species in cell culture models

Lipid metabolism dysregulation in the retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) and Bruch’s membrane in dry AMD is characterized by impaired reverse cholesterol transport resulting from reduced expression and functional activity of cholesterol transporters such as ABCA1 and ABCG1. These deficiencies hinder normal cholesterol efflux, leading to intracellular accumulation of unesterified cholesterol and neutral lipids that contribute to the formation of drusen-like deposits in Bruch’s membrane (florian2022regulationofabca1 pages 1-2, florian2022regulationofabca1 pages 2-5).

Apolipoproteins—including ApoAI, APOE, and APOB—are critical for assembling nascent HDL particles through direct interactions with ABCA1; perturbations in these interactions ultimately exacerbate lipid retention within the RPE and promote extracellular deposit formation (florian2022regulationofabca1 pages 1-2, storti2019impairedabca1abcg1mediatedlipid pages 1-2). A concomitant reduction in fatty acid oxidation, manifesting as a metabolic shift from lipid oxidation to glycolysis under hypoxic or ageing conditions, further increases intracellular lipid load by impairing the processing of lipids derived from phagocytosed photoreceptor outer segments (florian2022regulationofabca1 pages 11-12, zhou2024pgc1αrepressiondysregulates pages 1-2). In response, lipid droplets form as intracellular reservoirs of neutral lipids, as demonstrated by OilRedO and LipidTOX staining (storti2019impairedabca1abcg1mediatedlipid pages 2-3, grubaugh2024microsomaltriglyceridetransfer pages 15-17). Quantitative assays in cell culture models—such as fluorescent cholesterol efflux measurements and mass spectrometry–based lipidomics—provide precise profiling of intracellular lipid species (florian2022regulationofabca1 pages 11-12, storti2019impairedabca1abcg1mediatedlipid pages 29-30).

This dysregulation underlines a failure in RPE lipid processing that is central to dry AMD pathogenesis. Accurate quantification of lipid species using imaging and lipidomic techniques is essential for understanding the molecular defects and for guiding the development of targeted therapeutic interventions.
