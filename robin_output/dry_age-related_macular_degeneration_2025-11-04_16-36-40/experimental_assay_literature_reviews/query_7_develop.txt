Query: Develop methodologies to model RPE barrier integrity and tight junction dynamics under dry AMD stressors, assessing expression and function of ZO-1, occludin, cadherins, trans-epithelial electrical resistance measurements, paracellular permeability assays, and high-content imaging of junctional proteins

Platform and stress paradigm. Use differentiated human RPE systems—ARPE-19, primary, or iPSC-derived RPE—grown on porous supports as monocultures or in bilayer co-culture with choroidal/retinal endothelial cells; microfluidic organ-on-chip and retina-on-chip formats enable perfusion and co-culture readouts relevant to oBRB physiology (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 300-302, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 302-304, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 298-300). Select membrane substrates with proven adhesion/permeability performance (PC or PET; avoid untreated ePTFE) and mature cultures longitudinally; document day-by-day TEER to track barrier development (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 138-141, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 13-14). Apply dry AMD–relevant stressors: hypoxia (1–5% O2 or CoCl2), oxidative stress (e.g., H2O2), and high-glucose/diabetic-like conditions, individually or combined to accelerate phenotypes (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 300-302, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 63-66, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 302-304).

Functional barrier assays. Monitor TEER with EVOM/ECIS for real-time integrity, standardizing temperature, pH, medium height, electrode condition/orientation, and blank corrections; acquire kinetic profiles pre-, during, and post-stressor (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 300-302, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 138-141, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 13-14). For closed chips/bioreactors, embed TEER electrodes to enable undisturbed continuous measurements (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 63-66). Complement TEER with paracellular FITC–dextran flux (multiple MWs) to compute diffusion coefficients and apparent permeability (Papp) in mono- and co-cultures (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 138-141, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 13-14, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 63-66).

Molecular and imaging endpoints. Quantify ZO-1, occludin, and cadherins by immunocytochemistry coupled to high-content/confocal imaging (including Z-sections) to score continuity, junctional length, and mislocalization; extend with Western/proteomic profiling when needed (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 13-14, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 300-302, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 302-304, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 309-311, hau2019recreationinvitro pages 89-90). Correlate structural metrics with TEER and Papp, recognizing that ZO-1 positivity alone does not guarantee a functional barrier (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 138-141). In microphysiological systems, combine integrated sensors with automated imaging for longitudinal multi-parameter assessment; probe cytoskeletal signaling (e.g., ROCK) to dissect stressor-induced junction remodeling (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 300-302, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 298-300, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 309-311).

This workflow models RPE barrier integrity under AMD-like insults using optimized RPE platforms, controlled stressors, and complementary TEER, tracer permeability, and high-content junctional imaging. It resolves both expression and function of ZO-1, occludin, and cadherins, enabling rigorous, longitudinal interrogation of tight junction dynamics (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 300-302, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 13-14, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 63-66).
