Query: Which mechanistic cell-based assays and high-content screening methodologies can be employed to evaluate therapeutic candidates for dry AMD, including fluorescent or luminescent ROS and mitochondrial function imaging, autophagy and inflammasome flux assays, phagocytic activity measurements, transepithelial resistance, cytokine and complement protein ELISA, lipid droplet quantification, and multiplexed readouts?

Mechanistic cell‐based assays for evaluating dry AMD therapeutic candidates include several high‐content screening approaches utilizing iPSC‐derived RPE models. Immunofluorescence imaging protocols have been established to monitor mitochondrial function and lysosomal activity using specific dyes such as those employed with LysoTracker, thereby enabling indirect assessment of autophagy flux (ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 12-14). The Cell Mito Stress Test, which relies on extracellular flux analysis, quantifies oxygen consumption rates to define basal respiration, maximal respiration, and spare respiratory capacity—parameters critical for evaluating mitochondrial efficacy (ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 12-14, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 4-6). Fluorescent or luminescent probes to detect reactive oxygen species (ROS) can be integrated within similar high-content imaging platforms, complementing mitochondrial assessments, while lipid peroxidation assays provide insight into oxidative stress and lipid metabolism (zhou2024pgc1αrepressiondysregulates pages 2-2). In addition, phagocytic activity is measured by quantifying the internalization of photoreceptor outer segments, a key functionality of RPE cells (ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 4-6). Cytokine release, exemplified by VEGF-A secretion, is determined via ELISA, which can be expanded to include complement proteins (ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 12-14). Moreover, lipid droplet accumulation is quantified using assays that evaluate fatty acid oxidation and lipid peroxidation (zhou2024pgc1αrepressiondysregulates pages 2-2). Although transepithelial resistance and explicit inflammasome flux assays are not detailed in the provided studies, these measurements can be incorporated into multiplexed readout platforms to further characterize RPE barrier and inflammatory responses.

Using these integrated, mechanistic assays, one can comprehensively assess mitochondrial function, oxidative stress, autophagy, phagocytosis, cytokine secretion, and lipid metabolism in RPE cells, thus providing robust endpoints for evaluating novel dry AMD therapies. This multifaceted approach is essential to elucidate therapeutic efficacy and mechanistic impact in disease-relevant cellular models.
