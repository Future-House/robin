Query: Studies investigating repurposed drugs with preliminary data showing improved RPE phagocytosis or outer segment clearance in vitro or in vivo, with available pharmacokinetic and safety data relevant to ocular delivery

Remofuscin (soraprazan) is a repurposed acid pump antagonist originally developed for gastrointestinal indications that has been evaluated in preclinical studies for the reduction of lipofuscin accumulation in retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) cells—a process linked to outer segment clearance. (lyu2023investigationofthe pages 18-22, julien‐schraermeyer2020penetrationdistributionand pages 1-2) In vitro experiments using aged primary human RPE cells and Abca4–/– mouse RPE cells have demonstrated that remofuscin effectively removes preexisting lipofuscin without inducing cytotoxicity or impairing the essential phagocytic function of these cells. (julien‐schraermeyer2020penetrationdistributionand pages 1-2) In vivo, a single intravitreal injection in pigmented Abca4–/– mice led to a more than twofold reduction in lipofuscin granules, with transmission electron microscopic autoradiography confirming selective drug accumulation in the RPE and prolonged retention—detectable for up to 20 weeks. (breiki2020trialinterventionsin pages 41-45, julien‐schraermeyer2020penetrationdistributionand pages 5-8) Quantitative pharmacokinetic studies using HPLC-MS corroborate these findings by revealing predictable ocular tissue distribution and minimal intraocular metabolism. (julien‐schraermeyer2020penetrationdistributionand pages 1-2, julien‐schraermeyer2020penetrationdistributionand pages 5-8) Safety evaluations, including extended oral dosing in monkeys and intravitreal delivery in mice, have reported no adverse ocular events, as evidenced by normal ERG responses, thereby underscoring the favorable ocular safety profile of remofuscin. (julien‐schraermeyer2020penetrationdistributionand pages 1-2, julien‐schraermeyer2020penetrationdistributionand pages 5-8, lyu2023investigationofthe pages 18-22) Although direct measurements of enhanced RPE phagocytosis or outer segment clearance were not explicitly provided, the maintained phagocytic capacity coupled with robust lipofuscin clearance supports the potential for improved outer segment turnover. (breiki2020trialinterventionsin pages 41-45, lyu2023investigationofthe pages 18-22)

Remofuscin exhibits a favorable pharmacokinetic and safety profile when delivered via the ocular route, making it a promising candidate for targeting lipofuscin-associated retinal degeneration. The preclinical data support further clinical evaluation of remofuscin for enhancing RPE clearance functions and potentially mitigating progressive retinal pathology.
