Query: Research on inflammasome activation (e.g., NLRP3) in RPE and its causal role in phagocytosis impairment, including evidence from genetic or pharmacological inhibition in cell and animal models

RPE constitutively express NLRP3 inflammasome components (NLRP3, ASC, procaspase‑1) and pro‑IL‑18, require priming for pro‑IL‑1β, and can be activated by AMD‑relevant stimuli (e.g., Alu RNA via P2X7‑dependent K+ efflux, lipofuscin/A2E, lysosomal damage), with assembled inflammasomes forming large cytoplasmic aggregates; autophagy constrains this activity but declines with age, conceptually linking inflammasome activity to disrupted cellular clearance and potentially phagocytic function (though direct phagocytosis measurements are not provided) (celkova2015nlrp3inflammasomeand pages 10-12, celkova2015nlrp3inflammasomeand pages 3-5).

Selective NLRP3 inhibitors (CRID3, IFM‑632, IFM‑514) suppress inflammasome readouts (IL‑1β, LDH) across diverse triggers in ARPE‑19 and primary human RPE, and in ex vivo mouse RPE/choroid/sclera; blue‑light–irradiated Abca4−/− eye cups show preserved RPE morphology with IFM‑632 or with Nlrp3 deletion, but no phagocytosis assays were performed (wang2019efficacyofnovel pages 4-6, wang2019efficacyofnovel pages 6-9, wang2019efficacyofnovel pages 1-2).

Hsp90 inhibition with geldanamycin prevents NLRP3 activation in ARPE‑19, hESC‑RPE, and primary RPE by promoting NLRP3 clearance, yet phagocytic outcomes were not assessed (piippo2018hsp90inhibitionas pages 1-2, piippo2018hsp90inhibitionas pages 7-8). iPSC‑RPE (AMD vs control) demonstrate inflammasome activation upon proteasome/autophagy blockade and higher baseline IL‑1β in AMD‑derived cells; phagocytic competence was confirmed at baseline, but causal impairment by inflammasome activation and its rescue were not tested (hytti2021differentialexpressionof pages 2-4, hytti2021differentialexpressionof pages 1-2).

Autophagy both limits inflammasomes and can reverse certain cytokine‑driven phenotypes; importantly, autophagy stimulation in RPE did not alter phagocytic activity in the cited model, and a macrophage‑centric CNV model localized caspase‑1 activation primarily to infiltrating macrophages rather than RPE (celkova2015nlrp3inflammasomeand pages 8-10, liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 7-9). Genetic data indicate Nlrp3/IL‑1β or P2X7R deficiency mitigates chorioretinal pathologies and Alu RNA–induced RPE degeneration, but phagocytosis readouts are not reported (korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 82-84, celkova2015nlrp3inflammasomeand pages 3-5).

There is robust evidence that NLRP3 activates in RPE and that genetic or pharmacologic inhibition reduces inflammasome signaling, cytotoxicity, and structural damage in cell and ex vivo/animal models. However, the provided sources do not demonstrate that NLRP3 activation causally impairs RPE phagocytosis, nor that genetic or pharmacologic inhibition rescues phagocytic function. Direct phagocytosis assays linked to NLRP3 modulation are not reported in these excerpts (wang2019efficacyofnovel pages 4-6, wang2019efficacyofnovel pages 6-9, wang2019efficacyofnovel pages 1-2, celkova2015nlrp3inflammasomeand pages 8-10, hytti2021differentialexpressionof pages 2-4, liu2016impairingautophagyin pages 7-9).
