Query: Reviews integrating genetic risk loci for dry AMD (e.g., CFH, ARMS2/HTRA1) with functional studies demonstrating how these variants impair photoreceptor outer segment phagocytosis by RPE cells

Multiple reviews synthesize genetic and functional data to link common dry AMD loci with impaired RPE phagocytosis of photoreceptor outer segments (POS). For CFH, expert summaries from CMLS highlight direct, cell-intrinsic defects in RPE from carriers of the Y402H risk allele—reduced mitochondrial respiration, decreased phagolysosomal activity, and weakened integrin–Bruch’s membrane (BM) adhesion—which together lower the energy supply and degradative capacity required for POS ingestion and trafficking (armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 8-10). These same reviews integrate extracellular changes—complement turnover products and MAC accumulating in BM/intercapillary septa, and FHR antagonists of FH—that promote a pro-inflammatory, oxidative microenvironment further compromising RPE phagocytic function; low-level complement activity may aid physiological POS clearance, but chronic activation is injurious (armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 8-10). Complement dysregulation as a causal driver is reinforced by CD46-deficient mice that develop drusen, RPE degeneration, and elevated MAC/VEGF, implicating inadequate complement control in RPE injury that would secondarily blunt phagocytosis (bhumika2024geneticinsightsinto pages 7-9). Mechanistically, FH H402 is less effective at limiting C3b deposition and protecting oxidatively stressed RPE surfaces, amplifying complement-mediated damage (lin2018theroleof pages 67-70).

At the 10q26 locus, ARMS2 is characterized as an ECM-associated, secreted protein whose loss (via an indel-linked unstable transcript) is observed in risk-homozygous monocytes; recombinant ARMS2 binds properdin and increases C3b opsonization on ARPE-19, supporting a role in complement-mediated debris tagging for phagocytic clearance, such that ARMS2 deficiency would reduce efficient opsonization (armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 8-10, lin2018theroleof pages 11-14, lin2018theroleof pages 67-70). Although direct assays of ARMS2 effects on RPE POS phagocytosis are not shown in these reviews, the proposed mechanism is convergent with reduced clearance and drusen accrual (armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 8-10). HTRA1 overexpression—genetically driven and induced by oxidized POS-like stimuli—can remodel BM/ECM and proteolytically target surface proteins, including evidence for CD36 extracellular domain cleavage that alters oxidized lipid uptake; CFH can bind/protect CD36, yielding antagonistic regulation of oxPL uptake with functional consequences for lipid-rich POS handling (lu2021theinterplayof pages 6-7, lin2019cfhandhtra1 pages 14-18, lin2019cfhandhtra1 pages 28-34). Complement–lipid crosstalk and lysosomal/proteostatic stress in RPE further impede phagosome–lysosome fusion and POS digestion, linking CFH/APOE interactions and lipid toxicity to degradative failure (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 5-6).

Collectively, reviews converge on two mechanistic axes: (1) CFH-centered complement dysregulation plus intrinsic RPE mitochondrial/lysosomal defects directly depress POS ingestion and degradation; and (2) ARMS2/HTRA1-driven changes alter opsonization and ECM/receptor biology, indirectly reducing POS clearance. Direct experimental links are strongest for CFH; for ARMS2/HTRA1, the evidence is mechanistic but less directly tied to RPE POS phagocytosis assays (armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 8-10, lin2018theroleof pages 11-14, lin2018theroleof pages 67-70, lu2021theinterplayof pages 6-7, lin2019cfhandhtra1 pages 14-18, lin2019cfhandhtra1 pages 28-34, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 5-6).

In sum, CFH Y402H is linked to reduced RPE mitochondrial and phagolysosomal function and to uncontrolled complement at BM, directly undermining POS phagocytosis. ARMS2 loss limits properdin-dependent opsonization and HTRA1 excess remodels ECM and perturbs CD36, plausibly impairing lipid-rich POS handling; however, direct POS phagocytosis assays for ARMS2/HTRA1 are still limited in these reviews (armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 8-10, lin2018theroleof pages 11-14, lu2021theinterplayof pages 6-7).
