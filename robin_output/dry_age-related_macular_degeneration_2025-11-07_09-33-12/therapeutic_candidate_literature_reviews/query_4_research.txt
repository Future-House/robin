Query: Research articles reporting screening of commercially available compound libraries (e.g., FDA-approved or bioactive collections) for enhancers of RPE phagocytosis, including details on library composition, hit rates, and follow-up mechanistic studies

Several studies have employed high-throughput, phenotypic screening platforms based on human pluripotent stem cell–derived RPE to identify enhancers of photoreceptor outer segment (POS) phagocytosis using commercially available compound libraries. For instance, one set of reports screened the MicroSource Discovery Systems Pharmakon library, comprising 1,600 FDA-approved compounds, while others screened combined collections such as the Prestwick and LOPAC-Sigma libraries (~2,400 compounds) (karlUnknownyearsvenschreiterkaterina pages 11-13, karlUnknownyearsvenschreiterkaterina pages 2-3, schreiter2020ahumanretinal pages 9-11). In these assays, RPE seeded in 384-well plates were challenged with fluorescently labeled and sonicated POS after priming with MFGE8 (and in some cases, GAS6 was omitted to emphasize MERTK-independent pathways) (karlUnknownyearsvenschreiterkaterina pages 2-3, karlUnknownyearsvenschreiterkaterina pages 27-28). Hits were defined by stringent Z-score thresholds (typically Z ≥ +3 for enhancements), yielding low hit rates on the order of approximately 0.08–0.13% (karlUnknownyearsvenschreiterkaterina pages 8-10, schreiter2020ahumanretinal pages 7-9). Confirmed hits such as ramoplanin (RM) and pyrithione zinc (PZ) were repurchased and assessed in dose–response experiments using both 384-well and transwell formats. Downstream mechanistic studies—which included confocal imaging, FACS analysis, western blotting, scanning electron microscopy, and transcriptomic profiling—demonstrated that RM, for example, not only enhanced POS uptake but also rescued internalization defects in MERTK mutant RPE by upregulating regulators such as S1PR5, MFSD2A, and ERMN (schreiter2020ahumanretinal pages 11-13, schreiter2020ahumanretinal pages 5-7, karlUnknownyearsvenschreiterkaterina pages 10-11).

This research shows that screening FDA-approved and bioactive compound libraries can yield reproducible enhancers of RPE phagocytosis. Subsequent mechanistic follow-up validates these hits and provides insight into their underlying molecular pathways.
