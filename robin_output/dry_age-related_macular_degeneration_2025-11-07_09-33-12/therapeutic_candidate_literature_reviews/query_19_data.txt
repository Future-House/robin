Query: Data on patient-derived RPE organoids or explants used to model phagocytosis defects in dry AMD for drug screening, including validation of disease phenotype and assay reproducibility

No published datasets demonstrate patient-derived RPE organoids or explants that model dry AMD phagocytosis defects and are validated for drug screening with quantitative reproducibility metrics (e.g., Z′, inter-donor CV) (xuan20213dengineeringfor pages 4-5, mesch2021…invitro pages 74-77, mesch2021…invitro pages 77-79, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 14-16, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 17-19, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 14-17). iPSC-derived 3D RPE organoids can phagocytose FITC-labeled POS, and organoid/retina-on-chip configurations have visualized POS uptake, but these studies are not in AMD patient–specific lines; they also face signal interference from pigmentation/autofluorescence, interior heterogeneity, and device-to-device variability that limit assay readiness for screening (mesch2021…invitro pages 74-77, mesch2021…invitro pages 77-79, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 14-16). 

Patient-derived iPSC-RPE monolayers capture phagocytosis biology with standard POS-binding/engulfment and pulse–chase degradation assays, yet AMD-relevant phenotypes are often donor/genotype- and stress-context–dependent, and cross-lab standardized differentiation/validation is lacking (bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 11-12, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 12-14, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 9-11). Proof-of-concept screening in patient-derived RPE has largely used oxidative-stress viability or complement-induced pathology readouts rather than phagocytosis endpoints (e.g., curcumin as a ROS-reducing hit; complement-suppression screens), underscoring a gap for phagocytosis-focused assays (xuan20213dengineeringfor pages 4-5, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 14-17, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 12-14). 

Consensus validation guidance for phagocytosis assays includes: POS dose optimization, reporting particles-per-cell and fraction of phagocytic cells, pulse–chase degradation kinetics, donor genotyping and metabolic profiling, replication across donors, and consideration of co-culture/organ-on-chip to improve physiological relevance—steps not yet reported for patient-derived organoid/explant phagocytosis screens (bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 11-12, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 12-14).

Direct, validated patient-derived organoid or explant assays of RPE phagocytosis defects for drug screening—with quantitative reproducibility—are not yet available. Existing 3D and iPSC-RPE platforms show feasibility but remain at proof-of-concept without standardized phagocytosis readouts or multicenter reproducibility metrics. Methodological standardization and rigorous validation are the key unmet needs before deployment in screening. (xuan20213dengineeringfor pages 4-5, mesch2021…invitro pages 74-77, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 14-16)
