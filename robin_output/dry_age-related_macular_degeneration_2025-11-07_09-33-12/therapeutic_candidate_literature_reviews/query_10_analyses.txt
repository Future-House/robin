Query: Analyses of structure–activity relationships and lead optimization efforts for small molecules identified as enhancers of RPE phagocytosis, including any ADME profiling and medicinal chemistry efforts

The provided materials do not report structure–activity relationships (SAR), lead-optimization series, or ADME/PK data for bona fide small-molecule enhancers of RPE phagocytosis; instead, they describe an HTS-to-lead framework directly applicable to discovering and optimizing such enhancers (including activity thresholds, staged confirmation, orthogonal assays, and primary RPE validation) (sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 6-8, sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 1-2). Primary hit triage entails independent resupply confirmation, matched-condition cytotoxicity assays, and counter-screens to remove non-specific/autofluorescent interference; tertiary confirmation switches assay modality (e.g., luminescence vs fluorescence), employs primary cells, and uses phenotype-relevant assays to prioritize on-target activity in RPE systems (sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 6-8). Assay engineering guidance includes miniaturization to 384/1536 wells, robust performance criteria (e.g., Z′ > 0.5; cell-based CV < 15%), and appropriate detection technologies—principles that can be adapted to RPE phagocytosis readouts to generate reliable SAR (sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 4-6).

Medicinal chemistry is envisioned as iterative, chemoinformatics-guided design–make–test cycles to increase potency/selectivity, improve solubility, and optimize ADME/Tox; where target structures exist, modeling can guide analog design (sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 6-8). ADME/PK profiling is integrated early (small-animal PK), followed by efficacy in disease-relevant models and multidisciplinary preclinical development; iPSC-derived RPE cells and repurposing libraries are practical accelerants for translation (sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 1-2, sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 8-9, sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 9-11). High-content, imaging-enabled HTS (e.g., CLSM of uptake/lysosomal trafficking) and multiplex biochemical readouts provide multiparametric phenotypes to build SAR and rank liabilities for phagocytic endpoints (liu2018creativeuseof pages 7-9).

Transferable medicinal-chemistry lessons from phagocytosis modulators in monocytes/macrophages include removing reactive disulfides, exploring phenyl‑pyrazoles/pyrroles, and deploying isoxazoles to improve solubility; in silico drug-like filters, rigorous analytical QC, and early cytotoxicity triage were used, though these data concern inhibitors rather than RPE enhancers (loriamini2023smallmoleculedrugs pages 9-11, loriamini2023smallmoleculedrugs pages 2-6, loriamini2023smallmoleculedrugs pages 12-14, loriamini2023smallmoleculedrugs pages 11-12).

There are no reported SAR, medicinal-chemistry series, or ADME/PK results for RPE-phagocytosis enhancers in the provided sources (sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 6-8). The context instead specifies a rigorous phenotypic HTS-to-lead path with orthogonal confirmation in primary/iPSC-derived RPE, iterative chemistry guided by chemoinformatics, and integrated ADME/PK and animal efficacy (sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 1-2, sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 8-9, sun2017drugdiscoveryand pages 4-6). Related phagocytosis inhibitor campaigns offer concrete, transferable med‑chem tactics (e.g., removal of reactive motifs; isoxazoles for solubility) to guide enhancer optimization (loriamini2023smallmoleculedrugs pages 9-11).
