Query: Publications evaluating small-molecule agonists or antagonists of receptor tyrosine kinases highly expressed in RPE (e.g., PDGFRβ, FGFR1) for their impact on phagocytic uptake of photoreceptor outer segments in disease-relevant assays

The available context does not include any publications that directly evaluate small‐molecule agonists or antagonists of PDGFRβ or FGFR1 for modulating the phagocytic uptake of photoreceptor outer segments in disease‐relevant assays. SU5402, a multi‐targeted inhibitor with potent activity against FGFR1 and more modest effects on PDGFRβ, is cited as being used in RPE differentiation protocols to promote survival and maturation (arterocastro2019theidentificationof pages 6-8), yet no quantitative or qualitative data are provided linking this agent—or other similar small molecules—to changes in RPE phagocytosis. Furthermore, while several studies detail robust methods and experimental systems to assess RPE phagocytosis (mazzoni2014understandingphotoreceptorouter pages 1-2, yu2018phagocytosedphotoreceptorouter pages 9-10, chiang2017quantitativephosphoproteomicsreveals pages 20-21), they do not report any direct evaluation of PDGFRβ/FGFR1 modulators in this context (arterocastro2019theidentificationof pages 9-10, kevany2010phagocytosisofretinal pages 9-10). Thus, the literature provided supports the use of small molecules in RPE differentiation and outlines approaches for studying phagocytosis, but does not provide evidence for a direct impact of PDGFRβ or FGFR1 modulation on photoreceptor outer segment uptake.

The cited studies do not demonstrate that small-molecule modulation of PDGFRβ or FGFR1 affects RPE phagocytosis in disease-relevant models. Further targeted research is needed to directly evaluate this potential therapeutic approach.
