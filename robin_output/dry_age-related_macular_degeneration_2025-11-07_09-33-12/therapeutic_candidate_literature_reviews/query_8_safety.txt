Query: Safety, ocular pharmacokinetics, and tissue distribution studies of topically administered or intravitreally delivered small molecules targeting RPE phagocytosis, including data in non-human primates or human volunteers

ClinicalTrials.gov returned zero registered human studies that simultaneously meet: small molecule, topical or intravitreal delivery, RPE/retinal pigment epithelium phagocytosis targeting, and PK/tissue distribution/safety endpoints in adult or older-adult populations, highlighting an absence of qualifying clinical trials under the specified constraints (Clinical Trial Search: 2c327c09be3b).

Within the provided evidence, there are no non-human primate or human volunteer PK, tissue-distribution, or safety studies of small molecules explicitly designed to modulate RPE phagocytosis (Clinical Trial Search: 2c327c09be3b). Related but non-equivalent NHP data exist for intravitreal peptide complement inhibitors (compstatin analogs), which are not small molecules nor RPE-phagocytosis modulators; these show prolonged intraocular residence at C3-saturating levels for >3 months after a single IVT dose and retinal/choroidal localization, but lack safety outcomes in the cited pages (hughes2020prolongedintraocularresidence pages 2-3, hughes2020prolongedintraocularresidence pages 1-2).

Preclinical rabbit studies using levofloxacin as a high melanin-binding model small molecule demonstrate route-dependent ocular PK and pronounced enrichment/retention in pigmented tissues (including choroid–RPE) after topical or intravitreal dosing; these data inform delivery principles but are not NHP/human and do not address RPE phagocytosis as a pharmacologic target (bahrpeyma2025bindingofocular pages 152-157, bahrpeyma2025bindingofocular pages 164-165). Broad PK constraints emphasize that topical delivery seldom achieves therapeutic posterior-segment levels, whereas intravitreal dosing provides direct retinal/RPE access; again, these are general principles rather than target-specific NHP/human data (bahrpeyma2025bindingofocular pages 36-40, bahrpeyma2025bindingofocular pages 157-158). Intravitreal biologic programs in NHPs (e.g., lampalizumab) illustrate safety endpoints and tolerability patterns but are not small molecules or RPE-phagocytosis agents (bantseev2018nonclinicalsafetyassessment pages 1-2, bantseev2018nonclinicalsafetyassessment pages 5-7).

There are no identified NHP or human studies of topically or intravitreally delivered small molecules specifically targeting RPE phagocytosis with PK/tissue-distribution/safety readouts. The ClinicalTrials.gov query returned zero trials meeting these combined criteria, and available preclinical data are either non-small-molecule NHP studies or rabbit small-molecule PK exemplars not tied to RPE phagocytosis (Clinical Trial Search: 2c327c09be3b, hughes2020prolongedintraocularresidence pages 2-3, bahrpeyma2025bindingofocular pages 152-157).
