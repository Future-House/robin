Query: Perform a comprehensive analysis of inflammatory cytokine and chemokine profiles secreted by retinal pigment epithelial cells and choroidal endothelial cells in dry age-related macular degeneration, focusing on IL-1β, IL-6, TNFα, MCP-1, their signaling pathways, effects on RPE survival and barrier function, and potential modulation in drug assays.

Retinal pigment epithelial (RPE) cells in dry age‐related macular degeneration (AMD) secrete a spectrum of pro-inflammatory cytokines—most notably IL-1β, IL-6, and TNFα—alongside the chemokine MCP-1 (CCL2), which acts to recruit monocytes and sustain local inflammation (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 30-32, siddiqui2024investigatingchemokineinducednfîºb pages 10-12). Activation of specific receptors by these mediators triggers intracellular signaling cascades. IL-1β signals via IL-1RI and recruits MyD88, IRAK, and TRAF6, leading to TAK1 and IKK complex activation; this culminates in IκB degradation and NF-κB nuclear translocation, inducing transcription of further pro-inflammatory genes (siddiqui2024investigatingchemokineinducednfîºb pages 10-12, shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 27-30). IL-6 signals via its receptor IL-6R/gp130, engaging JAK/STAT3 and secondarily stimulating NF-κB, whereas MCP-1 binds CCR2 to activate G-protein–coupled pathways including PLC-mediated generation of DAG and IP3 and downstream MAPK activation (siddiqui2024investigatingchemokineinducednfîºb pages 29-32, siddiqui2024investigatingchemokineinducednfîºb pages 20-22).

These cascades result in increased production of reactive oxygen species and upregulation of matrix metalloproteinases, processes that compromise RPE survival and barrier function by disrupting tight junction integrity and extracellular matrix stability (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 30-32, shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 27-30). Several preclinical assays have demonstrated that pharmacologic modulation—with agents such as dihydromyricetin, vinpocetine, quercetin, and resveratrol—attenuates NF-κB and MAPK signaling pathways, thereby reducing cytokine secretion, oxidative stress, and barrier dysfunction in RPE models (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 30-32, moustardas2023mapkpathwaysin pages 19-20).

It must be noted that the provided evidence does not offer detailed quantitative cytokine profiles or a direct comparison between RPE and choroidal endothelial cells in dry AMD (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 30-32, shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 32-35). 

RPE-derived cytokine release drives chronic inflammation and subsequent cellular dysfunction in dry AMD. Modulating these signaling pathways via targeted pharmacologic agents is a promising therapeutic strategy.
