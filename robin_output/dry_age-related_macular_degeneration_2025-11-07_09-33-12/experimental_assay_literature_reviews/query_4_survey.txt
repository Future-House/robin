Query: Survey the spectrum of in vitro retinal pigment epithelial cell culture models used in dry age-related macular degeneration research, covering primary RPE, immortalized lines, iPSC-derived RPE, three-dimensional organoid systems, co-culture with choroidal endothelial cells, air-liquid interface configurations, and their respective phenotypic fidelity to in vivo pathology.

Primary human RPE (fetal/adult) cultured as confluent, polarized monolayers on permeable, track‑etched/transwell membranes are the molecular/functional benchmark. They reproduce native morphology, tight junctions, apical–basal polarity and directional secretion, with transcriptomic/proteomic concordance to in vivo RPE; AMD donor primary RPE retain disease “metabolic memory,” showing altered bioenergetics and complement responses, and can deposit drusen components and trigger complement activation in vitro (rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 11-12, lynn2018aconvenientprotocol pages 20-20, rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 6-7, fisher2022humanipscand pages 14-15, wu2022tissueengineeringin pages 11-12, fisher2022humanipscand pages 11-12).

Immortalized lines (ARPE‑19) are accessible and suited to screening but exhibit reduced fidelity unless long‑term, optimized transwell culture is used; they typically form only a rudimentary barrier, though ZO‑1+ polarized monolayers with directional secretion can be achieved. Misidentification and loss of macular traits are recognized risks, underscoring cautious interpretation (rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 6-7, lynn2018aconvenientprotocol pages 8-10, lynn2018aconvenientprotocol pages 4-5, lynn2018aconvenientprotocol pages 20-20, wu2022tissueengineeringin pages 6-7).

Pluripotent stem cell–derived RPE (hiPSC/hESC‑RPE) recapitulate key functions (phagocytosis, polarity) and robust barrier metrics (e.g., TER ~280 Ω·cm2), reproduce AMD‑relevant phenotypes (drusen-like deposits, CFH‑linked mitochondrial dysfunction), and show improved stress resistance when polarized. However, iPSC‑RPE often lack aged/diseased signatures; they match treatment responses of non‑AMD primary RPE but diverge from AMD haRPE unless disease‑like matrices or stressors are introduced. Scaffold choice modulates fidelity: degradable PLGA supports basal infoldings, basal lamina deposition and polarized VEGF, while nondegradable PET can favor drusen‑like mound accumulation; nitrite‑modified ECM induces inflammatory/complement programs and exposes AMD‑specific metabolic deficits (rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 5-6, fisher2022humanipscand pages 14-15, fisher2022humanipscand pages 2-4, fisher2022humanipscand pages 12-14, fisher2022humanipscand pages 11-12, gong2020stemcell‐derivedretinal pages 1-2).

Three‑dimensional organoids and engineered retina constructs capture layered retinal architecture and outer segment‑like structures but frequently lack a reconstituted RPE–photoreceptor interface and show variable maturation/integration, limiting full RPE–photoreceptor fidelity (rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 11-12, rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 6-7, wu2022tissueengineeringin pages 4-6).

RPE–endothelium co‑cultures and organ‑on‑chip add choroidal interactions and hemodynamics: endothelial co‑culture tightens RPE barrier via lysyl oxidase/β1‑integrin; microfluidic platforms yield perfusable capillaries and VEGF‑driven endothelial migration across RPE; perfusion/mechanical stress modulate VEGF/PEDF, enhancing pathophysiologic relevance (rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 6-7, wu2022tissueengineeringin pages 7-9, wu2022tissueengineeringin pages 6-7, rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 11-12).

Air–liquid interface/transwell configurations remain foundational for polarity and Bruch’s‑membrane–like exchange, enabling matrix deposition and directional secretion; long‑term culture is required for maturation and, on certain membranes (e.g., PET), can yield drusen‑like deposits (rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 11-12, lynn2018aconvenientprotocol pages 3-4, lynn2018aconvenientprotocol pages 8-10, rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 5-6).

Primary RPE on permeable supports is the reference for in vitro fidelity, including disease “memory.” iPSC‑RPE recapitulate many functions and genetic risk–linked phenotypes but often require disease‑mimicking contexts to reveal AMD traits; immortalized lines are practical but lower‑fidelity. 3D/organoid and vascular co‑culture systems add architectural and choroidal complexity, yet a fully faithful in vitro recapitulation of the RPE–photoreceptor–choroid unit remains unfinished (rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 11-12, fisher2022humanipscand pages 14-15, rizzolo2022retinalcelltransplantation pages 6-7, wu2022tissueengineeringin pages 4-6, wu2022tissueengineeringin pages 7-9).
