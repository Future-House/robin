Query: Conduct a literature search on the role of complement system activation and NLRP3 inflammasome assembly in retinal pigment epithelial cell degeneration during dry age-related macular degeneration, encompassing details of complement factor expression, activation triggers, downstream inflammatory mediators, regulatory feedback mechanisms, and potential assayable protein interactions.

Complement activation is pervasive at the RPE/Bruch’s membrane interface in dry AMD and is supported by genetics, tissue deposition, and systemic biomarkers. Alternative pathway components (C3, CFB, CFD) assemble the C3 convertase C3bBb, stabilized by properdin, driving C3/C5 cleavage to produce C3a/C5a and the terminal C5b‑9/MAC; regulators CFH, CFI, FHL‑1, CD46, CD55, and CD59 normally constrain this activity but risk variants (e.g., CFH Y402H) and age‑related ECM changes impair control, favoring aberrant activation and MAC deposition on RPE/choriocapillaris (armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 1-2, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 10-11, armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 10-11, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 14-15, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 33-34). ARMS2 can bind properdin, and complement gene loci (CFH, C3, CFB, CFI, C2, CD46) contribute to AMD susceptibility, implicating local alternative pathway dysregulation in disease initiation (armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 7-8). Oxidative stressors relevant to RPE (lipofuscin/A2E, HNE, cigarette smoke, oxPOS, H2O2) increase C3/CFB/C5 expression and reduce inhibitors (CFH, CD46/55/59), while TLR2 signaling links redox injury to complement gene upregulation and senescence (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 14-15).

Complement crosstalk primes and activates the NLRP3 inflammasome in RPE. C5a provides priming via NF‑κB; C1q can trigger activation via a phagolysosome/caspase‑1–dependent route; C3a promotes ATP efflux and P2X7R signaling to activate NLRP3; sublytic MAC elevates cytosolic Ca2+, also licensing NLRP3 (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 33-34, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 14-15). Canonical NLRP3 assembly requires priming (NLRP3, pro‑IL‑1β transcription) and a second signal (ROS/TXNIP, lysosomal membrane permeabilization/cathepsins, P2X7R‑dependent K+ efflux/Ca2+ influx), culminating in ASC specking, caspase‑1 activation, and secretion of mature IL‑1β/IL‑18; non‑canonical caspase‑4/5 pathways and nucleic‑acid sensors (cGAS→IFN‑β→caspase‑4, AIM2) can feed into this cascade in AMD RPE (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 12-13, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 86-87, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 76-79, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 79-82, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 82-84). Human AMD tissues and fluids show increased NLRP3, pro‑IL‑1β/IL‑18, caspase‑1 activity, and IL‑1β/IL‑18, supporting inflammasome engagement in RPE pathology (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 12-13, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 76-79, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 168-171). IL‑1β amplifies IL‑6/IL‑8/MCP‑1 and recruits microglia/macrophages; IL‑18’s role is context‑dependent; complement factor H–related 3 can induce endogenous complement and drive IL‑1β/IL‑18 maturation in RPE (korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 79-82, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 76-79, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 87-89). Cell death outcomes include caspase‑1–dependent pyroptosis; GSDMD is often uncleaved in RPE while GSDME cleavage is observed after atRAL, highlighting heterogeneity in pore-forming executioners (korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 82-84, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 87-89).

Assayable nodes and interactions:
- Complement: C3/C3a/C3b/iC3b/C3d, Bb, C5/C5a, sC5b‑9 and membrane MAC; convertase activity (C3bBb stabilization), CFD/properdin levels, and regulators CFH/FHL‑1/CFI/CD46/CD55/CD59 by ELISA, Western, activity assays, and tissue deposition imaging (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 10-11, armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 1-2, armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 10-11, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 33-34).
- Inflammasome: NLRP3–ASC–procaspase‑1 oligomerization (co‑IP, native gels), ASC specks, caspase‑1 cleavage/activity, mature IL‑1β/IL‑18 (ELISA/Western), non‑canonical caspase‑4 activation, GSDMD/GSDME cleavage, P2X7R–NLRP3 co‑localization, ATP release, ROS/TXNIP, LMP/cathepsin release, autophagy flux (LC3, p62) (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 12-13, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 82-84, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 86-87, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 79-82, piippo2019inflammasomesignalingand pages 47-49).
Regulatory feedback includes CFH/CFI/FHL‑1 control of C3b turnover, membrane inhibitors blocking convertases/MAC, autophagy restraining ROS‑driven NLRP3, and Hsp90/NLRP3 quality control (armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 1-2, armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 10-11, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14, piippo2019inflammasomesignalingand pages 91-95). Clinical trials targeting CFD or properdin have been mixed, underscoring incomplete mechanistic resolution; C3 inhibition (APL‑2) reduced GA growth in phase II, motivating integrated complement–inflammasome biomarker strategies (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 12-13, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 11-12).

In dry AMD, oxidative injury and genetic dysregulation drive alternative pathway activation at the RPE interface, producing C3a/C5a and MAC that prime/activate NLRP3 via ATP/P2X7R, lysosomal, ROS, and Ca2+ signals. NLRP3 assembly in RPE yields caspase‑1 activation, IL‑1β/IL‑18 release, and inflammatory cell recruitment, with pyroptotic and noncanonical features detectable by defined molecular assays (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 12-13, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 33-34, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 14-15). Parallel measurement of complement convertases/MAC and inflammasome readouts in RPE, fluids, and tissue will clarify causality and therapeutic windows (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 12-13, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 10-11, piippo2019inflammasomesignalingand pages 47-49).
