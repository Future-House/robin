Assay Overview: Quantify mitochondrial membrane potential (Δψm) in retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) models using JC‑1 (ratiometric plate reader or microscopy) or TMRE/TMRM (quantitative flow cytometry or imaging). Readouts are the JC‑1 red/green ratio or TMRE/TMRM fluorescence intensity, optionally normalized to a potential‑independent mitochondrial mass probe (e.g., MitoTracker Green) to control for mitochondrial content/loading. Assay can be run under basal conditions and after acute mitochondrial stressors (e.g., ETC inhibitors, redox perturbation) to define dynamic range, and then used to test candidate therapeutics for Δψm preservation. Suitable cells include ARPE‑19, AMD RPE cybrids, iPSC‑RPE, and primary human adult RPE (haRPE) from AMD and non‑AMD donors; published protocols include JC‑1 at 2 µM, 10 min, plate‑reader detection, and TMRE at 50 nM, 20 min, flow cytometry (488/575 nm) (yumnamcha2019auranofinmediatesmitochondrial pages 2-3, wang2020d609protectsretinal pages 9-10, yin2022commonmethodsin pages 5-6).

Biomedical Evidence: Mitochondrial dysfunction is a central axis in dry AMD: human donor RPE show decreased mitochondrial mass, disrupted architecture, reduced ETC subunits, and increased mtDNA damage before degeneration, leading to reduced ATP and altered redox signaling. The inner membrane electrochemical gradient drives ATP synthesis; membrane damage lowers Δψm, promotes release of mtDNA and apoptogenic factors, and activates inflammasome‑mediated sterile inflammation—all observed or implicated in AMD RPE biology. Thus, Δψm is a proximate, mechanistically relevant parameter linking bioenergetics failure to inflammatory and cell‑death pathways in the RPE (ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 2-3, fisher2018perspectiveonamd pages 1-2, fisher2018perspectiveonamd pages 2-2, korhonen2021antimycinainducedmitochondrial pages 1-2).

Previous Use: In ARPE‑19, Δψm was measured by JC‑1 to demonstrate that TrxR inhibition (auranofin) induces mitochondrial dysfunction, mitophagy, ATP loss, and pyroptotic death, establishing feasibility and linkage of Δψm to bioenergetics and cell fate. AMD transmitochondrial cybrids treated with a PGC‑1α–upregulating agent used mitochondrial membrane potential as a drug‑responsive endpoint among a panel of mitochondrial readouts. Additional RPE studies cite JC‑1 for Δψm under oxidative stress and fission modulation, and TMRE flow cytometry has been deployed alongside ROS, apoptosis, LDH, ATP, and mitochondrial imaging in ARPE‑19 to profile protective agents, indicating assay portability into multi‑parametric drug testing. Method reviews support TMRM/TMRE for linear, sensitive quantitation and recommend mass normalization controls (yumnamcha2019auranofinmediatesmitochondrial pages 2-3, nashine2019pu91drugrescues pages 1-2, yako2021pharmacologicalinhibitionof pages 10-10, wang2020d609protectsretinal pages 9-10, yin2022commonmethodsin pages 5-6).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths—Directly interrogates a core mitochondrial parameter required for ATP production and linked to inflammasome activation; sensitive, rapid, and scalable; established protocols in RPE lines; integrates well with orthogonal endpoints (ATP, OCR, mitophagy, inflammasome markers) to build translational relevance. Weaknesses—JC‑1 can be artifact‑prone (aggregation, concentration dependence) and Δψm signals are confounded by mitochondrial mass/loading; require normalization and positive/negative controls. Many AMD donor studies emphasize OCR/ATP over Δψm, and donor heterogeneity implies Δψm alone will not predict therapeutic benefit; model choice matters (haRPE may retain disease signatures better than naïve iPSC‑RPE), necessitating confirmation across RPE models and donors (yin2022commonmethodsin pages 5-6, cai2019highthroughputscreeningidentifies pages 1-2, ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 7-7, ebeling2020improvingretinalmitochondrial pages 1-2, fisher2022humanipscand pages 11-12, fisher2022humanipscand pages 1-2).

Δψm assays with JC‑1/TMRE are technically established and biologically aligned with AMD RPE pathogenesis, and they have been used successfully in RPE drug‑response studies. They are useful as part of a multiparametric panel (ATP/OCR, mitophagy, inflammation) and should be validated across RPE models and donors with appropriate normalization controls. Used this way, Δψm provides a sensitive, actionable readout to triage mitochondria‑targeted therapeutics for dry AMD (yumnamcha2019auranofinmediatesmitochondrial pages 2-3, wang2020d609protectsretinal pages 9-10, fisher2018perspectiveonamd pages 1-2).
