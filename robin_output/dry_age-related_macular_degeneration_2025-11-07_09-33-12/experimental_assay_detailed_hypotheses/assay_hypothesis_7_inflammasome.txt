Assay Overview: The assay models RPE-intrinsic inflammasome signaling implicated in geographic atrophy by priming human RPE monolayers with complement cues (sublytic MAC or C5a) and activating with ATP (P2X7R) or lysosomal destabilizers (e.g., Leu-Leu-OMe), then quantifying ASC speck formation by high-content imaging and IL-1β release by ELISA; optional orthogonal readouts include cleaved caspase‑1 (p20) and IL‑18 to strengthen pathway assignment (human primary or iPSC‑RPE preferred; ARPE‑19 only as a secondary model) (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 14-15, gao2019thereductionof pages 1-2, marneros2023roleofinflammasome pages 1-3, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 86-87, hytti2021differentialexpressionof pages 1-2).

Biomedical Evidence: RPE and neural retina release ATP and express P2X7R; ATP→P2X7 engagement drives caspase‑1 activation, NLRP3 signaling, IL‑1β/IL‑18 maturation, and RPE death, while lysosomal destabilization is a canonical NLRP3 trigger; Alu RNA accumulation in RPE (DICER1 deficiency) primes NLRP3 and induces mitochondrial ROS, providing endogenous, AMD-relevant inputs (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 14-15, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14). In vivo, repeated Aβ1–40 exposure in rodent RPE elevates caspase‑1, induces NF‑κB priming, increases IL‑1β/IL‑18, and activates pyroptosis/apoptosis, supporting assay readouts and complement/drusen relevance (gao2018evidenceforthe pages 1-2, gao2018evidenceforthe pages 12-12). Clinically, IL‑1β/IL‑18 elevations have been reported in AMD, though human data are heterogeneous and often measure expression rather than activation (wang2019efficacyofnovel pages 1-2, marneros2023roleofinflammasome pages 1-3, marneros2023roleofinflammasome pages 3-4).

Previous Use: IL‑1α‑primed RPE stimulated with LMP, oxidative stress, or P2X7 agonists shows caspase‑1 cleavage and IL‑1β/IL‑18 secretion; responses are blocked by caspase‑1 inhibitors, validating pathway dependence (gao2019thereductionof pages 1-2, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 87-89). Selective NLRP3 inhibitors suppress IL‑1β and LDH in human/murine RPE and reduce ex vivo eye‑cup activation, demonstrating pharmacological tractability for screening (wang2019efficacyofnovel pages 1-2, korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 87-89). P2X7 antagonism prevents ATP‑induced IL‑1β/RPE apoptosis; NRTIs inhibit Alu RNA–driven caspase‑1 and show efficacy in mouse GA/CNV in a P2X7‑dependent manner (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 14-15).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths—Mechanistically anchored stimuli (C5a/MAC prime; ATP/LMP activate), disease-relevant readouts (ASC specks, IL‑1β), and established pharmacologic responsiveness make this a useful platform for mechanism-based screening and MoA deconvolution in RPE (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 14-15, wang2019efficacyofnovel pages 1-2). Weaknesses—NLRP3 expression in human RPE is disputed with validated reagents, raising the possibility that signals reflect non‑NLRP3 inflammasomes or contaminating immune cells; rigorous reagent validation, genetic controls, and orthogonal readouts (caspase‑1 p20, IL‑18, GSDM cleavage) are required (kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 3-4, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 2-3, marneros2023roleofinflammasome pages 1-3). Monocultures omit microglia/complement dynamics and chronicity; human data linking RPE‑intrinsic inflammasome activation to GA progression remain limited, so translational inference should be cautious (korhonen2024inflammasomeactivationin pages 86-87, marneros2023roleofinflammasome pages 3-4, gao2018evidenceforthe pages 12-12).

This assay is mechanistically justified and pharmacologically tractable for probing RPE inflammasome biology relevant to GA, provided complement-based priming is used and stringent validation is built in (akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 13-14, akhtar‐schafer2018modulationofthree pages 14-15, wang2019efficacyofnovel pages 1-2). However, ambiguity around NLRP3 expression in RPE and the multicellular nature of AMD necessitate orthogonal readouts and confirmatory models to ensure disease relevance (kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 3-4, kosmidou2018issueswiththe pages 2-3, marneros2023roleofinflammasome pages 3-4).
