Overall Evaluation: Strengths and weaknesses of this assay for testing therapeutics for dry age-related macular degeneration.

Assay Overview: This cell-based assay models local complement activation at the RPE–Bruch’s membrane (BrM) interface by exposing differentiated human RPE monolayers to normal human serum (NHS) ± test agent and quantifying deposited complement fragments on the RPE surface. Primary or iPSC-derived RPE cultured to polarity on transwells are preferred; ARPE-19 can be used with caveats. Surface immunostaining (non‑permeabilized) for cleaved C3 fragments (C3b/iC3b/C3(H2O) with cleavage-specific antibodies) and terminal complement complex (C5b‑9/MAC) is quantified by confocal imaging; soluble C3a/C5a/SC5b‑9 in apical/basal media can be measured by ELISA. Functional outputs include transepithelial electrical resistance (TER), VEGF/cytokine secretion, MMP-2 activity, NF‑κB activation, CD59 surface levels, lysosome exocytosis, and mitochondrial stress. Acute complement challenge typically uses 5–10% NHS in veronal-buffered saline with Ca2+/Mg2+ for 30–180 min; controls include heat‑inactivated or complement‑depleted sera, and reconstitution ± inhibitors (e.g., anti‑C5) (cabrera2022increasedcellstiffness pages 4-7, tan2016protectiveresponsesto pages 1-1, fernandezgodino2015alocalcomplement pages 10-11, fernandezgodino2018changesinextracellular pages 5-8, yang2017complementmediatedregulationof pages 1-2, annamalai2021naturalimmunoglobulinmbased pages 3-4).

Biomedical Evidence: Complement dysregulation—particularly alternative pathway (AP) amplification—is strongly implicated in AMD genetics and pathology; complement components (C3, CFH) and MAC accumulate in BrM/drusen and at the RPE–choroid interface (armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 1-2, tan2016protectiveresponsesto pages 1-1). RPE produce complement locally, and abnormal ECM can drive AP activation via C3 tick‑over, depositing cleaved C3 on ECM and promoting sub‑RPE deposits; C3 deletion abolishes deposits in EFEMP1 models (fernandezgodino2015alocalcomplement pages 1-2, fernandezgodino2015alocalcomplement pages 2-4, fernandezgodino2018changesinextracellular pages 5-8). In vivo, AP activation (iC3b, Ba) and factor B dependency correlate with drusen‑like deposits, and FB inhibition reduces deposits, supporting AP-targeted readouts (crowley2023complementfactorb pages 2-4, crowley2023complementfactorb pages 1-2). Complement fragments trigger RPE inflammatory responses (NF‑κB; IL‑1β/IL‑18/GM‑CSF), and sublytic MAC on RPE is documented and modulated by CD59 trafficking and lipid status (cao2016theroleof pages 120-125, cao2016theroleof pages 9-13, tan2016protectiveresponsesto pages 1-1, tan2016protectiveresponsesto pages 1-3). Patient iPSC‑RPE carrying CFH Y402H show elevated C5b‑9 lysosomal deposition and lysosome dysfunction reversible by C3 blockade, linking deposition to dysfunction (cerniauskas2020complementmodulationreverses pages 1-2, cerniauskas2020complementmodulationreverses pages 2-4).

Previous Use: RPE monolayers challenged with NHS or complement‑focusing protocols have been used to detect and inhibit complement activation, quantify C3b/C5b‑9 deposition, and link deposition to functional outputs (TER, VEGF, cytokines) (annamalai2021naturalimmunoglobulinmbased pages 3-4, tan2016protectiveresponsesto pages 1-1). Targeted inhibitors (e.g., anti‑C5) block MAC‑associated ApoE accumulation; AP‑targeted fusion proteins and scFv‑directed inhibitors reduced complement in RPE monolayers and in vivo, demonstrating assay utility for mechanistic screening (yang2017complementmediatedregulationof pages 1-2, annamalai2021naturalimmunoglobulinmbased pages 1-3, annamalai2021naturalimmunoglobulinmbased pages 3-4). EFEMP1 mutant RPE/ECM systems established that RPE‑derived C3 activates via tick‑over on abnormal ECM and that standard C3 binders may be insufficient in this context, informing inhibitor selection and readouts focused on upstream C3 activation (fernandezgodino2018changesinextracellular pages 1-5, fernandezgodino2018changesinextracellular pages 5-8). iPSC‑RPE Y402H models showed that C3 inhibition reduces intracellular C5b‑9 and rescues lysosomal function, providing direct drug‑responsive endpoints (cerniauskas2020complementmodulationreverses pages 1-2). Methodological precedents for serum‑based MAC staining and complement manipulation are also well established in ocular endothelial systems (zeng2016molecularresponseof pages 1-2, cabrera2022increasedcellstiffness pages 4-7).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths: High disease relevance to AP dysregulation at the RPE–BrM interface; clear genetic, tissue, and cell‑based evidence that C3/C3a and MAC deposition drive early pathology and RPE dysfunction; robust, quantitative, drug‑responsive readouts; compatibility with polarized human RPE and ECM contexts; ability to couple deposition with functional outputs (barrier, cytokines, lysosome function) (armento2021thecomplementsystem pages 1-2, fernandezgodino2015alocalcomplement pages 1-2, cerniauskas2020complementmodulationreverses pages 1-2, tan2016protectiveresponsesto pages 1-1). Weaknesses: Acute serum exposure models may not capture chronic/aging context, systemic versus local complement sources, or microglia/choroid contributions; RPE susceptibility varies with polarity, lipids, and genotype; deposition alone may not predict injury, and C3a‑driven pathology may be missed by MAC-only readouts; ECM-bound tick‑over can be insensitive to some C3 inhibitors (mohlin2018amodelto pages 1-2, cabrera2022increasedcellstiffness pages 7-8, fernandezgodino2015alocalcomplement pages 1-2, fernandezgodino2018changesinextracellular pages 1-5, zeng2016molecularresponseof pages 7-8). Best practice is to measure both upstream (C3a/C3b) and downstream (C5b‑9) with functional endpoints, use polarized primary/iPSC‑RPE with relevant ECM, and validate hits in orthogonal models (EFEMP1 mice, BrM explants) (fernandezgodino2018changesinextracellular pages 1-5, crowley2023complementfactorb pages 2-4, yang2017complementmediatedregulationof pages 1-2).

This RPE complement deposition assay is well‑justified and useful for early-stage testing of complement‑modulating therapeutics for dry AMD, provided it includes upstream C3 and functional readouts and appropriate polarity/ECM context. It captures key AP biology and is drug responsive, but results should be validated in models that incorporate chronicity and tissue complexity.
