Assay Overview: This assay utilizes pH‐sensitive fluorescent dyes (LysoSensor or LysoTracker) to quantify lysosomal pH in RPE cells loaded with A2E—a key lipofuscin derivative that disrupts lysosomal acidification. Changes in fluorescence intensity or ratiometric signal are measured via live‐cell imaging or plate readers, allowing assessment of both lysosomal pH and downstream functional endpoints such as autophagic flux. The assay is typically performed in human RPE cell lines such as ARPE-19 or more physiologically relevant iPS-RPE (choi2024protectiveeffectof pages 18-29, choi2024protectiveeffectof pages 12-18).

Biomedical Evidence: Lysosomal acidification is central to RPE degradative function because lysosomal enzymes require an acidic environment for activity. A2E accumulation in RPE lysosomes promotes alkalinization, impairing proteolysis, arresting autophagy, and ultimately contributing to cell death—a process strongly implicated in dry AMD pathogenesis (choi2024protectiveeffectof pages 29-36, choi2024protectiveeffectof pages 18-29, guha2014approachesfordetecting pages 12-14). Given the clinical association between lipofuscin accumulation and AMD, restoration of lysosomal acidity is a promising therapeutic target.

Previous Use: Several studies have employed pH-sensitive dyes in A2E-loaded RPE models to monitor drug-induced reacidification and correlating improvements in autophagic flux and cell viability (choi2024protectiveeffectof pages 12-18, choi2024protectiveeffectof pages 36-40). These applications have provided valuable pharmacodynamic readouts critical for early-stage drug discovery.

Overall Evaluation: The assay’s major strength lies in its disease relevance and ability to provide quantitative, high-throughput data reflective of a core pathogenic mechanism in dry AMD. However, limitations include the need for rigorous calibration, potential dye sensitivity to experimental conditions, and the choice of RPE model, which require complementary functional assays for validation (guha2014approachesfordetecting pages 6-8, guha2014approachesfordetecting pages 8-9).

Restoring lysosomal acidity in A2E-loaded RPE is a valuable pharmacodynamic endpoint for therapeutic screening. Rigorous controls and orthogonal functional readouts will be critical to enhancing the translational potential of this assay.
