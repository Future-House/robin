Assay Overview: This assay models the RPE’s physiologic clearance phagocytosis of shed photoreceptor outer segments (POS) and the early degradative step of phagosome acidification, a locus repeatedly implicated in dry AMD pathogenesis. pHrodo-labeled POS are fed to polarized RPE monolayers (primary human RPE or hiPSC-RPE preferred; ARPE-19 as lower-fidelity alternative), and internalization into acidified phagosomes is quantified by plate reader or high-content imaging as an increase in pHrodo fluorescence per cell; bound versus internalized fractions can be distinguished by differential staining, and orthogonal readouts (rhodopsin decay, Lysotracker colocalization, lysosomal pH/CTSD activity) can be layered to separate uptake from downstream trafficking/maturation. POS can be isolated from human or animal retina and supplemented with physiological bridging ligands (e.g., MFG-E8) to ensure αvβ5/MerTK engagement; dosing at ~5–10 POS/cell and attention to media conditions are recommended to achieve robust, receptor-mediated uptake. (lieffrig2023clearancephagocytosisby pages 6-8, stahl2021evaluatingthephagocytosis pages 5-12, vargas2022probingphotoreceptorouter pages 1-2, egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 10-18)

Biomedical Evidence: POS phagocytosis by RPE is essential for retinal homeostasis; failure to clear cargo causes accumulation of lipofuscin and drusen, oxidative stress, complement susceptibility, and ultimately photoreceptor/RPE loss characteristic of dry AMD. Genetic and preclinical models with impaired recognition/internalization (αvβ5, MFG-E8, MerTK/RCS rat) or defective lysosomal degradation (cathepsin D deficiency, elevated lysosomal pH) develop POS accumulation and retinal degeneration, establishing causality. Critically, human AMD donor RPE exhibit a substantial phagocytic deficit compared to age-matched controls, with age-related decline superimposed, directly linking impaired POS handling to human disease. (egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 26-32, si2023theroleof pages 4-5, lieffrig2023clearancephagocytosisby pages 30-35, inana2018rpephagocyticfunction pages 1-2, egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 21-26)

Previous Use: Human donor RPE and hiPSC-RPE POS assays have quantified disease-relevant uptake and degradation kinetics, revealed AMD-associated defects, and demonstrated rescue by secreted factors and receptor ligands (e.g., MFG-E8, trophic RTK ligands), validating these assays as pharmacology-responsive readouts. FRET and acidification-based live-cell assays discriminate uptake from trafficking/lysosome fusion and respond to autophagy/endo-lysosomal perturbations (Atg5, VPS11/18 knockdown; chloroquine), enabling mechanism-of-action triage. Oxidative stress and lipid droplet models reduce POS uptake and are pharmacologically reversible, further supporting screening utility. New metrics such as “Total Consumptive Capacity” extend these platforms to chronic lipofuscin-like burden. (inana2018rpephagocyticfunction pages 1-2, egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 10-18, voisin2022proteinsassociatedwith pages 1-2, yako2022lipiddropletaccumulation pages 1-2, zhang2023improvedlipofuscinmodels pages 1-3)

Overall Evaluation: Strengths: strong disease-mechanism proximity; human-relevant cell sources; quantitative, scalable signal that integrates internalization and acidification; compatible with mechanistic controls (αvβ5/MerTK engagement, lysosomal pH, CTSD activity) and orthogonal endpoints (rhodopsin decay). Weaknesses: pHrodo signal conflates uptake with acidification and will underreport when therapeutics alkalinize lysosomes without correcting uptake; POS donor variability, circadian PS exposure, and missing in vivo bridging ligands can confound; immortalized RPE differ from native RPE. Mitigations: standardize POS prep and dosing; supplement bridging ligands; time assays to diurnal biology; pair pHrodo with bound/internalized discrimination, rhodopsin Westerns, lysosomal pH/CTSD assays, and long-term UAM/lipofuscin models to cover maturation and chronic endpoints. (stahl2021evaluatingthephagocytosis pages 5-12, lieffrig2023clearancephagocytosisby pages 6-8, egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 10-18, vargas2022probingphotoreceptorouter pages 1-2, egbejimi2022decipheringunderlyingmechanisms pages 26-32, zhang2023improvedlipofuscinmodels pages 1-3)

In sum, a pHrodo-POS RPE assay is well-justified, mechanistically anchored, and pharmacology-responsive for dry AMD therapeutic testing. It should be deployed within an assay suite that separates uptake from trafficking and captures lysosomal competence and chronic lipofuscin burden. This increases translational relevance while controlling for confounds intrinsic to acidification-dependent readouts.
