Assay Overview: Primary human RPE monolayers cultured on Transwells with stable TER are exposed to oxidative stress plus complement (e.g., 0.5 mM H2O2 + 25% normal human serum, NHS) to induce sublytic complement activation, then conditioned media (preferably basal to avoid serum MMP background) are collected ± test agent and analyzed by gelatin zymography to quantify pro- and active MMP‑2/9 by densitometry; parallel readouts include TER, apical/basal VEGF and PEDF by ELISA, and complement-depleted/reconstituted sera for mechanism controls (C7-depletion/re-addition) (bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 2-3, bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 4-5, bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 5-6, bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 6-7).

Biomedical Evidence: Dysregulated gelatinases drive ECM turnover at the RPE/Bruch’s membrane (BrM) interface by degrading type IV collagen and mobilizing matrix‑bound growth factors; aging and AMD associate with altered MMP‑2/9 levels/activity in BrM, drusen, and choroid, with complement components deposited in BrM, supporting complement–MMP cross‑talk in dry AMD pathogenesis (bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 1-2, bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 2-3, caban2022theroleof pages 5-7, caban2022theroleof pages 22-23). Activated MMP‑2/9 shift the VEGF/PEDF balance toward pro‑angiogenic signaling without early transcriptional changes, linking activity to functional microenvironmental changes relevant to early disease (bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 1-2, bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 5-6). Genetic and clinical data implicate MMP‑9 and TIMP/MMP imbalance, albeit with heterogeneity across cohorts and sample types, underscoring the need for tissue‑level activity assays (liutkeviciene2015theroleof pages 5-6, oszajca2018associationanalysisof pages 1-3, liutkeviciene2015theroleof pages 1-2).

Previous Use: In polarized primary RPE, H2O2 + NHS increased MMP‑2/9 mRNA, secretion, and activation basally in a C7‑dependent manner; MMP inhibition reduced VEGF, increased PEDF in supernatants without altering mRNA, and TER loss correlated with apical VEGF rise (R = 0.92), establishing activity‑driven remodeling under sublytic complement (bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 6-7, bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 5-6, bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 4-5). Independent haRPE studies showed Aβ or APMA elevates pro/active MMP‑9 by zymography and disrupts barrier function, which is rescued by MMP inhibition or MMP‑9 siRNA, supporting causal linkage to epithelial dysfunction (cao2013amyloidβinducedmatrixmetalloproteinase9 pages 1-2, cao2013amyloidβinducedmatrixmetalloproteinase9 pages 5-7). Multiple ocular cell models report pharmacologic suppression of MMP‑2/9 at mRNA/protein/activity levels under stress, demonstrating screening utility (caban2022theroleof pages 15-17).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths: measures enzymatic activity directly; mechanistically anchored to BrM ECM turnover, complement signaling, and VEGF/PEDF biology; compatible with causality tests (complement depletion/reconstitution, selective inhibition/siRNA) and functional endpoints (TER, permeability) (bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 2-3, bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 1-2, cao2013amyloidβinducedmatrixmetalloproteinase9 pages 1-2). Weaknesses: conditioned‑media zymography under-reports matrix‑bound/cell‑surface activity; NHS contains MMPs (apical confounding); pro‑ vs active enzyme and TIMP/MT1‑MMP regulation complicate interpretation; the reductionist injury lacks BrM architecture/chronicity; inhibitor off‑targets require controls (bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 4-5, bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 1-2, caban2022theroleof pages 2-4, caban2022theroleof pages 22-23, bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 6-7). Best use is within a panel: zymography + TER + VEGF/PEDF, complemented by ECM-relevant assays (collagen IV degradation, BrM‑mimetic substrates), localization of activity, and complement readouts (C3a/MAC) to link activity changes to structural remodeling pertinent to dry AMD (bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 2-3, caban2022theroleof pages 7-8, caban2022theroleof pages 23-25).

This MMP‑2/9 zymography assay in RPE monolayers models a complement/oxidative trigger of ECM protease activation central to BrM remodeling in dry AMD and yields actionable, activity‑level readouts tied to functional barrier and VEGF/PEDF changes (bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 1-2, bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 5-6). Its translational value increases when combined with compartmental controls, ECM‑specific substrates, and complement markers to mitigate serum confounds and capture matrix‑bound proteolysis (bandyopadhyay2012matrixmetalloproteinaseactivity pages 4-5, caban2022theroleof pages 22-23).
