Assay Overview: This assay involves transducing cultured primary RPE cells with a tandem mCherry‐GFP‐LC3 reporter to directly monitor autophagosome formation, maturation, and lysosomal clearance via live‐cell imaging. The dual‐fluorophore system distinguishes autophagosomes (GFP+mCherry, yellow) from autolysosomes (mCherry only, red), providing a quantitative measure of autophagic flux in a cell type central to dry AMD pathogenesis (cox2025age‐associateddeclinein pages 7-8, mcwilliams2019acomparativemap pages 3-4).

Biomedical Evidence: Defective autophagy and impaired clearance of damaged organelles are well documented features of dry AMD, contributing to RPE degeneration, oxidative stress, and metabolic shifts. Post-mitotic RPE cells depend on efficient autophagic flux to maintain homeostasis, and clinical and experimental studies have linked autophagosomal accumulation with disease progression (golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 10-11, golestaneh2018dysfunctionalautophagyin pages 9-10).

Previous Use: The mCherry‐GFP‐LC3 reporter has been successfully used in RPE and ocular tissues to assess autophagic flux under various pharmacological treatments, where agents such as chloroquine and trehalose have demonstrated altered autophagosome maturation (cox2025age‐associateddeclinein pages 7-8, mcwilliams2019acomparativemap pages 3-4).

Overall Evaluation: This assay is a powerful tool for early-stage drug discovery as it provides a real-time, dynamic readout of autophagy—a process intimately linked to dry AMD pathology. Its strengths lie in direct visualization, quantitative assessment, and relevance to cellular dysfunction observed in AMD. Potential limitations include the complexity of LC3-associated phagocytosis in RPE and the need to control for circadian variations in autophagic activity, necessitating corroboration with complementary biochemical analyses (cox2025age‐associateddeclinein pages 7-8, mcwilliams2019acomparativemap pages 3-4).

The autophagic flux reporter assay is therefore a promising approach for evaluating therapeutics in dry AMD. It delivers critical insights into autophagosome dynamics that can be directly targeted by candidate compounds.
