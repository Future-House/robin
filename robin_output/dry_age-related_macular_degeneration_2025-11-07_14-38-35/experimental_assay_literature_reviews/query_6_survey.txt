1. Hypothesis: Oxidative stress impairs TFEB nuclear translocation in ARPE-19 cells, arresting autophagic flux at the autophagosome–lysosome fusion step.  
   Assay: Treat ARPE-19 with H₂O₂ ± TFEB overexpression or trehalose; monitor TFEB localization by immunofluorescence, LC3-II levels ± bafilomycin A1 by western blot, p62 accumulation, and fusion via mCherry-GFP-LC3 colocalization with LAMP2.

   Assay: Pulse RPE with A2E + blue light; detect LMP by galectin-3 recruitment and LysoTracker loss, quantify LC3-II and p62, assess fusion with tandem-fluorescent LC3; test rescue with cathepsin D stabilizer or TFEB activator.
