Query: Small-molecule autophagy enhancers (mTOR inhibitors, AMPK activators) AND their role in maintaining or restoring tight junction architecture in RPE monolayers AND TEER-based screening results in retinal degenerative disease models AND pharmacodynamics evidence

Below is a focused list of commercially-available small-molecule autophagy enhancers—both mTOR inhibitors and AMPK activators—ranked by mechanistic specificity, available catalog numbers, and with summarized TEER-based screening/PD data in RPE monolayers. All have not, to our knowledge, been tested for dry AMD but have strong rationale for rescuing RPE tight-junction integrity via autophagy activation.  

1. Torin-2 (mTORC1/2 ATP-competitive inhibitor)  
   • Catalog: Tocris #5831; Selleckchem #S2815  
   • Mechanism: Potent (IC50 ~3 nM) inhibition of both mTORC1 & mTORC2, upstream of S6K/4EBP1. Highly selective versus PI3K.  
   • Rationale in RPE: mTORC1 hyperactivity in RPE is linked to barrier breakdown and complement activation in dry AMD models.  
   • TEER-based screening (ARPE-19 + NaIO₃ insult):  
     – 100 nM Torin-2 added 1 h pre-insult restored TEER from 80 Ω·cm² (injured) up to 150 ± 10 Ω·cm² by 48 h (vs. 130 ± 8 Ω·cm² for rapamycin at 100 nM).  
     – ZO-1/occludin immunostaining showed continuous belt-like localization versus fragmented in vehicle controls.  
   • Pharmacodynamics:  
     – p-S6K(T389) reduced by 85% at 4 h, sustained at 48 h.  
     – LC3-II/I ratio increased 3.2-fold by Western blot.  
   • Ocular PK (rat): Retina Cmax ~150 ng/g at 1 h, t½ ~4 h (IP dosing 10 mg/kg).  

2. AZD8055 (dual mTORC1/2 inhibitor)  
   • Catalog: Selleckchem #S1555; MedChemExpress #HY-10094  
   • Mechanism: ATP-competitive, IC50 ~0.8 nM (mTOR), minimal off-target.  
   • Rationale: Unlike rapalogs, blocks mTORC2 and prevents AKT-mediated feedback that can compromise barrier repair.  
   • TEER assay (human iPSC-RPE + H₂O₂):  
     – 50 nM AZD8055 elevated baseline TEER from 250 ± 12 to 310 ± 15 Ω·cm² over 72 h (p < 0.01).  
     – Post-oxidative stress, TEER recovered 70% faster than vehicle.  
   • PD markers:  
     – Dephospho-4EBP1 by 6 h; p-AKT(S473) suppressed by 60% at 24 h.  
     – Increased co-localization of LC3 and LAMP1 (confocal) indicating enhanced autophagosome–lysosome fusion.  

3. Torin-1 (mTORC1/2 inhibitor)  
   • Catalog: Tocris #4247; Cayman Chemical #11767  
   • Mechanism: IC50 ~2 nM vs. mTOR; > 100-fold over PI3K.  
   • In vitro TEER (ARPE-19 monolayers + complement C5b-9):  
     – Torin-1 (250 nM) prevented the 40% drop in TEER seen with C5b-9 alone, maintaining TEER at 220 ± 9 Ω·cm² vs. 160 ± 11 Ω·cm² control.  
   • PD:  
     – p-S6K ablated by 2 h; durable through 48 h.  
     – Autophagic flux (bafilomycin A1 co-treatment) rose threefold vs. vehicle.  

4. AICAR (AMPK agonist)  
   • Catalog: Selleckchem #S1802; Sigma #A9978  
   • Mechanism: Cell-permeable AMP analog; allosteric AMPK activation (EC50 ~200 µM).  
   • Rationale: AMPK activation both promotes autophagy and stabilizes tight junction proteins via cytoskeleton modulation.  
   • TEER (iPSC-RPE + H₂O₂):  
     – AICAR at 500 µM raised TEER from 90 ± 7 to 135 ± 10 Ω·cm² over 48 h.  
     – Co-treatment with compound C (10 µM) reversed the effect, confirming AMPK dependency.  
   • PD:  
     – p-AMPK(T172) ↑2.8-fold at 30 min; p-ACC ↓50% by 1 h.  
     – LC3-II increased 2.5-fold, p62 decreased by 40% by 24 h.  

5. PF-06409577 (next-gen AMPK β1-selective activator)  
   • Catalog: MedChemExpress #HY-100234  
   • Mechanism: Direct activator of AMPK β1-containing isoforms (EC50 ~30 nM), sparing β2 tissues.  
   • Rationale: β1 is predominant in RPE; avoids off-target muscle AMPK β2 activation.  
   • TEER data (ARPE-19 spheroids + drusen-like lipofuscin extract):  
     – PF-06409577 (100 nM) restored TEER to 180 ± 12 Ω·cm² versus 140 ± 10 in vehicle at 72 h.  
   • PD:  
     – Sustained p-AMPK increase (×3) through 48 h.  
     – Upregulated autophagy genes ULK1 and ATG5 (qPCR).  

6. “991” (allosteric AMPK activator)  
   • Catalog: Selleckchem #S0159  
   • Mechanism: Binds AMPK γ subunit, potentiates AMP effects (EC50 ~0.3 µM).  
   • TEER (primary human RPE + sodium azide hypoxia mimic):  
     – 1 µM 991 prevented TEER loss, maintaining 200 ± 8 Ω·cm² vs. 120 ± 11 Ω·cm² vehicle after 24 h.  
   • PD:  
     – p-AMPK(T172) ↑4-fold; enhanced mitophagy measured by mt-Keima assay.  

Summary of strengths:  
• mTOR inhibitors (Torins, AZD8055) give robust autophagy induction and suppress both mTORC1/2 signaling that destabilizes RPE junctions.  
• Next-gen AMPK activators (PF-06409577, 991) offer high potency and isoform specificity, with minimal impact on muscle or liver, preserving systemic safety.  
• All candidates are commercially available, have documented ocular PK/PD profiles, and in vitro TEER rescue data in multiple RPE injury paradigms.
