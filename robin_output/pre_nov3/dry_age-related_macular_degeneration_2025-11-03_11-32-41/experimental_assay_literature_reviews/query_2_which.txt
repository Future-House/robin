Query: Which in vitro cell‐culture assay systems (2D monolayers, 3D organoids, co‐cultures with microglia or choroidal endothelial cells) and readouts (high‐content imaging of mitochondrial membrane potential, ROS production, complement component deposition, autophagic flux reporters, inflammasome activation markers, lipid droplet quantification, phagocytic uptake assays) have been developed or optimized to model dry AMD pathophysiology, and what are the best practices for assay validation, positive/negative controls, dynamic range assessment, and reproducibility in high‐throughput drug screening?

In vitro assay systems documented for dry AMD-relevant mechanisms span: (i) 2D RPE monolayers with polarity/tight-junction readouts and targeted stress paradigms (e.g., ARPE‑19 lysosomal dysfunction with chloroquine), as well as hiPSC‑RPE monolayers used both on coverslips and within microfluidic devices (double-channel formats for apical/basal secretion) (achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 25-26, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 23-25, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 19-21). (ii) 3D retinal organoids derived from hiPSCs, including long‑term cultures and their integration with RPE in a perfused retina‑on‑a‑chip (RoC) that recreates the RPE–photoreceptor niche and supports multi‑layer physiology (achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 11-14, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 14-15). (iii) Co‑cultures: RO–RPE co‑culture in RoC, microfluidic RPE–endothelial co‑cultures modeling choroidal angiogenesis, and eye‑on‑a‑chip RPE on 3D ECM interfaced with perfusable endothelial networks; RPE–microglia/macrophage interactions are represented in complementary paradigms probing complement/inflammatory crosstalk (achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 19-21, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 23-25, pradhan2020biofabricationstrategiesand pages 75-79, martinelli2022brainandretinal pages 16-17).

Validated readouts include phagocytic uptake of photoreceptor outer segments by RPE, lysosomal enlargement/dysfunction (LAMP2) and cell death (PI) after chloroquine, polarized VEGF‑A secretion (ELISA) under perfusion, ultrastructural EM, and calcium imaging; these are executed with dose/time‑responses and compartment‑resolved quantification in the RoC (achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 11-14, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 19-21, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 7-9). Lipid accumulation under chloroquine is reported in ARPE‑19 monolayers, supporting lipidopathy readouts in 2D formats (achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 23-25). Immune/co‑culture paradigms report complement factor B upregulation in RPE driven by macrophage/microglia cytokines and inflammation‑linked gliosis/vascular effects during Aβ‑induced degeneration (martinelli2022brainandretinal pages 16-17). The excerpts do not document high‑content mitochondrial membrane potential, ROS production, complement deposition imaging, canonical autophagic flux reporters, inflammasome markers, or quantitative lipid droplet imaging in the RoC (achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 7-9, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 11-14, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 19-21).

Best practices for validation include explicit negative and mechanistic positive controls (untreated; chloroquine, gentamicin), concentration/time‑series to establish dynamic range, multiplexed orthogonal endpoints (PI, LAMP2, structural markers, EM), polarized/compartment‑resolved analysis, adequate replication with independent experiments, defined statistics and source data, and perfusion‑enabled longitudinal sampling; isogenic hiPSC‑derived components and fully specified reagents/fabrication parameters bolster reproducibility for high‑throughput screening (achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 11-14, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 14-15, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 16-18).

2D RPE monolayers, 3D hiPSC retinal organoids, and microfluidic co‑cultures (RO–RPE; RPE–endothelium) are established for dry AMD‑relevant biology, with immune/RPE crosstalk models supporting complement/inflammation mechanisms (achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 19-21, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 23-25, pradhan2020biofabricationstrategiesand pages 75-79, martinelli2022brainandretinal pages 16-17). Robust readouts include RPE phagocytosis, lysosomal stress, VEGF secretion, EM, and calcium imaging; lipid accumulation is shown in ARPE‑19, while mitochondrial potential, ROS, complement deposition, autophagic flux, and inflammasome assays are not documented here (achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 11-14, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 19-21, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 23-25, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 7-9). Assay validation should use defined controls, dose/time‑responses, orthogonal endpoints, compartment‑resolved analyses, rigorous statistics/replication, and fully traceable methods under perfusion to ensure dynamic range and reproducibility in screening (achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 11-14, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 14-15, achberger2019mergingorganoidand pages 16-18).
