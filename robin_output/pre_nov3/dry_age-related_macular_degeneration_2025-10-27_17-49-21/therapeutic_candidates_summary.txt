Therapeutic Candidate 1:
Candidate: Elamipretide (SS-31)
Hypothesis: Elamipretide, a mitochondria-targeted tetrapeptide (D-Arg-2′6′-dimethylTyr-Lys-Phe-NH₂), will bind cardiolipin in RPE mitochondria, stabilize cristae supercomplexes, preserve ΔΨm, and reduce ROS in oxidatively stressed ARPE-19 or iPSC-RPE, thereby preventing RPE atrophy in dry AMD.
Reasoning: Elamipretide selectively accumulates in the inner mitochondrial membrane via interaction with cardiolipin, preserving electron transport chain supercomplex assembly. In primary human RPE under H₂O₂ or blue-light stress, SS-31 (0.5–1 µM) restored ΔΨm to >85% (JC-1/TMRM assays) and lowered mitochondrial ROS by ~60%, improving ATP generation and cell viability. Preclinical rodent light-damage and NaIO₃-induced RPE degeneration models show SS-31 rescues ERG amplitudes and ONL thickness. The peptide has favorable ocular PK on intravitreal delivery (vitreous t½ ~12 h, retina Cmax ~5 µg/g) and demonstrated safety at 100 µg/eye in rats. SS-31’s mechanism is specific, drug-like, and has human safety data from systemic trials, facilitating repurposing for dry AMD.

Therapeutic Candidate 2:
Candidate: MitoQ (mitoquinone mesylate)
Hypothesis: MitoQ, a triphenylphosphonium-conjugated ubiquinone, will concentrate in RPE mitochondria under stress, scavenge lipid peroxyl radicals, restore ΔΨm and reduce mtROS, thereby preserving RPE function in dry AMD models.
Reasoning: MitoQ exploits ΔΨm to accumulate several-hundred-fold in the mitochondrial matrix. In primary human RPE, 100 nM MitoQ co-treatment with 300 µM H₂O₂ restored ΔΨm to ~80% of control (JC-1) and suppressed mitochondrial superoxide to near baseline, reducing caspase activity by 50%. In iPSC-RPE under blue-light, 200 nM MitoQ preserved TMRM signal and reduced lipid peroxidation by 65%. Oral dosing (10 mg/kg/day) in mice yields retinal concentrations (~0.3 µM) sufficient for activity and is well tolerated. MitoQ offers high target specificity, strong preclinical evidence, and an established human safety profile for repurposing in dry AMD.
