Query: What advanced human cell culture models—including induced pluripotent stem cell-derived RPE, co-cultures with choroidal endothelial cells, or retinal organoids—most accurately recapitulate key pathophysiological features of dry AMD, and what performance metrics should be used to benchmark each model’s suitability for drug evaluation?

Advanced human cell culture models that recapitulate dry AMD include patient‐derived hiPSC‑RPE monocultures, co‑culture systems with choroidal endothelial cells, and 3D retinal organoids. hiPSC‑RPE cultured on aged Bruch’s membrane mimetics or nitrite‑modified ECM reproduce key disease phenotypes—such as reduced phagocytosis, impaired adhesion, altered gene expression, and increased drusen‐related proteins and inflammatory mediators—which can be quantitatively benchmarked by measuring phagocytic activity, cell viability, ECM attachment, and molecular profiles (dujardin2024developmentofa pages 267-268, dujardin2024developmentofa pages 274-276).

Co‑culture platforms that combine hiPSC‑RPE with choroidal endothelial cells (and sometimes pericytes or fibroblasts) offer enhanced structural and functional fidelity by promoting basement membrane remodeling, endothelial fenestration, and improved barrier properties. These systems can be evaluated using performance metrics such as transepithelial electrical resistance (TEER), permeability assays, VEGF/PEDF balance, and endothelial maturation markers (bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 14-16, dujardin2024developmentofa pages 273-274).

Retinal organoids, particularly long‐term hiPSC‑derived RPE organoids, provide a more physiologic 3D environment that replicates retinal stratification, pigmentation, and Bruch’s membrane-like structures. Benchmarking these models involves assessing structural endpoints (ultrastructural integrity, drusen-like deposit formation), functional assays (photoreceptor outer segment phagocytosis, TEER), and expression of RPE maturity markers (mesch2021towardsanin pages 74-77, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 14-21).

Patient‑derived hiPSC‑RPE, co‑culture systems with choroidal endothelial cells, and retinal organoids are complementary in capturing dry AMD pathophysiology. Measurement of functional, molecular, and structural endpoints is essential for robust drug screening.
