Query: What validated biochemical and functional biomarkers best reflect RPE barrier integrity, phagocytic activity of photoreceptor outer segments, and tight junction maintenance in vitro, and how can these multi-parametric readouts be integrated into a screening platform for potential dry AMD therapeutics?

Validated biomarkers

Barrier integrity and tight-junction maintenance
- Functional: transepithelial electrical resistance (TEER/TER) and fluorescein/dye permeability to quantify barrier strength and paracellular leak (viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 119-119, nicholls2024developmentofan pages 229-232).
- Biochemical/structural: expression and junctional localization of claudins (RPE-enriched claudin-19, claudin-3; also occludin, JAMs) and ZO-1/ZO-2 scaffolds; claudin-19 loss lowers TEER, and inflammatory/oxidative stress reduces ZO-1, linking protein changes to barrier failure (du2023ocularimmuneprivilege pages 1-2, wong2022exploringthepathogenesis pages 6-7, naylor2019tightjunctionsof pages 6-8).
- Imaging analytics: high-content ZO-1 morphometrics with machine-learning classification increases sensitivity and spatial resolution relative to bulk TEER (gao2024useofmachine pages 1-5).

Phagocytic activity toward POS
- Functional: quantitative POS phagocytosis assays (fluorescently labeled POS; internalized counts per cell and population homogeneity; assessment of phagosome–lysosome fusion/degradation) (viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 119-119, viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 23-26, wong2022exploringthepathogenesis pages 6-7).
- Mechanistic markers: αvβ5 (binding), MerTK (internalization initiator), CD36 (rate regulator); MERTK protein as a competence marker; accumulation of sequestosome-1/p62 indicates impaired processing (viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 23-26, nicholls2024developmentofan pages 115-118, du2023ocularimmuneprivilege pages 1-2, nicholls2024developmentofan pages 173-178).

Ion transport/purinergic signaling relevant to RPE function
- ATP-evoked Ca2+ responses (P2Y2–IP3–ER): maturation increases amplitudes; slowed rise/decay indicates downstream dysfunction relevant to fluid regulation (viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 119-119, viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 119-120, viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 98-100).

Integration into a dry AMD screening platform

- Culture hPSC/primary RPE on permeable inserts or BRB-on-chip with continuous TEER and endpoint fluorescein permeability; apply oxidative/inflammatory challenge modules (e.g., H2O2, TNFα) to model disease and quantify rescue (nicholls2024developmentofan pages 229-232, nicholls2024developmentofan pages 47-51, du2023ocularimmuneprivilege pages 1-2).
- Run high-content IF for ZO-1, claudin-19/3, occludin with ML-based TJ morphometry; pair with TEER to capture both spatial and bulk barrier effects (gao2024useofmachine pages 1-5, du2023ocularimmuneprivilege pages 1-2).
- Implement standardized POS uptake/degradation assays; quantify internalization, MerTK/CD36 levels, and p62 accumulation to resolve binding vs processing defects (viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 23-26, nicholls2024developmentofan pages 115-118, du2023ocularimmuneprivilege pages 1-2, viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 119-119).
- Add ATP-stimulated Ca2+ imaging to report transport/signaling competence and maturation state (viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 119-119, viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 119-120).
- Include polarity readouts: apical PEDF and basolateral VEGF secretion as pharmacodynamic biomarkers; leverage on-chip readouts for anti-angiogenic response (viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 23-26, nicholls2024developmentofan pages 229-232, nicholls2024developmentofan pages 47-51).
- Optional early-sensitivity layer: basolateral sEV proteomics for desmosome/hemidesmosome cargo shifts as preclinical barrier-dysfunction indicators (hernandez2023polarizeddesmosomeand pages 1-2).

Efficacy criteria: increased TEER/reduced permeability; restored TJ protein localization; normalized ATP–Ca2+ kinetics; improved POS uptake/degradation with reduced p62; and corrected VEGF/PEDF polarity (viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 119-119, du2023ocularimmuneprivilege pages 1-2, viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 23-26, nicholls2024developmentofan pages 229-232).

In vitro, the most robust panel combines TEER/permeability, TJ protein expression/localization (claudin‑19/3, occludin, ZO‑1), quantitative POS phagocytosis (plus MerTK/CD36 and p62), and ATP‑evoked Ca2+ signaling. These readouts map barrier strength, junction maintenance, and phagocytic competence to therapeutic response. Embedding them in a TEER-enabled, high-content imaging platform with polarity biomarkers (VEGF/PEDF) yields a sensitive screen for dry AMD candidates (viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 119-119, du2023ocularimmuneprivilege pages 1-2, viherialaUnknownyearinvitromaturation pages 23-26, nicholls2024developmentofan pages 229-232).
