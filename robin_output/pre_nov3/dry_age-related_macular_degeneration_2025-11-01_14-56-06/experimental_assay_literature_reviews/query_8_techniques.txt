Query: Techniques to quantify oxidative damage biomarkers in RPE cultures, including assays for lipid peroxidation products (MDA, 4-HNE), protein carbonylation, DNA oxidation (8-OHdG), and high-throughput ROS detection using fluorescent and colorimetric platforms in dry AMD models

Lipid peroxidation (MDA, 4‑HNE). For bulk MDA, use TBARS (spectrophotometric/fluorimetric MDA–TBA adduct), but confirm specificity by HPLC of the MDA–TBA adduct or switch to HPLC-based MDA quantification; stable lipid peroxidation indices such as 8‑epi‑PGF2α can be quantified by immunoassay as complementary endpoints, and volatile alkanes (pentane/ethane) can be measured by GC when applicable (US20210041467A1 pages 11-12). Modern high‑resolution lipidomics (HRMS ± ion mobility/MS/MS) directly identifies oxidized phospholipid species in RPE, while BODIPY C11 (581/591) or 665/676 enables live-cell lipid peroxidation imaging/flow cytometry; note TBARS lacks specificity, so orthogonal confirmation is recommended (chen2024internationalconsensusguidelines pages 16-17). 4‑HNE and related aldehydes are best addressed by chromatographic separation (HPLC) with specific detection and by immunodetection of protein adducts (ELISA/RIA; IF/IHC with 4‑HNE adduct antibodies) (US20210041467A1 pages 11-12, chen2024internationalconsensusguidelines pages 2-3).

Protein oxidation. Quantify protein carbonyls with a validated carbonyl assay in cell lysates, and detect lipid-peroxidation–derived protein adducts (MDA-, 4‑HNE–protein) by ELISA/RIA or immunostaining/IF in RPE cultures (gurunathan2021palladiumnanoparticleinducedoxidative pages 2-3, US20210041467A1 pages 11-12, chen2024internationalconsensusguidelines pages 2-3).

DNA oxidation. Measure 8‑OHdG using commercial ELISA kits; this marker is widely deployed in ocular/AMD contexts and can be applied to RPE culture DNA or nucleoside extracts (US20210041467A1 pages 11-12, kushwah2023oxidativestressand pages 7-8, lopezcontreras2020importanceofthe pages 8-11).

High-throughput ROS/lipid ROS detection. Use fluorescent lipid peroxidation probes (BODIPY C11 family) compatible with plate readers, imaging, and flow cytometry for rapid screening in RPE; alkyne-tagged lipid probes (LAA) provide click-chemistry capture for imaging/quantification; EPR provides orthogonal direct radical detection when feasible; fluorescent probe-based platforms are recognized in ferroptosis guidelines (chen2024internationalconsensusguidelines pages 16-17, chen2024internationalconsensusguidelines pages 2-3). As corroborative redox context, measure glutathione (GSH/GSSG) and antioxidant enzyme activities alongside peroxidation readouts (US20210041467A1 pages 11-12, lopezcontreras2020importanceofthe pages 8-11).

In RPE cultures modeling dry AMD, quantify MDA with TBARS but confirm by HPLC; profile 4‑HNE by HPLC and immunodetection of adducts; assess protein carbonyls with a carbonyl assay; and quantify 8‑OHdG by ELISA (US20210041467A1 pages 11-12, chen2024internationalconsensusguidelines pages 2-3, gurunathan2021palladiumnanoparticleinducedoxidative pages 2-3, kushwah2023oxidativestressand pages 7-8). For high‑throughput ROS/lipid ROS, deploy BODIPY C11 probes on plate-reader/flow cytometry platforms and consider HRMS lipidomics or EPR as orthogonal validation (chen2024internationalconsensusguidelines pages 16-17, chen2024internationalconsensusguidelines pages 2-3).
