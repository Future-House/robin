Assay Overview: This assay measures activation of the lysosome–autophagy transcriptional program in RPE by quantifying TFEB nuclear translocation. ARPE-19 cells are transiently transfected with TFEB-GFP (with optional S142A/S211A phospho-mutants for control), treated with candidate compounds (± disease-relevant stressors such as POS pulse or oxidative insults), and imaged by high-content microscopy to compute nuclear/cytoplasmic TFEB-GFP ratios per cell via automated segmentation; hits are expected to increase nuclear TFEB, indicating engagement of mTOR/AKT–TFEB signaling that drives CLEAR gene expression and lysosomal biogenesis (follow-up: qPCR of CLEAR targets, LAMP1, cathepsin activity, autophagy flux) (pan2019nutrientdeprivationand pages 2-5, mesquita2021purificationandcharacterization pages 30-33, pan2019nutrientdeprivationand pages 1-2, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 10-13).

Biomedical Evidence: RPE lysosomal/autophagy failure, lipofuscin accumulation, impaired POS degradation, and oxidative stress are central in dry AMD; human donor RPE shows reduced lysosomal hydrolases and autophagy flux, and TFEB is the master regulator whose phosphorylation/localization controls lysosomal biogenesis (phospho-TFEB elevated and nuclear TFEB reduced in AMD) (abokyi2025agerelateddrivingmechanisms pages 2-3, abokyi2025agerelateddrivingmechanisms pages 8-9, boya2023lysosomesinretinal pages 4-6, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 6-6). TFEB activation in RPE enhances autophagy, POS handling, visual pigment regeneration, and reduces lipofuscin-like material in AMD-relevant models, providing a proximal mechanistic link between TFEB nuclear entry and disease-modifying biology (abokyi2025agerelateddrivingmechanisms pages 2-3, abokyi2025agerelateddrivingmechanisms pages 7-8).

Previous Use: In ARPE-19/hfRPE POS-pulse models, mTORC1 inhibition induces TFEB programs and reduces POS-dependent autofluorescent granules; constitutively active TFEB similarly prevents/resolves AFG load, establishing causality between TFEB activation and improved lysosomal clearance (preclinical RPE systems) (falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 10-13, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 36-39). TFEB nuclear translocation has been measured directly in retinal cells under AKT inhibition or trehalose, linking translocation to increased autophagy; Pan et al. established TFEB-GFP reagents and phospho-site controls in ARPE-19 for localization assays (karim2020investigatingaktactivation pages 10-12, pan2019nutrientdeprivationand pages 2-5). Disease-relevant signaling (AKT2/SIRT5) represses TFEB nuclear entry and CLEAR genes in iPSC-RPE and mouse models, underscoring the translational relevance of a TFEB translocation readout (ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 1-2, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 6-6).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths: mechanistically proximal to a central AMD pathology node; scalable and quantitative; integrates multiple disease-relevant inputs (mTORC1, AKT2, Ca2+/calcineurin) (boya2023lysosomesinretinal pages 4-6, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 1-2). Weaknesses: ARPE-19 limitations; nuclear TFEB can be uncoupled from functional degradation (e.g., lysosomotropic stress, defective TFEB promoter binding in AKT2 contexts); some literature is preprint (pan2019nutrientdeprivationand pages 7-9, ghosh2023theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 8-11, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 10-13). Mitigations: pair screening with orthogonal function—CLEAR target induction, LAMP1 and lysosomal mass, cathepsin D/L activity and lysosomal pH, LC3/p62 flux (tandem LC3), POS-dependent AF/AFG reduction, and secretory autophagy markers (IL-1β, HMGB1); validate in primary/iPSC-RPE (cox2025age‐associateddeclinein pages 7-8, falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 5-7, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 6-6, boya2023lysosomesinretinal pages 4-6, falcao2024enhancinglysosomefunction pages 10-13).

A TFEB-GFP nuclear translocation assay in ARPE-19 is a credible, high-content primary screen for lysosome–autophagy engagement relevant to dry AMD. It should be embedded in a workflow with functional lysosomal and POS-clearance readouts and validated in primary/iPSC-RPE to ensure translational relevance (boya2023lysosomesinretinal pages 4-6, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 6-6).
