Query: Investigation into oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction contributions to RPE phagocytosis impairment in dry AMD, including identification of redox-sensitive signaling nodes and evaluation of pharmacological modulators in disease-relevant cell and animal models

Oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction are central to the impairment of RPE phagocytosis in dry AMD. Elevated ROS production from dysfunctional mitochondria results in decreased ATP levels and accumulation of oxidative damage that compromises lysosomal and autophagic clearance of photoreceptor outer segments (falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 4-5, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 7-8). Redox‐sensitive signaling nodes such as NRF2 and REV-ERBα are critical in this context. NRF2 mediates the expression of a suite of antioxidant enzymes, while REV-ERBα, through its redox-dependent thiol-disulfide mechanism, modulates NRF2 activity and overall antioxidant defenses (chen2023oxidativestressin pages 5-5, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 5-6). Moreover, PGC-1α involvement in mitochondrial biogenesis further underscores the interplay between redox homeostasis and energy maintenance in RPE cells (kushwah2023oxidativestressand pages 8-10, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16).

Pharmacological modulators have been evaluated in disease-relevant cell and animal models, providing proof-of-concept for therapeutic intervention. REV-ERBα agonists have been shown to enhance NRF2 activity and upregulate antioxidant enzymes such as SOD1 and catalase, thereby protecting RPE integrity (chen2023oxidativestressin pages 5-5). In addition, agents that restore mitochondrial function, including NMN and ZLN005, as well as compounds like resveratrol and nanoceria that scavenge ROS, demonstrate the potential to alleviate oxidative stress and improve phagocytosis (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 11-13, rohowetz2018reactiveoxygenspeciesmediated pages 11-13).

Oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction impair RPE phagocytosis in dry AMD through redox-sensitive signaling nodes such as NRF2, REV-ERBα, and PGC-1α. Pharmacological approaches that target these pathways hold promise for restoring RPE function and mitigating AMD pathology.
