Query: Comprehensive review of lipid metabolism dysregulation in dry AMD RPE—focusing on ceramide, cholesterol, phospholipid alterations—and their direct impact on phagocytic uptake and degradation kinetics in RPE cells

Dysregulated lipid metabolism in RPE converges on motor-dependent phagosome/autophagosome trafficking and lysosomal processing, directly slowing uptake and degradation of photoreceptor outer segments (POS). A cholesterol→acid sphingomyelinase (ASMase)→ceramide cascade, initiated when bisretinoids trap cholesterol and BMP in RPE lysosomes, elevates ceramide, increases tubulin acetylation, stabilizes microtubules, and inhibits autophagosome traffic and autophagic flux; because POS phagosomes rely on microtubule motors, this signaling axis slows degradative kinetics, and is reversible by cholesterol removal or ASMase inhibition (tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 27-31, tian2017atorvastatinpromotesphagocytosis pages 10-11). Aging-associated sphingolipid remodeling further biases this axis: UGCG upregulation and GlcCer accumulation with concurrent suppression of de novo ceramide synthases are linked to inflammation, ROS, and lysosomal dysfunction, predicting impaired phagosome maturation and fusion even without direct POS kinetic assays (alvarezbarrios2025dysregulatedlipidmetabolism pages 14-15).

Cholesterol mislocalization impairs organelle transport and phagosome clearance. A2E displaces cholesterol from lysosomal membranes, trapping it luminally; cholesterol-enriched organelle membranes recruit inward-directed motors and cluster perinuclearly, phenocopying kinesin-1 loss that blocks outer segment phagosome degradation (tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 12-16). Oxysterols directly blunt uptake: 7-ketocholesterol reduces RPE POS phagocytosis via ERK and promotes inflammatory signaling that further degrades phagocytic capacity (yang20197ketocholesteroldisturbsrpe pages 9-9, tian2017atorvastatinpromotesphagocytosis pages 10-11). Sterol synthesis defects (SLOS; 7DHC/oxysterols) cause persistent 1D4+ early phagosomes, failed phagosome–lysosome fusion, poor ROS clearance, and increased A2E, demonstrating a sterol composition–dependent kinetic block (rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 15-20).

Phospholipid pathology compounds the defect. Oxidized PUFAs from POS inhibit lysosomal enzymes; their poor degradability drives lipofuscin, impairs autophagy-dependent clearance, and slows POS degradation, while DHA can enhance autophagy but flux remains disturbed in AMD (kaarniranta2019fattyacidsand pages 6-10). Broad declines in PC/PE/PS and inhibition of PC biosynthesis alter membrane curvature/trafficking, undermining uptake and fusion steps (alvarezbarrios2025dysregulatedlipidmetabolism pages 14-15). Lipid-directed interventions can restore flux: hydrophobic statins enhance phagocytosis in RPE, and targeting cholesterol/ASMase rescues autophagosome motility (tian2017atorvastatinpromotesphagocytosis pages 10-11, tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 27-31).

Ceramide generated downstream of cholesterol/BMP-activated ASMase stabilizes microtubules and inhibits autophagosome and phagosome transport, slowing POS degradation; removing cholesterol or inhibiting ASMase reverses this (tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 27-31). Cholesterol/oxysterol and A2E-driven lysosomal cholesterol trapping, sterol synthesis defects, and oxidized phospholipids each impede uptake or phagosome–lysosome fusion, quantitatively reducing clearance and promoting lipofuscin accumulation (tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 12-16, yang20197ketocholesteroldisturbsrpe pages 9-9, rao2018compromisedphagosomematuration pages 15-20, kaarniranta2019fattyacidsand pages 6-10). Pharmacologic modulation of membrane lipids can partially restore uptake and degradative flux (tian2017atorvastatinpromotesphagocytosis pages 10-11, tan2018organelletraffickingin pages 27-31).
