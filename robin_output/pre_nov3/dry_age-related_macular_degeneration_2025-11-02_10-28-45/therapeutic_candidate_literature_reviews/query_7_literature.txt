Query: Literature on nuclear receptor agonists or modulators (PPARα/γ, LXRβ, RAR/RXR) that improve lipid handling and accelerate photoreceptor outer segment phagocytosis kinetics in primary human RPE, including mechanistic pathway confirmation, efficacy in AMD-relevant models, and human safety/pharmacokinetics data

The strongest primary human RPE evidence concerns LXR agonism. In aged-donor hRPE, GW3965 and TO901317 increased LXR transactivation, induced canonical targets (e.g., FASN, SREBF1), and, with siRNA knockdown of NR1H3/NR1H2 and DR-4 reporter assays, established receptor-specific pathway engagement; pharmacologically, GW3965 reduced palmitate/α-linolenic–induced neutral lipid accumulation and, together with TO901317, preserved mitochondrial membrane potential under oxidant stress, demonstrating improved lipid handling and mitochondrial resilience in hRPE (choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 4-5, choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 2-4, choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 13-14). In AMD-relevant systems, LXR expression declines with age in human RPE, isoform-specific knockouts recapitulate lipid-laden, inflammatory retinal phenotypes, and oral GW3965 in apoB100 mice reduces sub-RPE APOE deposition, retinal inflammation, and ultrastructural abnormalities while upregulating Abca1/Srebp1c in RPE-choroid, supporting translational efficacy of LXR pathway activation (choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 1-2, choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 11-12, choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 9-11, choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 14-15, choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 5-7). Pharmacology caveats include ligand off-targets (e.g., T0901317 cross-activating FXR/PXR) and systemic lipid effects such as transient hypertriglyceridemia; human safety/pharmacokinetics are not provided in the supplied literature (choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 17-18, choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 13-14).

For PPARα/γ and RAR/RXR, A2E can act as an endogenous RAR ligand, and several RAR inhibitors show photoprotective/anti-inflammatory actions in A2E-stimulated RPE that appear to stem from nonspecific modulation of PPAR and RXR transactivation; di-apocarotenoid norbixin activates PPARγ and is protective against A2E phototoxicity, reducing lipofuscin/A2E in vivo, but primary human RPE lipid-handling data and receptor-resolved mechanisms are not shown (fontaine2021a2einducedinflammationand pages 16-18, fontaine2024rarinhibitorsdisplay pages 15-16, fontaine2024rarinhibitorsdisplay pages 9-10). Across all sources, no study demonstrates accelerated photoreceptor outer segment phagocytosis kinetics in primary human RPE after PPARα/γ, LXRβ-specific, or RAR/RXR modulation, nor mechanistic confirmation for phagocytosis acceleration; human safety/PK data are absent (fontaine2021a2einducedinflammationand pages 16-18, fontaine2024rarinhibitorsdisplay pages 15-16, choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 4-5, choudhary2020lxrsregulatefeatures pages 17-18).

LXR agonists have mechanistically confirmed activity in primary human RPE that improves lipid handling and show efficacy in AMD-relevant mouse models. Evidence for PPARα/γ and RAR/RXR modulators is preclinical and indirect in RPE, with no primary human RPE phagocytosis-kinetics data. Human safety and pharmacokinetics for these agents in AMD are not reported in the provided literature.
