Query: Investigation of inflammatory cytokine milieu (IL-1β, TNF-α, IL-6) in dry AMD retina that modulates RPE phagocytosis, focusing on signaling mechanisms, functional assays in primary RPE, and in vivo AMD models

Pro‑inflammatory cytokines present in the aged/AMD retina—IL‑1β, TNF‑α, and IL‑6—converge on canonical inflammatory hubs in RPE, notably NF‑κB with intersecting MAPKs, NLRP3 inflammasome, and ROS/β‑catenin signaling; TNF‑α is repeatedly linked to RPE barrier compromise and VEGF upregulation via ROS‑dependent β‑catenin activation (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 30-32). Mechanistically, TNF‑α signals via TNFR1→TRADD/RIP1/TRAF2→IKK→IκB degradation, releasing p65/p50 to induce IL‑1β/IL‑6, chemokines (MCP‑1), adhesion molecules (ICAM‑1), iNOS/COX‑2, and VEGF, changes that drive chronic inflammation, oxidative stress, and RPE dysfunction (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 8-10). IL‑1β binds IL‑1RI/IL‑1RAcP and engages MyD88→IRAK1/4→TRAF6→TAK1→IKK to activate NF‑κB, inducing IL‑6, TNF‑α, MCP‑1/IL‑8, ROS‑generating enzymes, and VEGF, thereby perturbing RPE homeostasis (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 12-14). IL‑6 signals via IL‑6R/gp130→JAK/STAT3 with cross‑talk to IKK/NF‑κB, amplifying inflammatory mediators and VEGF and compromising RPE function under chronic activation (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 10-12, shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 25-27).

Functional assays in RPE: most data derive from ARPE‑19, where IL‑1β drives IL‑6/IL‑8/MCP‑1 and ICAM‑1/iNOS via MAPK and NF‑κB, effects suppressed by small‑molecule inhibitors; however, primary RPE phagocytosis (POS uptake/maturation) assays modulated by IL‑1β/TNF‑α/IL‑6 are not reported in the provided excerpts (koraneeyakijkulchai2023antiinflammatoryactivityand pages 1-2). A microglia–primary RPE co‑culture and subretinal microglia transplantation model show RPE structural/secretory changes and enhanced CNV, but no phagocytosis readouts (ma2009microgliainthe pages 1-2). Multiple excerpts explicitly note the absence of primary RPE phagocytosis assays tied to these cytokines (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 27-30, hernandez2025roleofoxidative pages 8-10).

In vivo models: TNF‑α promotes CNV via RPE ROS‑β‑catenin→VEGF; IL‑1 receptor allosteric modulation mitigates inflammation/photoreceptor toxicity; NF‑κB inhibition dampens RPE inflammasome/cytokine output; yet none of the cited in vivo studies quantify RPE phagocytosis in dry AMD after cytokine manipulation (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 30-32). Reviews and summaries emphasize cytokine presence, barrier permeability changes, and SASP‑rich myeloid infiltration, but again lack in vivo phagocytosis endpoints (cao2016theroleof pages 131-134, kauppinen2016inflammationandits pages 10-11).

Mechanistically, IL‑1β, TNF‑α, and IL‑6 activate NF‑κB/MAPK/NLRP3 and ROS/β‑catenin in RPE, driving inflammatory, oxidative, and angiogenic programs that plausibly impair phagocytosis (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 30-32, shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 12-14). However, within the provided literature, direct primary RPE phagocytosis assays and in vivo dry AMD phagocytosis outcomes after cytokine modulation are not reported (shafi2024cytokinedrivennfîºbactivation pages 27-30, cao2016theroleof pages 131-134). Dedicated studies using primary RPE POS‑uptake assays and in vivo phagocytosis readouts are needed (hernandez2025roleofoxidative pages 8-10).
