Query: Search for evidence of complement factor H variants (e.g., Y402H) and elevated C3a/C5a fragments directly causing RPE phagocytic dysfunction, including genetic, functional, and biochemical studies in patient-derived RPE cells and in vivo dry AMD models

Evidence from multiple studies indicates that CFH Y402H is associated with increased complement activation in RPE cells, with subsequent accumulation of activation fragments such as C3a, C5a, and C3d. Genetic investigations using patient‐derived iPSC–RPE cells demonstrate that the Y402H variant confers an AMD‐like phenotype characterized by impaired phagocytic function and lysosomal dysfunction, suggesting a direct impact on RPE cell clearance mechanisms (ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 10-11, ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 2-4). Biochemical analyses further report that under oxidative stress and lipid accumulation conditions, reduced levels of complement regulatory proteins (e.g., CD46 and CD59) exacerbate complement dysregulation, which may contribute to phagocytic impairment (tang2024aberrantlipidmetabolism pages 8-10, cao2016theroleof pages 120-125). In vivo dry AMD models, including mice exhibiting the CFH-H/H genotype, also show RPE dysfunction coupled with chronic inflammation and increased deposition of complement components, supporting the involvement of CFH genetic variants in disease pathology (ghosh2024theakt2sirt5tfebpathway pages 10-11, keir2017vegfregulateslocal pages 8-10). However, while these genetic, functional, and biochemical studies collectively associate CFH Y402H–driven complement activation with RPE phagocytic defects, direct causality between elevated C3a/C5a fragments and impaired phagocytosis remains to be definitively established (cao2016theroleof pages 27-31, cao2016theroleof pages 46-52).

CFH Y402H is implicated in enhancing complement activation and correlates with RPE phagocytic dysfunction in both patient-derived cell models and in vivo dry AMD systems. Additional mechanistic studies are needed to firmly establish elevated C3a/C5a as direct mediators of this dysfunction.
