Query: Review of neuroprotective growth factors (PEDF, specific VEGF-A isoforms) and their influence on RPE phagocytosis kinetics, as well as small molecule mimetics or pathway modulators tested in AMD-relevant in vitro and in vivo systems

PEDF is a multifunctional neuroprotective factor with a direct mechanistic role in RPE outer segment processing: PEDF-R (PNPLA2/ATGL) is required for degradation of photoreceptor outer segments by RPE, and ablation of PEDF-R causes photoreceptor degeneration (jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 10-11, jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 17-18). Genetic loss of PEDF induces RPE senescence and defects in phagocytosis, and PEDF is apically secreted into the interphotoreceptor matrix where RPE encounters shed outer segments, supporting a role at the phagocytic interface (jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 14-15). Binding of PEDF to PEDF-R activates PLA2, releases lipid mediators (e.g., NPD1), and preserves mitochondrial, cytoskeletal, and barrier functions, all consistent with sustaining phagocytic competence under stress (jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 10-11). However, no quantitative measurements of RPE phagocytosis kinetics in response to PEDF have been provided in the cited excerpts (jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 17-18, jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 16-17).

VEGF-A biology is well defined: VEGF165 is the predominant isoform, signals via VEGFR-1/2, and VEGF disrupts RPE barrier function; RPE secretes VEGF basally and PEDF apically, and NV-AMD features increased VEGF with reduced PEDF (zuloaga2015curcuminaenla pages 10-14, andriessen2020regulationofinflammation pages 26-29). The excerpts do not report isoform-specific effects of VEGF-A on RPE phagocytosis kinetics (jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 16-17).

AMD-relevant modulators include PEDF-derived peptides (e.g., 34-mer, 44-mer) and gene delivery, which reduce CNV, vascular leakage, and protect photoreceptors; collagen enhances 34-mer efficacy, and topical 34-mer reduced inflammation and leakage in diabetic mice (jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 14-15, jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 15-15, jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 6-7, jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 8-10). Engineered PEDF variants and nanoparticle formulations are being developed, but no small-molecule PEDF mimetics are detailed (jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 10-11, jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 17-18). Small molecules that shift the VEGF/PEDF axis include piperine, vildagliptin, and perindopril; curcumin suppresses HIF-1/VEGF and protects AMD-RPE; autophagy modulators (3-MA, chloroquine) regulate VEGF/PEDF expression and impact lysosomal function in ARPE-19 (jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 15-15, zuloaga2015curcuminaenla pages 69-72, li2019autophagyanew pages 5-6).

PEDF/PEDF-R signaling is required for the RPE degradation phase of phagocytosis, but kinetic effects have not been quantified in the provided sources. VEGF-A isoform-specific modulation of RPE phagocytosis is not documented here. AMD-relevant peptide/gene PEDF therapies and pathway-directed small molecules exist, yet direct phagocytosis-rate data remain a key gap (jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 10-11, jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 16-17, jakobsen2024functionalrolesof pages 6-7).
