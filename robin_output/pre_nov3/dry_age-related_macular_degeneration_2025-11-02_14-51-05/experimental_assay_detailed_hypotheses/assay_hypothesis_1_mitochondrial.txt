Assay Overview: This assay models the RPE mitochondrial bioenergetic deficit implicated in dry AMD by measuring real-time oxygen consumption rate (OCR) and extracellular acidification rate (ECAR) in hiPSC-derived or primary human RPE on Seahorse XF plates after a brief oxidative insult (e.g., H2O2 or NaIO3), enabling calculation of basal and ATP-linked respiration, maximal respiration, and spare respiratory capacity within hours and normalization to cell number (CMST workflow) (ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 14-15, ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 7-7, louie2022assessmentofposterior pages 49-56). hiPSC‑RPE and primary haRPE retain cardinal RPE features (pigmentation, tight junctions, polarity, phagocytosis), supporting biological relevance for mitochondrial assays in this cell type (ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 4-6, fisher2022humanipscand pages 2-4). ECAR is collected in parallel to quantify glycolytic shifts; decreased OCR and reduced compensatory ECAR after treatment indicate rescue of mitochondrial function (ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 7-7, bhattacharya2022modelingofmitochondrial pages 15-18).

Biomedical Evidence: Multiple human studies demonstrate that AMD RPE exhibit reduced OxPhos and glycolysis with lower ATP/GSH and altered stress responses, validating mitochondrial respiration as a central, disease-relevant endpoint (ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 7-8, ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 7-7). Patient iPSC‑RPE from AMD donors show reduced basal/maximal OCR and spare capacity, and are more vulnerable on aged/modified ECM, linking bioenergetic insufficiency to functional deficits in an AMD-relevant microenvironment (gong2020stemcell‐derivedretinal pages 10-11). Disease and genetic models that impair mitochondria (MELAS heteroplasmy; CCCP) show OCR deficits, glycolytic reprogramming, and altered AMPK/PGC‑1α/autophagy pathways, reinforcing the mechanistic suitability of OCR/ECAR readouts and AMPK‑mitophagy axes as pharmacodynamic markers (bhattacharya2022modelingofmitochondrial pages 1-2, bhattacharya2022modelingofmitochondrial pages 15-18). Oxidative RPE toxins (e.g., NaIO3) acutely depress OCR and increase glycolytic capacity in eye cup tissue, supporting the insult framework to mimic AMD-relevant mitochondrial injury (louie2022assessmentofposterior pages 49-56).

Previous Use: Seahorse CMST in patient iPSC‑RPE has been used to detect mitochondrial functional changes and to screen mitochondrial/homeostasis-targeting interventions, with donor-specific, time-dependent responses and the ability to resolve modest improvements in maximal respiration and spare capacity (ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 4-6, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 9-11). Primary haRPE from AMD donors display disease-specific baselines and distinct responses to oxidative and AMPK/autophagy perturbations, whereas unconditioned iPSC‑RPE often resemble non-AMD responses, indicating model choice and conditioning are critical for AMD-relevant rescue readouts (fisher2022humanipscand pages 2-4, fisher2022humanipscand pages 1-2, fisher2022humanipscand pages 11-12). iPSC‑RPE studies have also linked reduced OCR to impaired matrix interactions and complement activation under nitrite-modified ECM, broadening translatable phenotypes (gong2020stemcell‐derivedretinal pages 10-11).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths: strong mechanistic alignment with AMD RPE mitochondrial pathology; quantitative, multiplexed OCR/ECAR endpoints; medium-throughput in XF96; compatibility with patient-specific cells and fold-change analyses to control inter-donor variability; and precedent showing sensitivity to both pharmacologic modulators and oxidative insults (ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 7-8, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 14-15, louie2022assessmentofposterior pages 49-56, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 9-11). Weaknesses: Seahorse plates and acute assay format are suboptimal for highly polarized RPE and chronic stress paradigms; AMD haRPE may show paradoxical oxidative resistance, complicating “rescue” interpretation; iPSC‑RPE may lack AMD signatures without maturation/conditioning; and donor-to-donor variability necessitates replication and orthogonal endpoints (ATP/GSH/viability, AMPK/PGC‑1α/autophagy markers) (zhang2024longtermmonitoringof pages 14-15, ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 7-8, fisher2022humanipscand pages 11-12, ferrington2017alteredbioenergeticsand pages 7-7). Long-term OCR platforms (e.g., Resipher) can complement Seahorse for chronic dosing and recovery studies in differentiated RPE (zhang2024longtermmonitoringof pages 1-3).

This Seahorse-based mitochondrial respiration rescue assay is biologically well-justified for dry AMD and suitable for medium-throughput screening of bioenergetic modulators. To maximize translational value, pair it with AMD-relevant conditioning, carefully titrated oxidative insults, orthogonal stress/viability endpoints, and confirmatory studies in primary AMD haRPE or long-term OCR systems.
