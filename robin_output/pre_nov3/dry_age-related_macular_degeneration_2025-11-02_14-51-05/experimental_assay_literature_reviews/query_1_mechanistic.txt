Query: Mechanistic basis of dry AMD pathogenesis including retinal pigment epithelium oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, autophagy impairment, complement cascade dysregulation, inflammasome activation, lipid metabolism disturbances and drusen biogenesis; identification of key molecular targets and biomarkers across RPE, Bruch’s membrane, and choriocapillaris

Dry AMD is a compartmental, multifactorial disorder in which RPE-intrinsic failures (oxidative stress, mitochondrial injury, proteostasis/autophagy deficits, lipid dysregulation) interact with Bruch’s membrane (BrM)/ECM remodeling and choriocapillaris degeneration to yield drusen, chronic subretinal inflammation, and progressive photoreceptor loss (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 1-2). High ROS burden from light, POS phagocytosis, and age-compromised mitochondria promotes lipid peroxidation and lipofuscin/bis‑retinoid accumulation that photosensitize and injure RPE, accelerating DNA damage, senescence, and atrophy (chen2023oxidativestressin pages 1-2, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 6-7). With aging, UPS function declines and flux shifts to defective autophagy; p62/SQSTM1–NRF2 signaling is impaired, NRF2/PGC‑1 programs wane, and damaged respiratory chain components accumulate, driving a mitochondrial vicious cycle and proteotoxic/lipotoxic buildup (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 8-9, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 2-3, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 3-4). Oxidized/lipidated adducts and mitochondrial danger signals activate innate immunity via complement and NLRP3 inflammasome, elevating IL‑6/TNF‑α and sustaining para‑inflammation in subretinal space (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 8-9, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 1-2). Genetic susceptibility concentrates in complement/ECM loci (CFH, ARMS2/HTRA1), with complement activation (C3→MAC) and ECM perturbation (HTRA1 substrates; MMP/TIMP imbalance) fostering drusen growth and neovascular risk (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 1-2, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 7-8, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 6-7). Lipid metabolism variants (APOE, ABCA1, LIPC, CETP) and cholesterol-rich deposits shape drusen biogenesis; SDDs are enriched in unesterified cholesterol vs sub‑RPE drusen that contain both esterified and unesterified cholesterol (basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 4-5, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 5-6, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 6-7). BrM thickening, amyloid in drusen, and choriocapillaris attrition further impair trans‑epithelial exchange and escalate RPE detachment/migration and deposit accrual (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 2-3, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 1-2).

Key targets/biomarkers:
- RPE: oxidative/mitochondrial injury (respiratory chain proteins, ATP synthase subunits), NRF2/PGC‑1 activity, autophagy/UPS markers (LC3, p62/SQSTM1), lipofuscin/bis‑retinoids (quantitative AF), inflammatory readouts (IL‑6, TNF‑α, PTX3), complement regulators (CFH/CD59) (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 3-4, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 2-3, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 8-9, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 6-7, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 5-6). 
- BrM/ECM: BrM thickness, MMP/TIMP activity, HTRA1/EFEMP1 axis, amyloid‑β in drusen, drusen/SDD lipid composition and apolipoproteins (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 2-3, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 7-8, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 6-7, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 5-6). 
- Choriocapillaris: vascular thinning/degeneration metrics and RPE–choriocapillaris trophic signaling disturbances (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 1-2, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 1-2). 
- Genetics/complement: CFH Y402H, ARMS2/HTRA1 risk haplotypes, systemic/ocular complement activation assays (C3/MAC) (basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 4-5, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 1-2, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 6-7).

Dry AMD arises from oxidative/mitochondrial stress and clearance failure in RPE that, together with ECM/BrM remodeling, complement/inflammasome activation, and lipid dysregulation, drive drusen formation and chorioretinal atrophy (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 1-2, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 8-9, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 6-7). Cross-compartment biomarkers span RPE oxidative/mitochondrial and autophagy axes, BrM/ECM remodeling and drusen composition, choriocapillaris integrity, and complement genetic/functional readouts (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 2-3, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 5-6, chen2023oxidativestressin pages 1-2, basyal2024antioxidantsandmechanistic pages 4-5). These nodes nominate NRF2/PGC‑1, p62/LC3, complement (CFH/C3), HTRA1–ECM, and lipid-handling pathways as actionable targets (garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 2-3, garciagarcia2022pathophysiologyofagerelated pages 6-7, kaarniranta2023autophagyinagerelated pages 1-2).
