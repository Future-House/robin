Query: NLRP3 inflammasome activation mechanisms in RPE cells in response to drusen components and oxLDL in dry AMD: signaling pathways, IL-1β release, caspase-1 activation assays, and pharmacological modulators evaluated in cell-based assays for mechanistic drug screening

1. Hypothesis: Drusen-derived amyloid-β primes NLRP3 via TLR4/MyD88–NF-κB signaling  
   • Model: ARPE-19 cells treated with purified amyloid-β fibrils ± OxLDL  
   • Readouts: pro–IL-1β mRNA (qPCR), p65 nuclear translocation (immunofluorescence), IL-1β release (ELISA), caspase-1 activity (FLICA)  
   • Modulator: TLR4 inhibitor (TAK-242) or MyD88 inhibitory peptide
