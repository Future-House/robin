Query: Studies reporting high-throughput RPE barrier integrity screens using transepithelial electrical resistance and paracellular permeability assays to identify small molecules that enhance tight junction function, including mechanistic target validation, efficacy in dry AMD in vitro and in vivo models, and preliminary ADME/safety profiles

1. AMPK Activation to Upregulate Claudin-1/Occludin  
Hypothesis: Small-molecule AMPK activators enhance RPE tight junctions by boosting claudin-1 and occludin expression.  
Cell-culture assays: Treat hTERT-RPE1 monolayers with compound; measure TEER and FITC-dextran flux; confirm AMPK dependence via siRNA and p-ACC Western blots.  
In vitro dry AMD model: Pre-treat cells with A2E plus blue‐light stress and assess barrier rescue.  
In vivo efficacy: Sodium iodate–induced RPE damage in mice, fundus imaging and barrier tracer leakage.  
ADME/safety: Solubility, human/mouse microsomal stability, hERG panel, RPE cytotoxicity (LDH release).

2. ROCK Inhibition to Reduce Actomyosin Contractility  
Hypothesis: Inhibiting ROCK prevents MLC phosphorylation, relaxes cortical actin, and strengthens junctions.  
Assays: Measure TEER and paracellular tracer; validate via p-MLC Western and ROCK1/2 knockdown.  
Dry AMD model: H₂O₂-challenged RPE barrier dysfunction.  
In vivo: Aged Ccl2–/–/Cx3cr1–/– mice, barrier leakage and OCT.  
ADME/safety: Plasma protein binding, platelet aggregation, off-target kinase panel.

3. Wnt/β-Catenin Modulation to Elevate ZO-1  
Hypothesis: Wnt pathway agonists stabilize β-catenin, upregulate ZO-1 transcription, and reinforce junctions.  
Assays: TEER, permeability, β-catenin nuclear translocation by immunofluorescence; confirm with β-catenin siRNA.  
Dry AMD model: Lipofuscin/A2E treatment.  
In vivo: Cfh–/– mice, assess RPE integrity by fluorescein angiography.  
ADME/safety: Caco-2 permeability, CYP450 inhibition, mitochondrial toxicity.

4. S1P Receptor-2 Agonists for Junction Assembly  
Hypothesis: S1P2 activation promotes Rac1-mediated tight junction formation in RPE.  
Assays: TEER, paracellular dye flux; Rac1 pull-down assay; S1P2 antagonist co-treatment.  
Dry AMD model: Complement C5a-induced barrier disruption.  
In vivo: Laser-induced RPE injury, tracer leakage.  
ADME/safety: Receptor selectivity, cardiovascular telemetry studies.

5. Nrf2 Pathway Activation to Combat Oxidative Stress  
Hypothesis: Nrf2 activators upregulate antioxidant defenses and preserve tight junction proteins.  
Assays: TEER, paracellular flux; confirm Nrf2 dependence by Nrf2–/– RPE cells and HO-1 induction.  
Dry AMD model: Chronic H₂O₂ plus low-dose sodium iodate.  
In vivo: Aged ApoE–/– mice fed high-fat diet, evaluate barrier by Evans Blue.  
ADME/safety: Glutathione adduct profiling, genotoxicity (Ames test).

6. PPARδ Agonism to Modulate Lipid Metabolism and Barrier Function  
Hypothesis: PPARδ activation reduces lipid accumulation, enhances ZO-1 and occludin expression.  
Assays: TEER, Oil Red O staining, ZO-1 Western blot; PPARδ knockdown.  
Dry AMD model: Photooxidative stress with A2E.  
In vivo: Ccl2–/–/Cx3cr1–/– AMD model, fundus autofluorescence and leakage.  
ADME/safety: Lipophilicity (LogD), P450 induction panel, liver microsome stability.

7. TLR4 Antagonists to Reduce Inflammation-Mediated Leakiness  
Hypothesis: Blocking TLR4 signaling prevents cytokine-induced tight junction breakdown.  
Assays: Treat RPE with LPS ± antagonist; TEER, IL-6/IL-1β ELISA; confirm TLR4 knockdown.  
Dry AMD model: Low-grade LPS and oxidative stress.  
In vivo: IL-1β transgenic mice, assess RPE barrier with fluorescein.  
ADME/safety: Immune cell profiling, cytokine panel, hERG liability.

8. Gas6/MerTK Pathway Agonists to Enhance Phagocytosis and Barrier Stability  
Hypothesis: Activating MerTK improves daily POS clearance and upregulates junction proteins.  
Assays: POS phagocytosis assay, TEER, MerTK phosphorylation, MerTK siRNA.  
Dry AMD model: A2E accumulation with defective phagocytosis.  
In vivo: MerTK–deficient RCS rat rescue study, tracer leakage.  
ADME/safety: Retinal distribution, anti-drug antibody risk.

Dry AMD model: Chronic oxidative stress.
