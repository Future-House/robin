Query: impact of lipid peroxidation products, like 4-hydroxynonenal, malondialdehyde-adducts, and oxidized phospholipids, on mitochondrial respiration, electron transport chain integrity, and RPE cell viability in dry AMD models

Lipid peroxidation in the macula generates electrophiles (4‑HNE, MDA, HOHA/HOOA lactones, oxidized phospholipids) that covalently adduct proteins and aminophospholipids. In RPE, these reactions target mitochondrial respiratory chain proteins and ethanolamine phospholipids (PE), a lipid class enriched in the inner mitochondrial membrane and required for ETC organization and function, providing a direct route to damage ETC integrity via protein crosslinking/degradation and membrane lipid remodeling (schutt2003proteinsmodifiedby pages 2-2, guo2016…5hydroxy8oxo6octenoatelactonespart pages 45-49, guo2016…5hydroxy8oxo6octenoatelactonespart pages 361-365). Proteomic analyses of human RPE lipofuscin identify MDA- and HNE-modified mitochondrial respiratory proteins with evidence of cross-linking or partial proteolysis, strongly implying compromised complex assembly/turnover within the ETC (schutt2003proteinsmodifiedby pages 2-2). Mechanistically, aldehyde adduction of PE and other mitochondrial lipids is predicted to alter inner-membrane biophysics and protein–lipid interactions essential for oxidative phosphorylation, a vulnerability amplified in AMD where lipid peroxidation products accumulate and mitochondrial damage is prominent (guo2016…5hydroxy8oxo6octenoatelactonespart pages 45-49, chiras2015oxidativestressin pages 5-6, guo2016…5hydroxy8oxo6octenoatelactonespart pages 361-365).

Direct respiration or complex activity measurements are not reported in the cited excerpts; however, mitochondrial-linked metabolic readouts decline with oxidized lipids. Partly oxidized DHA‑PC produces steep, light‑exacerbated losses in MTT signal and delayed apoptosis in ARPE‑19, consistent with mitochondrial dysfunction; HOHA‑lactone similarly drives dose‑dependent ROS, CEP adduct formation, reduced MTT (LC50 ≈42 μM in ARPE‑19), and apoptosis, with primary hRPE being more resistant (bakker2024(photo)toxicityofpartially pages 9-12, bakker2024(photo)toxicityofpartially pages 18-19, guo2016…5hydroxy8oxo6octenoatelactonespart pages 79-88). HOHA/HOOA lactones and 4‑HNE trigger caspase‑3 activation and Annexin V–positive death in RPE, aligning with mitochondrial membrane perturbation pathways; nevertheless, complex‑specific inhibition and oxygen consumption data are not provided here (guo2016…5hydroxy8oxo6octenoatelactonespart pages 108-113, guo2016…5hydroxy8oxo6octenoatelactonespart pages 214-218, guo2016…5hydroxy8oxo6octenoatelactonespart pages 358-361).

Lipid peroxidation products accumulate in AMD and covalently modify mitochondrial respiratory proteins and mitochondrial lipids, plausibly disrupting ETC integrity. Oxidized phospholipids and aldehydic fragments depress mitochondrial-dependent metabolic activity and induce ROS and apoptosis in RPE, reducing viability. Quantitative effects on respiration rates and complex-specific activities are not directly measured in these excerpts and require targeted studies (schutt2003proteinsmodifiedby pages 2-2, guo2016…5hydroxy8oxo6octenoatelactonespart pages 45-49, bakker2024(photo)toxicityofpartially pages 9-12, bakker2024(photo)toxicityofpartially pages 18-19, guo2016…5hydroxy8oxo6octenoatelactonespart pages 79-88, guo2016…5hydroxy8oxo6octenoatelactonespart pages 358-361, chiras2015oxidativestressin pages 5-6).
