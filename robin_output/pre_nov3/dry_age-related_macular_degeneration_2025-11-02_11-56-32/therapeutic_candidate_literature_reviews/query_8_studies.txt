Query: studies on specific PGC-1α and SIRT1 activators or modulators in RPE models of dry AMD, demonstrating direct target modulation, mitochondrial biogenesis, restoration of oxidative phosphorylation, and functional rescue in vitro and in vivo

Multiple studies in RPE oxidative‐stress models implicate pharmacologic activation of the SIRT1–PGC‑1 axis, but no single study in the context provided demonstrates the entire chain (direct target modulation + quantified mitochondrial biogenesis + restored oxidative phosphorylation + in vivo functional rescue) in one package. Arbutin directly modulates the SIRT1/FOXO3a/PGC‑1α/β pathway in ARPE‑19 and primary RPE (RT‑qPCR/western), with SIRT1 dependence shown by sirtinol; it restores ΔΨm (JC‑1), lowers senescence and lipid peroxidation, improves migration, and yields in vivo structural rescue in NaIO3‑injured mouse retina, although OxPhos flux and canonical biogenesis metrics were not measured (supportive but indirect mitochondrial readouts) (tang2022arbutinprotectsretinal pages 9-11, tang2022arbutinprotectsretinal pages 1-2). A selective PGC‑1α transcriptional regulator, ZLN005, increases PGC‑1α and mitochondrial gene expression and restores respiration (basal/maximal OCR, spare capacity) in ARPE‑19, with protection lost upon PGC‑1α silencing, establishing direct PGC‑1α dependence and OxPhos restoration in vitro (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16). NAD+ boosting with NMN improves basal/maximal respiration, spare capacity, and ATP production in RPE cells from AMD donors, consistent with OxPhos restoration, though direct SIRT1 activation was not shown (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16). Compounds linked to PGC‑1α–driven mitochondrial biogenesis include hydroxytyrosol in RPE in vitro and an FDA‑approved mitochondrion‑stabilizing drug in AMD cybrids that increased mtDNA copy number and PGC‑1α/NRF‑1/2 program, with improved ΔΨm and reduced ROS (kaarniranta2018pgc1αprotectsrpe pages 7-10, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-14). Beyond arbutin’s NaIO3 rescue, in vivo functional rescue for direct PGC‑1α/SIRT1 activators in dry‑AMD–relevant models is limited in the excerpts (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16).

In sum, arbutin shows SIRT1/PGC‑1 pathway engagement with in vitro and in vivo RPE protection but lacks direct OxPhos/biogenesis quantification in the cited sections (tang2022arbutinprotectsretinal pages 9-11, tang2022arbutinprotectsretinal pages 1-2). ZLN005 and NMN provide in vitro evidence for PGC‑1α‑dependent or NAD+‑linked restoration of respiration; biogenesis readouts are documented in cybrid models, but comprehensive in vivo rescue for these agents is not shown here (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-14).
