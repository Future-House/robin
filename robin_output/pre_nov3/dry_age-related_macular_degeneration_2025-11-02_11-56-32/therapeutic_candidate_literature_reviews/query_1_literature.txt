Query: literature on small molecule modulators of mitochondrial respiration in RPE cells under induced dry AMD stress, with evidence of target engagement, rescue of bioenergetics, and efficacy in disease-relevant models, including mitochondrial oxygen consumption rate (OCR), ATP production assays, and RPE functional endpoints such as phagocytosis and barrier integrity

Several small molecules demonstrate target engagement and bioenergetic rescue in RPE under dry-AMD–relevant stress. Nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN) restores the NAD+ axis and improves basal/maximal respiration, spare respiratory capacity, and ATP production in RPE from AMD donors, while preventing H2O2-induced death—direct OCR/ATP evidence for bioenergetic rescue and pathway engagement (NAD+ metabolism) (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21). ZLN005 engages PGC‑1α (upregulates PGC‑1α and mitochondrial genes) and increases basal/maximal respiration and spare capacity in ARPE‑19, with protection lost upon PGC‑1α knockdown—clear target engagement plus OCR rescue (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16). Mitochondria-targeted niacin (TPP‑Niacin) reduces ROS and ameliorates H2O2‑induced mitochondrial dysfunction and Δψm loss in RPE (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16). Elamipretide, a cardiolipin-stabilizing tetrapeptide, increases cellular ATP, lowers mitochondrial ROS, and shows in vivo efficacy in an RPE‑specific SOD2 knockout model (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16). α‑Lipoic acid preserves Δψm and complex I–III activity in oxidant-stressed RPE; metformin/AMPK activators increase mitochondrial energy production and autophagic flux, consistent with pathway engagement (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16). Resveratrol improves mitochondrial bioenergetics, reduces ER stress, and modulates autophagy in RPE cells (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 18-20, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 11-13).

RPE functional endpoints are less commonly reported. RS9 (synthetic Nrf2 activator) promotes POS phagocytosis in RPE in a p62‑independent manner and engages Nrf2/AMPK, with efficacy signals in a primate CNV model (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 18-20). Pharmacologic suppression of mitochondrial fission improves POS phagocytosis and mitigates oxidative damage (Mdivi‑1 in ARPE‑19), supporting mitochondrial‑dynamics targeting for RPE function (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 7-8, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 10-12). Carnosine preserves ZO‑1 tight junctions, indicating barrier integrity rescue (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 18-20). Additional mitochondria-targeted agents (humanin, PU‑91, SkQ1) show mitochondrial engagement and disease-relevant protection, though explicit OCR or RPE functional endpoints are often not reported (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21).

Taken together, NMN and ZLN005 provide the strongest OCR/ATP rescue with target engagement in RPE, while RS9 and carnosine supply RPE functional endpoint evidence (phagocytosis, barrier integrity). Many other agents show mitochondrial engagement and in vivo protection but lack combined OCR/ATP plus RPE functional readouts in the provided literature (tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 18-20, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21).
