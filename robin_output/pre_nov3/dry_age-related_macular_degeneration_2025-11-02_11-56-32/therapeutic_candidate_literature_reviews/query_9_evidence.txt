Query: evidence for selective electron transport chain modulators (complex I, complex III, complex IV) that rescue mitochondrial respiration in RPE cells under dry AMD mimetic stress, with mechanism validation through biochemical assays, enzyme kinetics, and genetic perturbation

The provided context does not offer evidence for selective small‐molecule modulators that target individual ETC complexes (I, III, or IV) in RPE cells under dry AMD mimetic stress with mechanistic validation by biochemical assays, enzyme kinetics, or genetic perturbation. Instead, one study uses expression of an alternative oxidase (AOX) from Ciona intestinalis to bypass ETC deficiency, which broadly stimulates mitochondrial respiration without directly modulating a specific complex (chen2024alternativeoxidaseblunts pages 1-2). Other excerpts describe interventions such as α‑lipoic acid, nicotinamide mononucleotide, mitochondria‐targeted peptides (e.g., SS‑31/elamipretide), and photobiomodulation aimed at modulating overall mitochondrial function or disinhibiting complex IV via nitric oxide displacement; however, these studies do not provide evidence of selective modulation verified through isolated enzyme kinetics or genetic knockdowns/overexpression (nashine2021potentialtherapeuticcandidates pages 16-16, tong2022roleofmitochondria pages 14-16, han2018changesinretinal pages 32-36). None of the documented approaches employ targeted biochemical assays to validate direct complex‐selective effects on ETC activity in a dry AMD model. 

There is no direct evidence from the provided context for selective ETC complex modulators with detailed mechanistic validation in RPE cells under dry AMD mimetic stress. Further studies are required to identify and rigorously validate such targeted interventions.
