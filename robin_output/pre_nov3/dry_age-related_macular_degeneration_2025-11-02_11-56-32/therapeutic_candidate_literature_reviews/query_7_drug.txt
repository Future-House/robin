Query: drug repurposing screens identifying FDA-approved or clinically evaluated compounds that engage mitochondrial-specific targets, improve bioenergetics in RPE cells, and protect against dry AMD-related dysfunction, with evidence of target engagement and mechanism-specific biomarkers

Multiple repurposing-style evaluations and patient-specific iPSC‑RPE screens have identified clinically used compounds with mitochondrial target engagement, bioenergetic rescue, and RPE protection relevant to dry AMD. AMPK‑axis agents stand out: metformin (complex I modulation → AMP/ATP shift) activates AMPK/PGC‑1α, augments autophagy/mitophagy, and in donor/iPSC‑RPE raises maximal respiration and spare respiratory capacity with biochemical p‑AMPK induction; epidemiology links use to lower AMD odds, though a GA trial showed no effect on atrophy progression (biomarkers: p‑AMPK, OCR BR/MR/SRC/ATP, PGC‑1α) (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 4-6, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 9-11). AICAR (direct AMPK activator) increases p‑AMPK and improves mt function in subsets of patient iPSC‑RPE, supporting AMPK engagement and inter‑patient heterogeneity (biomarkers: p‑AMPK, OCR metrics) (ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 11-12, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 4-6). Rapamycin improves mt function in primary AMD RPE via mTOR inhibition and autophagy, whereas trehalose (mTOR‑independent autophagy) elevates LC3‑II/lysosomal markers yet provides limited mt benefit and can be detrimental with prolonged dosing (biomarkers: LC3‑II, lysosomal markers, OCR) (ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 11-12, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 9-11).

Mitochondria‑directed antioxidants/biogenesis modulators include PU‑91 (PGC‑1α activation, biogenesis, reduced ROS; mtDNA haplogroup‑dependent responses; SOD2/IL‑6/BAX modulation with quercetin) and the mitochondrial peptide humanin/HNG (↑ mtDNA copy number, STAT3 phosphorylation; ↓ caspase‑3/ROS; protection in ARPE‑19 and AMD cybrids) (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 39-40). SkQ1/visomitin suppresses RPE atrophy and Aβ burden while down‑modulating mTOR and αB‑crystallin in rodent AMD models (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21). NAD+ augmentation with NMN increases ATP‑linked mitochondrial function and mitigates senescence/DNA damage in RPE (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 39-40). Additional clinically evaluated redox modulators include dimethyl fumarate (Keap1/Nrf2 activation; Nrf2‑dependent prevention of TNFα‑induced mitochondrial dysfunction; HO‑1/NQO1 induction) and resveratrol (SIRT1/PGC‑1α biogenesis; improved bioenergetics; LC3II↑/p62↓; HO‑1/SOD↑; pro‑inflammatory cytokines↓) (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 17-18, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 18-20). Precision use is supported by patient‑specific variability and CFH‑risk genotype associations with impaired mt function in iPSC‑RPE (ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 11-12).

Clinically used agents that engage mitochondrial targets in RPE/AMD models include AMPK activators (metformin, AICAR), autophagy/mTOR modulators (rapamycin, trehalose), biogenesis/antioxidant agents (PU‑91, humanin, SkQ1/visomitin, NMN), and redox pathway activators (DMF, resveratrol). Mechanism‑specific biomarkers reported span OCR‑derived respiration/ATP metrics, p‑AMPK/PGC‑1α, LC3‑II/lysosomal markers, mtDNA copy number, STAT3/caspase‑3, ROS/SOD2, Nrf2 targets, mTOR activity, αB‑crystallin, and Aβ deposition (buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 20-21, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 9-11, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 4-6, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 39-40, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 17-18, buonfiglio2024recentadvancesin pages 18-20, ebeling2022testingmitochondrialtargeteddrugs pages 11-12).
