Query: Which high-content imaging modalities and live-cell fluorescent reporters can be employed to monitor RPE cell morphology, mitochondrial membrane potential, lysosomal pH, autophagic flux, lipid droplet accumulation, and barrier integrity in dry AMD culture assays to evaluate candidate therapeutics?

High-content modalities
- Longitudinal, plate-based live imaging: IncuCyte (GFP/RFP/phase) in 384-well format for time-resolved confluence and fluorescence metrics in RPE1, directly adaptable to primary/iPSC-RPE assays (yoon2023anmtorindependentmacroautophagy pages 35-38). 
- Widefield HCS for fixed or live plates: CellInsight CX5 (10×) for whole‑well lipid droplet quantification and morphology screening at scale (yoon2023anmtorindependentmacroautophagy pages 32-35). 
- Higher resolution: laser scanning confocal for LC3/LAMP2 colocalization and organelle mapping; SIM and Airyscan FAST for sub-diffraction mitochondria–lysosome dynamics with reduced phototoxicity; CLEM/TEM for ultrastructural validation (yoon2023anmtorindependentmacroautophagy pages 35-38, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 90-91, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 49-52, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 100-102, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 84-88, shang2021anovelmethod pages 4-7).

Live-cell fluorescent reporters and dyes
- RPE morphology/barrier: LifeAct‑GFP via adenoviral transduction for F‑actin morphology on flatmounts; live fluorescent tight‑junction markers delivered by adenovirus for barrier readouts; ZO‑1 immunostaining for fixed barrier integrity confirmation (shang2021anovelmethod pages 4-7). 
- Mitochondrial membrane potential: MitoTracker Deep Red for Δψm, integrated with confocal/HCS or flow cytometry (godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 90-91, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 100-102). 
- Lysosomal pH/acidic compartments: LysoTracker Deep Red and LysoView 650; mitochondrial acidification reporters (TOMM20‑mCherry‑EGFP or mCherry‑EGFP‑SYNJ2BP‑TM) and mt‑Keima for mitophagy/acidic delivery in live cells (godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 90-91, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 98-100, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 100-102, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 105-106, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 88-90). 
- Autophagic flux: tandem GFP‑LC3‑RFP‑LC3ΔG quantified on IncuCyte; photoconvertible LC3‑Dendra2 turnover; LC3B/LAMP2 confocal colocalization; flux controls with BafA1 or PepA/E64d (yoon2023anmtorindependentmacroautophagy pages 35-38, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 90-91, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 100-102, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 92-97). 
- Lipid droplets: BODIPY 493/503 HCS quantification (spot area per cell) and neutral‑lipid dyes for live LD–mitochondria imaging (yoon2023anmtorindependentmacroautophagy pages 32-35, godtliebsen2024exploringmitochondrialquality pages 49-52).

These modalities and reporters enable multiplexed, high-content evaluation of morphology, Δψm, lysosomal acidification, autophagic flux, lipid storage, and barrier features in dry AMD RPE assays.
