Assay Overview: Quantify cell-surface opsonins (C3b/iC3b/C3dg) on CFH-genotyped hiPSC-derived RPE after exposure to complement-competent human serum by flow cytometry using conformation-specific antibodies. The assay models alternative pathway control at the RPE/BM interface, where impaired FH/FHL-1 anchoring (CFH Y402H) and local complement secretion by RPE drive opsonization and downstream injury. Primary measurements are surface-associated C3 fragments (MFI/percent-positive), with pathway controls (heat-inactivated serum, C5- or C1q-depleted serum) and orthogonal readouts (C3a/C5a ELISA, MAC IF on monolayers). hiPSC-RPE from low- and high-risk CFH donors are cultured on transwells to preserve polarity and permit apical versus basal serum exposure if desired (hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 7-8, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 16-17, ong2024thecomplementsystem pages 7-9, mulfaul2022localfactorh pages 6-8, cox2023functionalassessmentof pages 56-60).

Biomedical Evidence: Complement activation and drusen-associated complement proteins/MAC are robust in AMD eyes, and CFH Y402H causally links to dysregulated opsonization via impaired FH/FHL-1 binding to heparan sulfate and increased C3b deposition at RPE/BM. RPE produce complement and regulators (FH, FHL-1, FI, C3) and activate complement under serum or hypoxic stress, leading to C3 fragments and MAC deposition; CFH(Y402H) cells show higher C3d deposition. FHL-1 is the dominant regulator in BM, and complement modulation (for example, FDA-approved C3 inhibition) underscores translational relevance of C3-derived readouts (hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 2-3, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 16-17, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 17-19, wilke2024complementregulationin pages 5-6, merle2023thenoncanonicalimpact pages 21-26).

Previous Use: Patient-specific hiPSC-RPE secrete functional FH/FHL-1/FI that cleave C3b to iC3b; increased complement deposition (C5b-9) and APOE/lipid “drusen-like” deposits have been induced by human serum and stress, and genotype-dependent differences have been demonstrated. RPE cell assays have been used to benchmark complement inhibitors by confocal MAC imaging and ELISA; compstatin blocked C3 activity in human RPE assays, with species specificity guiding serum choice. FH variant studies combine binding/decay/cofactor and surface-based MAC suppression assays, supporting the logic of a surface C3b readout as a drug-screen endpoint (hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 7-8, hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 14-15, forest2015developmentofcell pages 89-93, ong2024thecomplementsystem pages 7-9, biggs2022anevaluationof pages 1-2).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths—Mechanistically proximal, quantitative, and genotype-informative readout of alternative pathway regulation on a disease-relevant cell type; can rank complement-regulator mimetics and distinguish regulators that shift C3b to iC3b/C3dg. The assay aligns with known CFH/FHL-1 biology and complements established RPE MAC/APOE deposition assays (cox2023functionalassessmentof pages 46-50, biggs2022anevaluationof pages 1-2, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 16-17). Weaknesses—Flow-cytometry requires detachment that may redistribute/loss of opsonins; serum introduces exogenous regulators confounding cell-intrinsic effects; lack of BM/GAG context where FHL-1 predominates; C3b deposition is upstream and will not capture anaphylatoxin signaling, MAC internalization, autophagy/mitochondrial dysfunction. Mitigations: pooled, genotype-matched sera with heat-inactivated and depleted controls; perform parallel MAC IF and C3a/C5a ELISAs; consider BM-like substrates or apical/basal challenges; add mitochondrial/autophagy endpoints in CFH(Y402H) lines (hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 7-8, wilke2024complementregulationin pages 5-6, behnke2022ifnβimmunomodulationand pages 31-33, ebeling2021impairedmitochondrialfunction pages 16-18, cox2023functionalassessmentof pages 56-60).

This C3b deposition assay is biologically and clinically relevant for dry AMD and suitable for screening complement regulator mimetics in CFH-genotyped hiPSC-RPE. It should be embedded in a panel with MAC, anaphylatoxins, and functional RPE health metrics and, where possible, include BM-like context to reflect FHL-1–mediated regulation (hallam2017aninducedpluripotent pages 2-3, bharti2022cellculturemodels pages 16-17, wilke2024complementregulationin pages 5-6).
