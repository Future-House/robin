Biomedical Evidence: Complement dysregulation—particularly alternative pathway amplification—is a central feature of AMD; macular tissues show increased C3/C3b/iC3b and TCC/MAC, and RPE/choroid express a near-complete complement transcriptome, supporting local activation and measurement of anaphylatoxins in RPE cultures (cox2023functionalassessmentof pages 56-60). C3a/C3aR signaling in RPE is sufficient to trigger basal deposit formation via proteasome inhibition and altered ECM turnover; C5a drives inflammatory/angiogenic programs, linking anaphylatoxin generation to disease-relevant biology (fernandezgodino2018c3atriggersformation pages 1-2, fernandezgodino2018c3atriggersformation pages 12-12). Clinically, elevated circulating TCC in AMD patients produces functional responses in RPE, underscoring translational relevance of complement-active biofluids and RPE readouts (busch2023increasedplasmalevel pages 1-2).

Previous Use: Oxidative stress plus normal human serum on ARPE-19 yields sublytic complement activation with increased VEGF and reduced barrier function; AP-targeted inhibitors (e.g., CR2-fH or targeting Annexin IV neoepitopes) reduce complement activation and VEGF and show in vivo efficacy, providing a discovery-to-validation path (annamalai2021naturalimmunoglobulinmbased pages 3-4, annamalai2021naturalimmunoglobulinmbased pages 1-3). Primary RPE secretes C3a measurable by ELISA, and patient plasma–RPE co-culture links TCC levels to RPE Ca2+ signaling and cytokine induction (ebeling2022inflammasomeactivationin pages 7-9, busch2023increasedplasmalevel pages 1-2). Independent paradigms demonstrate that exogenous C3a drives basal deposits and proteasome dysfunction that are prevented by C3aR antagonism, offering orthogonal pharmacology to validate assay specificity (fernandezgodino2018c3atriggersformation pages 1-2, fernandezgodino2018c3atriggersformation pages 2-4).

Overall Evaluation: Strengths—Biologic centrality (AP, C3a/C5a, MAC) and RPE-local activation make C3a/C5a ELISAs mechanistically proximal; the assay is pharmacologically responsive and can be linked to TER, cytokines, proteasome activity, MMP-2/TIMP-3, TCC/MAC, and structural deposit readouts for construct validity (cox2023functionalassessmentof pages 56-60, annamalai2021naturalimmunoglobulinmbased pages 3-4, fernandezgodino2018c3atriggersformation pages 5-7). Weaknesses—Endogenous/intracellular complement and rapid C5a decay confound fluid-phase measurements; surface-restricted opsonization may not parallel anaphylatoxin release; ARPE-19 differs from primary RPE; Bruch’s membrane diffusion constraints complicate interpretation (trakkides2019oxidativestressincreases pages 9-11, trakkides2019oxidativestressincreases pages 11-13, lin2018theroleof pages 67-70, cox2023functionalassessmentof pages 56-60). Mitigations include serum controls, AP-specific inhibitors, surface C3b/MAC imaging, and parallel primary RPE validation with time- and dose-responses; given mixed clinical outcomes for distal inhibition, prioritize C3/C3aR-focused pharmacology and link ELISA readouts to downstream functional phenotypes (jiao2018theroleof pages 35-40, fernandezgodino2018c3atriggersformation pages 1-2).

Measuring C3a/C5a release from stressed RPE exposed to complement-competent serum is biologically grounded and technically tractable for screening complement-modulating therapeutics. It gains decision value when paired with orthogonal functional and structural endpoints and rigorous controls for AP engagement and endogenous complement. Use both ARPE-19 and primary RPE, and favor C3/C3aR-centric validation given disease-stage biology in dry AMD (cox2023functionalassessmentof pages 56-60, fernandezgodino2018c3atriggersformation pages 1-2, annamalai2021naturalimmunoglobulinmbased pages 3-4).
