Candidate lysosomal acidifiers are evaluated by measuring changes in lysosomal acidity using LysoTracker or ratiometric pH-sensitive reporters. Complementary readouts include assessments of protease activity, autophagic flux, and autofluorescence accumulation to capture downstream effects on cellular proteostasis (cardoso2018stemcellderivedretinal pages 58-61, cardoso2018stemcellderivedretinala pages 58-61).

Biomedical Evidence: Dry AMD is characterized by the accumulation of lipofuscin and impaired degradation of photoreceptor outer segments, processes that are driven by lysosomal alkalinization, A2E-induced inhibition of the V-ATPase, and consequent protease deficits. These events contribute to drusen formation and progressive RPE dysfunction, making lysosomal acidification a central target for intervention as supported by both clinical observations and mechanistic studies (miller2023impairedlysosomesin pages 2-2, falcao2025targetinglysosomaldysfunction pages 2-4).

Previous Use: Similar assays have previously been applied in hESC-RPE and ARPE-19 models, where pharmacological perturbations with chloroquine or bafilomycin A1 induced measurable changes in lysosomal pH and cargo processing. Such studies have linked lysosomal dysfunction to increased autofluorescence and impaired proteolysis, validating the use of pH-sensitive reporters for drug discovery (boya2023lysosomesinretinal pages 3-4, mesquita2021purificationandcharacterization pages 50-53).

Overall Evaluation: The assay presents a mechanistically relevant and scalable platform for early-stage screening of therapeutics targeting lysosomal acidification in dry AMD. Strengths include its direct measurement of a critical cellular dysfunction and its compatibility with high-throughput imaging. Weaknesses involve limitations intrinsic to dye-based pH measurements—such as potential confounds from RPE pigmentation and the indirect nature of the readout—which necessitate complementary evaluations of proteolytic activity and autophagic flux (cardoso2018stemcellderivedretinal pages 164-167, cardoso2018stemcellderivedretinala pages 164-167).

This lysosomal pH assay is a promising tool to assess drug candidates aimed at restoring lysosomal function in dry AMD. However, integrating orthogonal readouts will be essential to fully capture drug efficacy in reversing the complex proteostasis defects of the disease.
