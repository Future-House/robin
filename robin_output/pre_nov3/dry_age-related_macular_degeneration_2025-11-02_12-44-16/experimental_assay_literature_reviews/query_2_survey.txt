Query: Survey and evaluate existing and emerging cell culture assay frameworks for dry AMD drug screening, including 2D and 3D human RPE models, iPSC-derived co-culture systems with choroidal endothelial cells, retinal organoids, oxidative stress and complement activation assays, high-content imaging and multiplexed endpoint analyses quantifying RPE viability, drusen-like deposit formation, mitochondrial function, lipid peroxidation, autophagic flux, and inflammasome activation.

Human cell–based retinal models are prioritized over rodents for dry AMD because human retinal structure and physiology are better recapitulated, yet iPSC-derived systems often remain immature and benefit from layered co-culture to restore critical RPE–vascular interactions (gensheimer2025retinaonchipengineeringfunctional pages 6-7). Foundational 2D RPE monolayers (primary, iPSC-RPE, optimized ARPE-19) enable polarized assays of barrier function, but require rigorous provenance, authentication, and media standardization given ARPE-19 heterogeneity and medium-dependent phenotype (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 309-311, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 302-304).

Outer BRB microphysiological systems integrating RPE with endothelial cells provide quantitative, multiplexable readouts: TEER, 70 kDa dextran permeability, live/dead viability, and confocal high-content imaging of junctions and morphology; oxidative/inflammatory inducers (NaIO3, ML228, TNF-α/TGF-β) disrupt hexagonal ZO-1 patterning, increase permeability, reduce TEER, and drive EC sprouting quantified by CD31, with anti-VEGF blunting neovascularization (김지호2025developmentofa pages 81-88, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 60-66). These platforms support polarized secretome sampling (VEGF/PEDF), imaging of complement/drusen surrogates (APOE, C5b-9), and pharmacology, though use of ARPE-19/HUVEC and absent mitochondrial, lipid peroxidation, autophagy, and inflammasome modules are noted limitations to address (김지호2025developmentofa pages 81-88, gensheimer2025retinaonchipengineeringfunctional pages 16-18).

Oxidative stress is coupled to endogenous complement activation in RPE; combining NaIO3 with complement-competent human serum drives APOE and C5b-9 accumulation in co-culture with duration dependence, alongside cytokine kinetics (IL-1β/6/8) by qPCR, establishing a tractable complement–drusen assay suite (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 309-311, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 74-81). Patient iPSC-RPE with CFH Y402H exhibit lysosomal overload and autophagy dysfunction due to C3 turnover, rescued by C3 inhibition (Cp40), motivating inclusion of LC3/p62 flux and lysosome endpoints within complement paradigms (lin2021humanpluripotentstemcellderived pages 9-10).

Mitochondrial endpoints are well supported: nicotinamide enhances RPE mitochondrial metabolism/differentiation; iPSC-RPE enable testing of mitochondrial-targeted drugs; assays include membrane potential, OCR, ROS, and lipid peroxidation (MDA/4-HNE), with organoid-compatible depolarization probes validated for longitudinal use (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 309-311, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 302-304, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 103-108, aasen2020newdrugdiscovery pages 2-3, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 14-17). Lipid-focused analytics benefit from MALDI-MS imaging of deposits and careful selection of clearing protocols that preserve lipids for 3D imaging workflows (boutin20193dengineeringof pages 17-19, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 12-14). Inflammasome activation can be layered via NLRP3/IL-1β measurements and multiplexed cytokines, extending beyond the current IL-1β transcriptional readouts in oBRB assays (김지호2025developmentofa pages 103-108, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 74-81).

Retinal organoids and retina-on-chip models add translational context for secondary screens and validation: they support phagocytosis, TEM, qPCR/ELISA/Western, TEER/dextran assays, but must contend with PDMS absorption, flow requirements, and scalability limits that currently favor a tiered strategy—high-throughput 2D/co-culture primaries followed by organoid/on-chip secondary assays analyzed by high-content imaging and machine learning (gensheimer2025retinaonchipengineeringfunctional pages 10-12, gensheimer2025retinaonchipengineeringfunctional pages 16-18, aasen2020newdrugdiscovery pages 2-3, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 17-19, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 12-14, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 103-108).

A rigorous screening framework should deploy standardized 2D RPE and oBRB co-cultures for primary triage (TEER/permeability, viability, junctional integrity, complement/drusen imaging, cytokines), then escalate to iPSC-derived co-cultures and organoids for mitochondrial, lipid peroxidation, autophagy/lysosome, and inflammasome endpoints analyzed by high-content imaging with ML and multiplexed biochemical assays (김지호2025developmentofa pages 81-88, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 60-66, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 74-81, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 103-108, aasen2020newdrugdiscovery pages 2-3, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 14-17). Material and perfusion choices (COP/PS over PDMS; controlled flow) and secretome analytics further enhance assay fidelity and translational value (gensheimer2025retinaonchipengineeringfunctional pages 10-12, boutin20193dengineeringof pages 17-19).

Dry AMD screening can leverage validated 2D RPE and oBRB co-cultures for scalable barrier, viability, complement/drusen, and cytokine endpoints, then advance promising compounds into iPSC-derived co-cultures and organoids to quantify mitochondrial, lipid, autophagy/lysosome, and inflammasome biology with high-content, ML-enabled analysis (김지호2025developmentofa pages 81-88, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 74-81, 김지호2025developmentofa pages 103-108, aasen2020newdrugdiscovery pages 2-3). Addressing model caveats (cell provenance, ARPE-19/HUVEC substitution, PDMS absorption) and adopting standardized media and multiplexed assays will improve reproducibility and predictive power (lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 309-311, gensheimer2025retinaonchipengineeringfunctional pages 10-12, lugano2023bioengineeringanoptimised pages 302-304).
